## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **Four revenue lines, one cigarette.** GPI Q1 FY27 [V company presentation, 27-Jul-2026]:
   - **Gross Sales Value (GSV) ₹5,676 Cr** = everything the trade paid, incl. GST (40%), excise and NCCD.
   - **Gross revenue ₹3,820 Cr** = ex-GST, incl. excise (₹2,614 Cr). This is Ind AS "revenue from operations". GPI's "net sales" in its slides (₹3,806 Cr, ₹9,082 Cr in FY26) is on this ex-GST basis.
   - **Net revenue ₹1,206 Cr** = ex-GST, ex-excise. This is what screener shows as "Sales" and what this file uses for margins.
   - Headlines that said "GPI revenue +111% in Q1" (univest, outlookbusiness) used gross revenue [K]. Economically, revenue **fell 18.8%**.
   - The same trap applies to ITC (consolidated "revenue from operations" +27.6% in Q1 FY27 while standalone net revenue fell 14.4%) [V-sib] and VST (gross ₹862 Cr +108% vs net ₹256 Cr −14%) [K univest; V screener].
   - ITC's note 6 to its FY26 results states the accounting rule: **GST and compensation cess are excluded from gross revenue; excise is not** [V ITC Q4 FY26 results]. Before Feb-2026 most tax was GST + cess (excluded), so "gross revenue" was close to net. From Feb-2026 most tax is excise (included), so gross revenue ballooned. **Never compare gross revenue across the 1-Feb-2026 line.**
2. **"21% of net sales" for exports is on the ex-GST/incl.-excise basis.** GPI says FY26 unmanufactured-tobacco exports of ₹1,945 Cr were "21% of net sales revenue" [V Q4 FY26 presentation]. The denominator is ₹9,082 Cr. On screener's **net revenue of ₹6,391 Cr, exports are 30.4%**, and the whole international business (₹2,014 Cr) is **31.5%** [E]. Our primer applies 21% to ₹6,391 Cr (see §3.4).
3. **Market share by volume ≠ by value.** Our primer and screener say GPI has "~13–14%" of the legal market; diversified-fmcg.html uses ITC ~78 / GPI ~12 / VST ~8 (value, indicative) [V-sib]. But GPI sold **24.0 bn sticks in FY26** (2,001 mn/month) [V], VST **8.36 bn** [V VST AR], and ITC an estimated ~70 bn [V-sib, INDICATIVE]. That makes GPI **~22–24% of legal volume** [E], while its **domestic net cigarette revenue (~₹4,270 Cr) is ~13% of the three-company domestic pool** [E] (§3.2). GPI sells cheaper, shorter sticks per unit than ITC (net ~₹1.78/stick vs ITC ~₹3.9) and sells Marlboro to an associate distributor at a transfer price (Trap 6). Always say which basis.
4. **The excise notification has two number sets.** The **Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025** (notified ~11–12-Dec-2025) put **statutory tariff rates of ~₹2,700–11,000 per 1,000 sticks** in the schedule (earlier Act ~₹200–735) [V-snip PRS/taxguru/Tribune]. **Notification 03/2025-Central Excise of 31-Dec-2025** (F.No. CBIC-190349/72/2025-TRU, under s.5A; supersedes Notification 3/2019-CE, G.S.R. 486(E)) **exempts the excess**, so the **effective rates are ₹2,050–8,500** from 1-Feb-2026 [K: notification text reproduced on worldtradescanner.com, fetched]. Our brief cites "G.S.R. 955(E)"; diversified-fmcg.html also cites G.S.R. 955(E) and a 4-Feb-2026 corrigendum [V-sib]. I could not see the G.S.R. number on the fetched copy (it reads "G.S.R. (E)") — keep 955(E) as [V-sib].
5. **"Excise" now means three things for a cigarette.** (a) The new **basic excise duty** (the ₹2,050–8,500/1,000 above); (b) **NCCD** (National Calamity Contingent Duty, Finance Act 2001; unchanged since the Feb-2023 ~16% hike [V-sib]); (c) small legacy items. All three sit in the company's "excise duty" line. GST (40%) sits outside revenue. The **compensation cess is gone** (ended 31-Jan-2026). The **"Health Security se National Security Cess"** (Act of 2025) is a **pan masala** cess, **not a cigarette tax** (Receipt Budget 2026-27: RE FY26 ₹2,330 Cr, BE FY27 ₹14,000 Cr) [V]. Our GPI primer conflates them (§3.4).
6. **Marlboro is not sold by GPI to the trade.** GPI manufactures Marlboro under "exclusive procurement and supply agreements with Philip Morris International" [V Q1 FY27 presentation] and sells it to **Philip Morris India Trading Pvt Ltd** (24.80% owned by GPI), which distributes it. FY26 sales to this associate were **₹2,747 Cr** (FY25 ₹1,421 Cr) [V AR FY26 related-party note], and its **dividend to GPI was ₹258 Cr** (FY25 ₹193 Cr) — about **53% of screener's FY26 "other income" of ₹489 Cr** [E]. Part of Marlboro's economics therefore shows up in GPI's other income, not its operating margin. The related-party footnote also says GPI's purchases from PMITPL "have been reduced by ₹4,859 Cr (FY25 ₹3,322 Cr) owing to sale of Marlboro cigarettes manufactured by the Company" [V] — a netting arrangement whose mechanics the AR does not explain (§H).
7. **GPI's leaf exports are mostly sales to Philip Morris affiliates.** FY26 sales to "member entities of the group to which Philip Morris Global Brands Inc. belongs" were **₹1,362 Cr** (FY25 ₹1,447 Cr): Philip Morris Products S.A. ₹882 Cr, AO Philip Morris Izhora (Russia) ₹345 Cr, PT HM Sampoerna (Indonesia) ₹305 Cr, others small [V AR]. Against FY26 unmanufactured exports of ₹1,945 Cr and international GSV of ₹2,014 Cr, **PMI entities bought roughly 65–70% of GPI's export business** [E]. "Diversified dollar earner" really means "a second, export-side dependency on PMI".
8. **GPI's EBITDA has three bases.** Company "EBITDA (operating)" FY26 ₹1,584 Cr; screener OP ₹1,585 Cr; margins: **24.8% on net revenue** (screener), but **8.6% on GSV** (company slides compute margins on GSV) [V]. Q1 FY27: 15% on net vs 3.2% on GSV. Use net revenue for peer comparisons.
9. **ITC cigarette numbers: standalone vs consolidated.** FY26 cigarette segment revenue **₹37,100 Cr standalone** (used in diversified-fmcg.html) vs **₹40,601 Cr consolidated**; PBIT ₹21,051 Cr vs ₹22,246 Cr; excise ₹9,656 Cr vs ₹11,045 Cr [V ITC Q4 FY26 results]. Our ITC primer's "~₹40,600 Cr" is the consolidated figure. A secondary source gives ITC's Q1 FY27 excise as ₹10,408.93 Cr [K univest] vs ₹10,036 Cr standalone in diversified-fmcg.html — almost certainly the consolidated figure. This file uses **standalone** for ITC cigarettes throughout, as diversified-fmcg.html does.
10. **No official cigarette-volume series exists.** Every "industry volume" (90–110 bn sticks) is an industry/broker estimate [V-sib]. ITC discloses no volumes; GPI discloses monthly-run-rate volumes; VST discloses annual volumes. Illicit share (26.1%) and the revenue loss (~₹23,000 Cr) come from the industry's own "Tobacco Fact Sheet India 2026", quoted in GPI's AR [V AR, but the underlying source is industry-funded]. Label all of them INDICATIVE.

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **A tollgate business whose toll just doubled.**
  - On **1-Feb-2026** cigarettes moved from 28% GST + compensation cess to **40% GST + a new specific excise of ₹2,050–8,500 per 1,000 sticks by length** [K notification text; V-sib].
  - Industry/World Bank put the pre-reset tax incidence at **~50–53% of retail price** (WHO benchmark 75%) [K Business Today 1-Jan-2026]; brokers and press put the post-reset incidence at **~60–70%** [V-snip]; diversified-fmcg.html's composite puts the state's share of a ₹100 pack at **~69% vs ~50% before** [V-sib].
  - The Union **Receipt Budget 2026-27** shows **Basic Excise Duties jumping from ₹45,780 Cr (RE FY26) to ₹90,810 Cr (BE FY27)** — a ~₹45,000 Cr step-up that is overwhelmingly the tobacco reset — while the **GST compensation cess (₹1,50,570 Cr in FY25) goes to nil** [V Receipt Budget; attribution E].
  - Per stick, **GPI's excise went from ₹0.57 to ₹4.67** (Q1 FY26 → Q1 FY27: ₹327 Cr → ₹2,614 Cr on 5.71 → 5.60 bn sticks) [E from V]. ITC's went to roughly **₹6.0 per stick** (₹10,036 Cr on ~16.6 bn sticks a quarter) [E from V-sib].
- **The profit pool shrank by a third in one quarter, and the listed trio reacted differently.**
  - ITC cigarette PBIT **−35.1%** (₹5,145 → ₹3,341 Cr) [V-sib]; GPI EBITDA **−46%** (₹338 → ₹182 Cr) and PAT −44% [V]; VST OP **−35%** (₹77 → ₹50 Cr) and PAT −25% [V screener].
  - Volumes held up far better than profit: **GPI −2%** (company) [V]; **ITC ~−4–5%** (Nomura/brokers) [V-sib; K univest]; VST not disclosed.
  - Because the excise is a **fixed rupee sum per stick**, every rupee of price increase that survives GST and trade margin drops to the maker's net revenue. The recovery path is **price, not volume** [V-sib]. ITC took ~22–23% cumulative by Q1 and more in September (e.g., Gold Flake Premium ₹135, Classic Connect ₹428 per pack, pack sizes unstated) [V-sib; K univest]; GPI raised Marlboro Pocket **₹70 → ₹85 (+21.4%)** around May-2026 [K sahi.com] and calls its approach "a balanced pricing strategy… phased and not in one go" [V].
- **Godfrey Phillips was the fastest-growing cigarette company in India until the reset.**
  - Domestic volume **doubled in four years**: 11.2 bn sticks (FY22) → 13.96 → 15.54 → 20.05 → **24.0 bn (FY26, +20%)** [V company volume chart, × 12].
  - Net revenue FY22 ₹2,688 Cr → **FY26 ₹6,391 Cr** (2.4×); PAT ₹438 Cr → **₹1,526 Cr** (3.5×); OPM 24% → 25%; ROCE 17% → **30%** [V screener].
  - The industry grew ~7% in FY26 (VST AR) [V], so GPI took share; VST grew 8.6% [V]; ITC is implied at ~4–6% [V-sib].
  - The stock is the mirror image: bonus-adjusted **₹703 (Dec-2023) → ₹3,691 all-time high (5-Sep-2025) → ₹1,669 (23-Oct-2026, at its 52-week low)**, −55% from the peak and −45% over one year [V screener chart]. M-cap ₹26,049 Cr, P/E 19.0×, dividend yield 2.99% [V screener 23-Oct-26].
- **Godfrey Phillips is two-thirds a Philip Morris story.**
  - PMI (Philip Morris Global Brands Inc.) owns **25.10%** and sits in the promoter group [V shareholding via AR/SHP].
  - GPI makes Marlboro under licence and sells it through **PMI's own Indian distribution company, in which GPI holds 24.8%** [V].
  - PMI affiliates bought **₹1,362 Cr** of GPI's leaf/cigarette exports in FY26 [V]; GPI paid PMI ₹21 Cr of technical-service fees/royalty [V].
  - PMI itself is now a ~42% smoke-free company (YTD-2026) with no IQOS in India because heated tobacco devices are banned under the **Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019** [V SEC; K].
- **The Modi family owns the other ~47.5%, and its governance is the second overhang.**
  - K.K. Modi group entities and trusts hold ~47.5% (K K Modi Investment & Financial Services 29.85%, Good Investment 8.29%, Quick Investment 4.30%, etc.) [K voguestock SHP, Sep-2026].
  - Bina Modi (Chairperson & MD) was paid **₹60.3 Cr** and Charu Modi (executive director) **₹98.4 Cr** in FY26 — together **~10% of PAT** [V AR; E ratio].
  - At the **24-Aug-2026 AGM, 27.4% of public-institution votes went against Charu Modi's re-appointment** (she passed with 96.4% overall because the promoter block voted for) [V scrutiniser report].
- **Illicit trade and leaf are the two transmission belts to the rest of the economy.**
  - Illicit cigarettes are **~26.1% of the cigarette market**, ~1/3 of legal volume, costing the exchequer ~₹23,000 Cr a year (industry "Tobacco Fact Sheet India 2026" cited in GPI's AR) [V AR; INDICATIVE source].
  - The **2025-26 Andhra Pradesh FCV crop (~236 mn kg) ran 66% above its 142 mn kg authorisation**; auction prices fell to **₹213/kg vs ₹249/kg** a year earlier; no-bid bales peaked ~40%; the 2026-27 authorisation has been cut to **81 mn kg** [K Deccan Chronicle, Sep-2026].
  - India's tobacco and tobacco-product exports were **₹17,192 Cr / 368.9 mn kg** in the latest year per a Lok Sabha reply [V-sib; V-snip]; FY25 unmanufactured + products were ₹16,728 Cr / 364,576 t [V AR citing IBEF].

### 1.2 The value chain (cigarettes)

1. **Farm** — FCV tobacco is grown under Tobacco Board licensing in **Andhra Pradesh** (Southern Light Soils, Southern Black Soils, Northern Light Soils, Northern Black Soils) and **Karnataka** (Mysore light soils). The Board fixes an **authorised crop size**, registers growers and barns, and runs **electronic auctions** [K]. India produces ~**1,037 mn kg** of all tobacco types a year (FCV, burley, bidi, chewing, hookah) [V GPI AR citing Tobacco Fact Sheet India 2026]. Cost of FCV cultivation ~₹200/kg (Tobacco Board letter, Feb-2026) [V-sib].
2. **Leaf merchant / processor** — buys at auction, threshes (green-leaf threshing, GLT), redries, packs and ages leaf (1–2 years; hence ITC's ~209 inventory days and GPI's 233) [V-sib; V screener]. Exporters: ITC's leaf division, GPI, VST, Alliance One, Universal (unlisted MNC dealers) [K].
3. **Primary (cut) and secondary (making & packing) manufacture** — ITC 5 cigarette factories [V-sib]; GPI factories at Ghaziabad (UP) and Rabale/Mumbai, plus contract manufacturing via subsidiary International Tobacco Company Ltd (₹73 Cr of manufacturing charges paid in FY26) [V AR; location K]; VST at Hyderabad and Toopran [K].
4. **Tax gate** — excise and NCCD are paid at factory clearance; GST (40%) on the transaction (from 1-Feb-2026 valued on retail sale price per our ITC primer [primer-ITC; not verified here]).
5. **Distribution** — company → (C&F) → distributors (GPI "1,100+ distributors, 9,000+ field force" [V]) → wholesalers → ~1 mn+ paan/kirana outlets. Advertising is banned (COTPA s.5); point-of-sale display boards limited; loose-stick sales are the norm in most states (banned in Karnataka from 30-May-2025 and Himachal since 2015) [V-snip].
6. **Consumer** — legal smokers buy mostly single sticks; "pack price" is the anchor for pricing ladders (₹10/stick, ₹15/stick price points etc.) [K].

### 1.3 Where the value is captured (FY26, legal cigarettes)

- **The state takes the biggest share.** Composite from diversified-fmcg.html for ITC after Feb-2026: of ₹100 at retail, ~₹8 trade, ~₹26 GST, ~₹43 excise + NCCD, ~₹9 ITC costs, ~₹14 ITC EBIT [V-sib, INDICATIVE]. Pre-reset the state took ~₹50 [V-sib].
- **For GPI the maker's slice is thinner.** Q1 FY27: GSV ₹5,676 Cr → GST+cess ₹1,856 Cr (32.7%), excise ₹2,614 Cr (46.1%), net revenue ₹1,206 Cr (21.2%), EBITDA ₹182 Cr (**3.2% of GSV**) [V; E ratios]. Taxes = **78.8% of GSV** (GSV excludes the retailer's margin, so the share of the shelf price is a few points lower) [E].
- FY26 full year (mostly pre-reset): GSV ₹18,379 Cr → GST+cess ₹9,258 Cr (50.4%), excise ₹2,730 Cr (14.9%), net ₹6,391 Cr (34.8%), EBITDA ₹1,584 Cr (8.6%) [V; E].
- **Farmer share is small.** FCV auction value for AP 2025-26 is ~111.6 mn kg × ₹213/kg ≈ **₹2,380 Cr** by 24-Sep (season not over) [E from K]. Even at full production (~236 mn kg) that is ~₹5,000 Cr, against ₹50,000 Cr+ of annual cigarette excise and ~₹40,000 Cr+ of cigarette GST [E].
- **Leaf merchants** earn thin margins: VST's leaf business turned over ₹301 Cr for **₹34 Cr PBIT (11%)** in FY26 [V VST AR]; ITC Agri segment PBIT margin ~7% [V-sib].

## 3. Demand drivers and end-markets

### 3.1 Consumption structure (why cigarettes are a minority product in India)
- **GATS-2 (2016-17, MoHFW/WHO/TISS)** [K, not re-fetched]: 28.6% of adults (≈267 mn) used tobacco; **10.7% smoked**, **21.4% used smokeless**; cigarettes 4.0% of adults, bidis 7.7%. GATS-1 (2009-10): 34.6%. GATS-3 results not located this session (§H).
- Legal cigarettes are **~10%** of tobacco consumption (TII/industry; also GPI AR) and **~10–15%** in diversified-fmcg.html [V; V-sib]. The rest is bidi and smokeless, which are taxed far more lightly per unit of nicotine.
- **Per-capita**: ~100 bn legal sticks ÷ ~1 bn adults ≈ **~100 legal sticks per adult per year** [E], an order of magnitude below the global average (~800–900 per adult, WHO/industry) [K, approximate]. Adding ~35 bn illicit sticks takes India to ~135 [E].
- Indian smokers buy **loose sticks**, so price points per stick (₹10, ₹15, ₹20) matter more than pack prices [K].

### 3.2 What moves demand
1. **Price/affordability** — short-run price elasticity of Indian cigarettes ~**−0.3 to −0.5** [V-sib]; Q1 FY27 implied ~−0.2 for ITC (22–23% price, −4–5% volume) flattered by pre-loading [V-sib].
2. **Tax-driven relative prices** — the gap between a legal stick and (a) a smuggled stick, (b) a bidi, (c) a chewing-tobacco sachet. The Feb-2026 specific excise hit **short cheap sticks hardest in % terms** [V-sib], pushing the marginal smoker toward bidi/smokeless/illicit.
3. **Incomes and premiumisation** — mid-premium growth was "vibrant" in FY26; the low-price segment lagged (VST AR) [V]. GPI's Stellar/Four Square Crush and VST's EDITIONS rode the mid-premium wave [V ARs].
4. **Distribution reach** — GPI's growth came with "direct retail outlet coverage increased further" (AR) [V]; Ferrero distribution is being run through the same network [V].
5. **Regulation of access** — loose-stick bans (Karnataka 2025), 100 m school zones, age 21 in Karnataka; proposed national COTPA amendments (2020 draft: loose ban, age 21) not enacted [V-snip].
6. **Enforcement against illicit trade** — the swing factor post-reset (HDFC Securities: "illicit trade is the key risk to volume assumptions") [K univest].

### 3.3 Export demand (leaf)
- 2025-26 was a soft global leaf year: "higher crop production across major origins, including Brazil, Zimbabwe, Malawi and India… downward pressure on international tobacco prices… cautious procurement… staggered buying" (VST AR) [V].
- GPI's unmanufactured exports **fell 3%** (₹2,010 → ₹1,945 Cr) and Q1 FY27 was ₹248 Cr, "impacted due to various geopolitical factors" [V]. Destinations of Indian tobacco: UAE (~US$134 mn in FY26, the largest), Belgium, Indonesia, Egypt, USA, Turkey, Korea [V AR citing IBEF].

### 5.2 Why the structure is frozen
- **FDI in cigarette manufacturing is prohibited** (FDI policy bars FDI in manufacture of cigars, cheroots, cigarillos and cigarettes of tobacco or substitutes; since 2010) [K]. Cigarette manufacture also needs an **industrial licence** under the IDR Act [K]. That is why PMI, BAT and JTI work through licensing/minority stakes (PMI–GPI, BAT–ITC/VST) rather than their own factories.
- **Advertising is banned** (COTPA 2003 s.5); 85% pictorial warnings on both sides of packs since 1-Apr-2016 [K]. Incumbent brands and distribution cannot be out-marketed.
- **Tax cascades favour scale**: specific excise per stick is identical for all makers; the bigger maker spreads fixed costs, product reformulation (30+ ITC "portfolio interventions" in Q1 FY27) and trade incentives across more sticks [V-sib].

## 6. Business-model archetypes and their economics

| Archetype | Example | Revenue model | Margin on net revenue | Capital intensity | Key risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Integrated national leader** | ITC cigarettes | Own brands across all length slabs, own leaf, own packaging | **~77% PBIT on net (FY26)**; ~63% in Q1 FY27 [V-sib; E] | Very low (segment ROCE ~270% [E]) | Tax ratchet; illicit |
| **2. Licensed-MNC challenger + leaf exporter** | Godfrey Phillips | Own brands (Four Square, Red & White, Cavanders, Stellar, North Pole) + Marlboro made for an associate distributor + leaf/contract-manufactured exports, mostly to PMI | Company OPM **24.8% (FY26)**, 15% (Q1 FY27); segment result 23.5% of segment net [V; E] — diluted by low-margin leaf exports and transfer pricing | Low (segment ROCE ~50% [E]) | PMI dependency; governance; tax |
| **3. Regional value player + leaf exporter** | VST Industries | Value/mid brands in the South and East (Total, Charms, Editions) + leaf exports (15% of turnover) | OPM **30.7% (FY26)** — but 20% in FY25 and 19% in Q1 FY27 [V screener] | Low; heavy leaf inventory | Trade-down; BAT ties but no MNC brand |
| **4. Leaf merchant / processor** | ITC Agri (part), VST leaf, Alliance One, Universal | Buy at auction, thresh, redry, export | ~7–11% PBIT (VST leaf ₹34 Cr on ₹301 Cr; ITC Agri ~7%) [V; V-sib] | Working-capital heavy | Global leaf cycle; crop gluts |
| **5. Illicit / unorganised** | Smugglers, duty-evaders, bidi makers | Price arbitrage on tax | n/a | n/a | Enforcement |

**How the economics differ under the new tax** [E]:
- A **specific** excise is neutral on price but regressive on margin: it takes the same ₹4–6 per stick whether the stick retails at ₹10 or ₹20. Makers with **longer/premium** sticks (ITC king-size, Marlboro) can price through it; makers with **short/value** portfolios (VST, GPI's mass brands) face a larger percentage price rise and more trade-down risk.
- Because GST (40%) is charged on the excise-inclusive price, **₹1 of excise costs the smoker ~₹1.40 plus trade margin** — the multiplier that makes value sticks uncompetitive against bidis and illicit sticks.
- Leaf-export earnings are untouched by the domestic tax (and benefit from a weak rupee: ₹94.9/$), which is why GPI and VST's leaf arms became relatively more important in Q1 FY27 — even though GPI's own exports fell to ₹248 Cr in that quarter [V].

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 The Feb-2026 tax reset (Force 1)

**What changed** [V-sib unless noted]:
- **Jul-2017 → Jan-2026:** cigarettes paid **28% GST + compensation cess** (5% ad valorem + ₹2,076–4,170 per 1,000 sticks by length), plus a small basic excise and **NCCD** (raised ~16% in the Feb-2023 Budget).
- **22-Sep-2025:** GST 2.0 created the **40% "demerit" slab**; tobacco stayed on 28% + cess until the cess-backed loans were repaid.
- **11–12-Dec-2025:** **Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025** notified; statutory tariff for cigarettes raised from ~₹200–735 to ~₹2,700–11,000 per 1,000 sticks [V-snip Tribune/PRS].
- **31-Dec-2025:** **Notification 03/2025-Central Excise** (s.5A exemption; F.No. CBIC-190349/72/2025-TRU; supersedes 3/2019-CE) fixes the **effective** rates below, **effective 1-Feb-2026** [K notification text, fetched via worldtradescanner mirror]. Diversified-fmcg.html cites it as G.S.R. 955(E) with a **corrigendum of 4-Feb-2026** [V-sib].
- **1-Feb-2026:** compensation cess ends; **40% GST + new excise** apply. Same day: Union Budget FY27 presented (Sunday) [V-snip].

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

1. **1930s–1970s: the colonial inheritance.** Godfrey Phillips India Ltd was incorporated in 1936 (CIN L16004MH1936PLC008587) [V AR letterhead]; ITC (Imperial Tobacco Company of India, 1910) and VST (Vazir Sultan Tobacco, Hyderabad) were the other houses [K]. PMI joined the K.K. Modi group in a joint venture in **1979** [V-snip DSIJ].
2. **2000s: Marlboro arrives; specific excise by length.** Excise by length bands has been the Indian system for decades; GPI began making Marlboro for PMI's Indian JV in the 2000s [primer; K].
3. **FY13–FY18: the excise ratchet and the lost decade.** Sharp annual excise increases (FY13–FY17) plus the GST cess (2017) → legal volumes fell from the FY12 peak (~110 bn) to roughly ~80–85 bn by FY18 [V-sib peak; K trough approx.]. Nomura: industry revenues were flat over FY13–FY18 [K asianews]. ITC's stock went nowhere for ~5 years [V-sib].
4. **FY19–FY25: the tax holiday.** Apart from the Feb-2023 NCCD hike, rates were largely stable; volumes recovered (ITC 4.5–6.5% quarterly growth through FY26 [V-sib]; industry ~7% in FY26 [V]); illicit share was contained (industry claim) [K]. GPI's volume more than doubled FY22–FY26 [V]; ITC re-rated from ~₹200 to ~₹500 [V-sib]; ITC peaked at ₹490 (27-Sep-2024), VST at ₹481 (6-Sep-2024), GPI at ₹3,691 (5-Sep-2025, bonus-adjusted) [V screener].
5. **2024: GPI simplifies.** Exit from 24Seven (announced Apr-2024; Delhi court lifted the injunction on exit on 31-Jul-2024); Samir Modi not re-appointed to the board (7-Aug-2024); Charu Modi appointed [primer; V-snip].
6. **2025: GST 2.0 and the bonus.** 22-Sep-2025 GST 2.0; GPI 2:1 bonus (record date 16-Sep-2025); GPI stock at record highs [V-snip; V].
7. **Dec-2025 to Feb-2026: the reset.** Excise Act amended (Dec-2025), Notification 03/2025-CE (31-Dec-2025), effective 1-Feb-2026. Cigarette stocks fell **10–25% in January 2026** [K outlookmoney]; GPI −29% in January alone (₹2,825 → ₹1,997 month-end) [V screener chart]. Price hikes of ₹22–25 per 10-stick pack followed in February [V-snip]; GPI jumped 16% intraday on 18-Feb-2026 on price-hike reports [K].
8. **FY27 Q1: the first full quarter.** Excise 8× (GPI) / 7.7× (ITC); profits −35% to −46%; volume −2% (GPI) to −5% (ITC). Tobacco Board/AP leaf glut; 2026-27 crop authorisation cut to 81 mn kg [K]. Citi upgraded ITC to Buy on 5-Oct-2026 [K]. All three stocks at or near 52-week lows on 23-Oct-2026 [V].

## 11. Technology and disruption risks

1. **Reduced-risk products are banned in India** (PECA 2019 covers heat-not-burn) [K]. If the ban were ever relaxed, PMI (IQOS) — not GPI — would own the device franchise; GPI's licence covers Marlboro cigarettes [V] and the primer notes PMI kept smoke-free products outside the GPI relationship [primer]. Disruption risk to GPI is therefore asymmetric: an RRP opening helps PMI more than GPI.
2. **Nicotine pouches (ZYN etc.)** — PMI shipped 3.2 bn ZYN pouches in Q3-2026 globally [V]; India status: not clearly covered by PECA; FSSAI/drug regulators may treat as nicotine products (§H).
3. **Format engineering** — slimmer/shorter sticks ("sleek", "micro") to drop into lower excise slabs: ITC launched Gold Flake Indie Paan Sleek, American Club Magic Mix Sleek etc. [V-sib]. Expect the government to respond with slab redefinitions (the notification already counts filters as at least 11 mm) [K].
4. **Track-and-trace / tax stamps** — WHO FCTC Protocol-style digital tax stamps would help legal makers against illicit trade; India has not adopted them nationally [K, verify] (§H).
5. **Quick commerce** — 70–80% CAGR channel (IBEF, cited in GPI AR) [V]; age-verification and COTPA compliance unclear (§H). Quick commerce also killed 24Seven's convenience-store economics [primer].
6. **Leaf technology** — GPI's Integrated Production System (IPS) on the HDBRG 2025 crop; burley push; new varieties (Sun-Cured Virginia, Kurnool Rustica, Lanka, Lalchoupadia) [V AR].

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert analyst leads with)

1. **The Feb-2026 tax reset turned a stable tollgate into a pricing contest.** A specific excise of ₹2,050–8,500 per 1,000 sticks on top of 40% GST lifted tax from ~50–53% to ~65–70% of price; the Centre budgets basic excise at ₹90,810 Cr in FY27, double FY26 RE. Makers' profit per stick fell ~1/3 (ITC) to ~1/2 (GPI) in one quarter. Recovery requires ~12–15% more price on top of the ~20%+ already taken. (§7.1, §9)
2. **Volume elasticity vs illicit leakage.** History (FY13–FY18) says legal volumes fall and illicit rises when tax ratchets; Q1 FY27 volumes fell only 2–5% because of phased pricing and pre-loading. Illicit is already ~26% (industry). The key question for FY27–FY28 is whether volume declines stay in single digits. (§3, §12)
3. **The Philip Morris embrace (GPI-specific).** PMI owns 25.1% of GPI, licenses Marlboro, distributes it through a company 24.8% owned by GPI, buys ~2/3 of GPI's exports and receives royalties. This is GPI's moat (only Indian home of Marlboro) and its dependency. (§5.3, Part 2)
4. **Governance and family control (GPI-specific).** ~47.5% sits with K.K. Modi entities controlled by Bina Modi; the Samir Modi dispute is live; executive-promoter pay (₹159 Cr, ~10% of PAT) and a 27% institutional vote against Charu Modi's re-appointment are the measurable signs. (Part 2)
5. **Leaf cycle and export dependence.** A 2025-26 AP glut (236 vs 142 mn kg authorised), falling auction prices (₹213 vs ₹249/kg) and soft global demand hit leaf exporters, while the weak rupee helps. GPI's exports fell 3% in FY26 and Q1 FY27 was weak. (§3.3, §8)
6. **Frozen structure and the closed door.** FDI ban + licensing + ad ban mean no new entrant; the competitive game is ITC vs GPI in share and price architecture, with VST squeezed in value segments. (§5.2)
7. **No smoke-free hedge.** India bans HNB/e-cigarettes; Indian makers carry 100% combustible risk while global peers move to ~40% smoke-free. (§10, §11)

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Cigarette maker, per period:**
Net revenue = Σ over slabs [ sticks_s × ( RSP_s × (1 − trade margin) ÷ (1 + GST 40%) − excise_s − NCCD_s ) ]
EBIT = Net revenue − (leaf + materials + conversion + distribution + overhead) per stick × sticks
PAT = (EBIT + other income − interest) × (1 − tax rate)

**Worked example, GPI value filter stick (≤65 mm), post-reset [E, ILLUSTRATIVE]:**
- Retail price ₹10.00 per stick; trade margin ~8% → ₹9.20 to company incl. GST.
- Strip GST at 40%: ₹9.20 ÷ 1.40 = ₹6.57 ex-GST (= "gross revenue").
- Excise ₹2.10 (slab 2402 20 30) + NCCD and legacy duties ~₹0.3–0.5 [E] → ~₹2.50.
- Net revenue ≈ ₹4.07 per stick.
- Costs (leaf, paper, filter, conversion, distribution) ~₹1.2–1.6 per stick [E].
- EBIT ≈ ₹2.5–2.9 per stick (~60–70% of net) — **on paper**. GPI's actual company EBITDA was ₹0.33 per stick in Q1 FY27 [E from V], which shows that (a) much of its volume sells at far lower RSPs than ₹10 per stick, (b) price increases were phased ("not in one go"), and (c) GPI absorbed part of the tax. Use the equation for direction; calibrate with reported numbers.

**Same equation, ITC king-size, from diversified-fmcg.html [V-sib]:** RSP ₹14 → trade ₹1.12 → GST ₹3.68 → excise + NCCD ₹6.00 → ITC net ₹3.20 → cost ₹1.20 → PBIT ₹2.00.

**Leaf exporter:** Revenue = tonnes × US$/kg × ₹/$; margin = export price − (auction price + processing ~₹30–50/kg [E] + freight). FY25 FCV: 146,954 t × US$6.10/kg = US$895.9 mn = ₹7,571 Cr [V AR citing IBEF].

## 4.5 New insights the first pass missed (for the writer)

1. **ITC is bigger, and GPI smaller, by volume than Part 1 assumed.**
   - CRISIL's ~19% (GPI) and ~7% (VST) volume shares put the legal market at ~120–125 bn sticks and ITC near ~88 bn.
   - The Q1 FY27 excise split (ITC 75.7 / GPI 19.7 / VST 4.6) corroborates this.
   - Consequences: ITC's per-stick economics are lower (net ~₹3.1, PBIT ~₹2.4 in FY26) than the V-sib ₹3.9/₹3.0. GPI's "challenger" story is about **value under-capture** (13% of value on ~19% of volume), not a hidden volume lead.
2. **Profit is concentrating in the price-setter.**
   - The listed profit pool fell 36% in Q1 FY27. ITC's share rose to ~95%; GPI's segment margin halved to ~12%; VST's OPM fell to 19%.
   - A specific excise rewards whoever can push price through without losing sticks, and that is ITC with ~73% of volume and a deep slab-engineered portfolio.
3. **GPI's Marlboro economics sit largely outside GPI's P&L.**
   - PMITPL earned **₹1,142 Cr** in CY2025, about 75% of GPI's own PAT. GPI books only its 24.8% (₹283 Cr).
   - The Marlboro channel is also hit: GPI's share of associates' profit fell 56% in Q1 FY27.
   - PMITPL is a single customer worth 30% of GPI's revenue, with undisclosed contract terms.
4. **The GST base change matters as much as the excise.** GST moved from 28% of transaction value to **40% of RSP** (CGST Rule 31D). Tax now rises automatically with every retail price increase. That widens the illicit gap at the bottom of the ladder, where illicit sells at ₹10–15/stick against ~₹19 for Kings Slims.
5. **The state is building an anti-evasion toolkit, not just raising rates.** From 1-Feb-2026 the Lok Sabha reply lists:
   - a capacity-based excise levy for specified tobacco products;
   - monthly capacity/production declarations;
   - withdrawal of the IGST-refund route on tobacco exports;
   - track-and-trace for evasion-prone goods.
   The IGST change could hurt exporters' working capital, which is relevant for GPI and VST leaf [E].
6. **VST is not just a BAT satellite.** R. K. Damani's Bright Star Investments holds **25.95%**, close to BAT's 32.16%. VST also changed auditors (to Price Waterhouse) and CEO in 2026.
7. **ITC is buying its brand stack from BAT.** It bought ₹404 Cr of brand assets in FY26 (Benson & Hedges, Dunhill, Rothmans of Pall Mall) and launched Rothmans GT/XT in Q1 FY27. Combined with BAT's 2025 sell-down to 22.9%, this points to ITC **owning the international trademarks it uses in India**, which cuts future royalty or ownership friction [E interpretation].
8. **GPI's governance metrics are now quantifiable.** Pay at 685:1 (Bina Modi) and 1,119:1 (Charu Modi) against a median employee whose pay fell 0.92%. A ₹759 Cr investment in its own promoter company. 27.4% institutional dissent. CRISIL's explicit "monitorable" on the family dispute.
