## The forces
1. The HRC–coking-coal spread, plus the rupee, sets integrated profits. The worked spread is ~₹30,400/t (Oct-26) against ~₹18,400/t at the Dec-25 trough.
2. China's surplus (119 Mt of exports in 2025) runs into India's trade wall: safeguard duty 12% → 11.5% → 11% to Apr-2028, a $675/t floor, and FTA leakage. India is a net importer again in Apr–Sep 2026.
3. Domestic demand is growing 7–9% on government capex; construction and infrastructure are ~60% of demand.
4. Raw-material security and the mining regime: auction premiums of 90–150%, Tata's legacy mine leases lapse in March 2030, and the SC 2024 levy ruling has been followed by MMDR 2026.
5. Decarbonisation costs are arriving before the technology: CBAM's definitive phase, EU quotas, CCTS targets and the green-steel taxonomy.
