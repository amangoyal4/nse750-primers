## The five defining forces
1. **Formalisation and consolidation.** RERA, GST, the 2018 NBFC crisis and Ind AS 115 raised the cost of being small. The listed share of sales has roughly doubled in six years: 28 listed developers booked ~₹1.95 lakh cr in FY26, about 30% of top-7-city value.
2. **Premiumisation: value up, volume flat.** Top-7-city units fell ~14% in CY2025 while value rose to over ₹6 lakh cr. Homes above ₹1 cr are 55% of sales, and affordable supply has collapsed.
3. **Land is the profit pool.** Margin is made at land acquisition. Business development is an arms race (record land deals in 2025, large QIPs), with JDA and redevelopment as the capital-light routes.
4. **The rate and affordability turn.** The RBI cut 125 bp in 2025, then hiked to 5.50% on 7-Oct-2026. Unsold stock is ~6.3 lakh homes (~19 months), and Q1 FY27 bookings for the 14 reporting members fell sharply on launch timing. The report reads this as late cycle, not the end of the cycle.
5. **The annuity pivot and REIT-isation.** Office leasing set records on GCC demand, and the six REITs are worth over ₹2.17 lakh cr. DLF, Phoenix, Prestige, Brigade and Oberoi are building rental income as a second engine.
