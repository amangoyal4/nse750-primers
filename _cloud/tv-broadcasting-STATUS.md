## h1
Indian TV broadcasting: <em>a shrinking screen and one giant</em>

## Forces
1. Linear-TV shrinkage: 11.5m pay homes lost in CY2025; free TV (~5.3 crore homes) now outnumbers pay DTH (4.86 crore, Jun-26, −13% YoY).
2. TV's ad recession: CY2025 TV ad −10% to ₹26,300 Cr; digital is 63% of ad spend; the 12-minute ad cap was removed in Aug-2026; ratings blackout.
3. One giant: JioStar's FY26 operating revenue of ₹31,048 Cr is about 2.1× the three members combined; 34% viewing share; IPL/ICC rights.
4. OTT economics: ZEE5 near breakeven; JioHotstar scale; Sun NXT undisclosed.
5. Regulation, sports and governance: TRAI NTO and its 2024 amendments, the Television Ratings Policy 2026 and the BARC suspension; the Zee–Sony collapse, the SEBI order, warrants and the US$1.097bn ICC claim; Sun's related-party distribution.
