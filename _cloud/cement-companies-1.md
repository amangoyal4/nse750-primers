**Bases and conventions (read first)**
- "Screener EBITDA" means screener.in "Operating Profit" (Sales − Expenses). It excludes other income. Company "EBITDA/PBIDT" figures often include other income (UltraTech's PBIDT does), so the two bases are labelled separately everywhere.
- Screener "Sales" = revenue from operations, including other operating income. It can differ slightly from a company's "Net Sales" (for example UltraTech FY26: screener ₹88,512 Cr vs the company's net sales of ₹87,384 Cr).
- Net debt (screener basis) = Borrowings − Investments. It ignores bank deposits and cash held outside "Investments", so I also give the company-reported figure wherever one exists.
- EV = market cap (13 Oct 2026) + the latest net debt (basis stated). EV/EBITDA uses FY26 screener EBITDA, with the TTM figure in brackets.
- Market caps and prices are screener.in's "Current Price" and "Market Cap" as shown on 13 Oct 2026 (that day's or the latest close).
- **Q2 FY27 (Sep-26 quarter) is not out for any of the five as of 13 Oct 2026.** Every screener quarter table ends at Jun-2026. UltraTech's board meets on **Mon 19 Oct 2026** to approve Q2 results [V: UltraTech BM notice dated 1 Sep 2026, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ULTRACEMCO1_01092026142819_SE_Letter_-_BM_Notice.pdf]. The other four usually report from late October to mid-November [K: past results calendars].
- Industry context used across the packs: GST on cement was cut from 28% to 18% from 22 Sep 2025 (56th GST Council meeting, 3 Sep 2025) [K: GST Council/CBIC notification]. Q1 FY27 costs rose on the West Asia war: petcoke went to US$160/t before easing to $130–135/t, against $110–115/t pre-war [V: Arihant Dalmia note], and packing-bag prices rose from ₹9.5 to ₹14/bag [V: same]. Industry volume growth for Q1 FY27 was put at 7–8% [V: Arihant UltraTech note; PL Dalmia note].

### 1h. Capex, guidance, M&A
- FY27 guidance: **double-digit volume growth** (industry 7–8%). Q2 cost +₹130–140/t. Net debt/EBITDA below 1× [V: Arihant].
- India Cements: EBITDA/t has climbed from ₹386 (Q2 FY26) to ₹400, ₹509 and ₹603 (Q1 FY27). The target is **₹1,000/t by FY28**, backed by ~₹2,000 Cr of efficiency capex. ICL's green power share is targeted to go from 3% to 86% by end-FY28 [V: Arihant; ICICI].
- Kesoram cement (~10.75 MTPA) was merged in FY25. The 8.69% Star Cement minority stake cost ₹851 Cr [K: primer sources; Business Standard 2025].

### 1i. Story bullets
1. **Scale leader growing faster than the industry.** Q1 FY27 volume rose +12–13% against an industry 7–8%. 200 MTPA domestic capacity was reached a year early, with ~242 MTPA in view by FY28. The top-2 consolidation (UltraTech + Adani) keeps tightening.
2. **Integration pays off.** ICL and Kesoram brands are fully migrated. ICL EBITDA/t is ₹603 and rising toward ₹1,000/t, which is the near-term earnings bridge.
3. **Cost buffers.** Green power at 47% and a lead distance of 360 km absorbed the war-led fuel shock. EBITDA/t still rose YoY (₹1,214 vs ₹1,198), although realisation gains were only ~1–3%.
4. **Balance sheet held through an M&A spree.** Net debt/EBITDA is 0.87×, FY26 OCF was ₹14,398 Cr, and a ₹240 special dividend was paid. The new Cables & Wires business is a capital-allocation watch-point.

### 1j. Risks
- Q2 FY27 cost inflation (+₹130–140/t) during monsoon seasonality, so EBITDA/t is likely to dip QoQ.
- Industry-wide capacity additions (an Adani push) could cap pricing. Realisation growth is only ~1% YoY on domestic grey.
- The valuation (19.5× EV/EBITDA, 4.1× P/B) already assumes ₹1,300+/t EBITDA by FY28 (ICICI estimate ₹1,308/t).
- Diversification into Cables & Wires carries execution and return-on-capital risk. Promoter stake drift (−0.85 pp) is to be explained.

### 2i. Risks

- The South is the most oversupplied region. Adani (Penna, Ambuja) and Ramco or Dalmia additions add to that.
- The Tamil Nadu mineral-bearing land tax (₹160/t of limestone [V: Ramco Q1 FY27 release]) applies to ICL's TN plants.
- Fuel: the shift from petcoke to coal raised fuel cost per kcal to ₹1.96.
- Consolidated vs standalone PAT gaps, legacy disputed liabilities and provisions (MOSL adjusted for "provision for disputed liabilities for earlier years").
