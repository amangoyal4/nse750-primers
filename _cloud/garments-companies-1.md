**Units and conventions**
- ₹ crore (Cr) = 10 million. mn pcs = million pieces. FY26 = Apr-2025 to Mar-2026; Q1 FY27 = Apr–Jun 2026; Q2 FY27 = Jul–Sep 2026. All five members have a March year-end.
- **"OP" = screener operating profit = revenue − operating expenses, before other income.** This is the EBITDA basis used in all tables and aggregates. Several companies' own "EBITDA" **includes other income**: Gokaldas (FY26 company EBITDA ₹434 Cr = OP 357 + other income 77), Raymond Lifestyle (Q1 FY27 company EBITDA ₹135 Cr = OP 90 + other income 45) and Page (Q1 FY27 company EBITDA ₹289 Cr = screener OP 289, so the same here). Pearl reports "Adj. EBITDA" excluding ESOP cost and excluding other income (Q1 FY27 ₹164 Cr = screener OP 164).
- **PAT basis: "attributable reported" = screener's "Profit for EPS" line** (profit attributable to owners, after exceptional items). Screener's headline "Net Profit" row for consolidated companies is **before** the minority adjustment. It differs for Pearl (FY26 Net Profit 270 vs attributable 278: minorities carried a ₹8 Cr loss) and Kitex (Net Profit −13 vs attributable +6: minorities carried a ₹19 Cr loss). Pre-exceptional attributable PAT = screener's "Profit for PE" line, shown in a separate column.
- **Screener's P/E = market cap ÷ TTM "Profit for PE" (pre-exceptional, attributable).** I checked this for all four positive-earnings members. Raymond Lifestyle: TTM Profit-for-PE = 78.61 + 82.44 + 10.73 − 22.59 = ₹149 Cr; ₹3,367 Cr ÷ 149 = **22.6×**, which matches screener. On reported FY26 PAT (₹46 Cr) the P/E is **~73×** [E]. This matters: our primer says "~27×" on ₹3,980 Cr ÷ ₹46 Cr, which is arithmetically ~87×.
- EV/EBITDA = (m-cap + borrowings − investments) ÷ FY26 OP on the screener basis. Where the company reports its own net debt/cash, that version is shown too, and the preferred one is stated. Screener "borrowings" **include lease liabilities** (Ind AS 116) for all five members. This inflates the debt of store-heavy Page and Raymond Lifestyle and of Gokaldas.
- Screener's **inventory days and debtor days** are on screener's own basis (inventory ÷ cost of materials-type denominator). They are not comparable with company-reported "NWC days". Use them for ranking, not for prose.

## READ THIS FIRST: eight definitional traps

1. **Page's numbers are standalone (= company).** Screener has no consolidated page for PAGEIND. Do not mark this as a basis mismatch; Page has no meaningful subsidiaries [screener].
2. **Two members' P&L include material "exceptional" items, and the sign differs.**
   - Page FY26: −₹35 Cr pre-tax (−₹26 Cr after tax) in Q3 FY26 (Dec-25). Pre-exceptional PAT ₹790 Cr vs reported ₹764 Cr [screener schedules]. Likely the new Labour Codes' gratuity/leave remeasurement that hit many Indian companies in Q3 FY26 [E — verify in the Q3 notes].
   - Raymond Lifestyle FY26: −₹97 Cr pre-tax / −₹64 Cr after tax (−₹39.6 Cr in Dec-25 and −₹62.8 Cr in Mar-26 quarters) → pre-exceptional PAT ₹110 Cr vs reported ₹46 Cr [screener].
   - Kitex FY26: a **+₹39 Cr after-tax exceptional gain** (₹14 Cr in Sep-25 quarter). Ex-gain, the group lost ₹52 Cr (₹33 Cr attributable) [screener].
   - **Gokaldas: conflict.** Screener tags ₹24 Cr pre-tax / ₹35 Cr after tax of FY26 as "exceptional" (pre-exc PAT ₹66 Cr). But the company's FY26 table calls ₹100.1 Cr "PAT before exceptional items" [V Q1 FY27 PPT p.10]. Treat Gokaldas reported = ₹100 Cr and do not use screener's ₹66 Cr in prose. In aggregates, the pre-exceptional column uses screener's ₹66 Cr for consistency, with a note.
3. **Minority interests run "backwards" at Pearl and Kitex.** In both, minorities absorb losses (new-unit start-ups), so attributable PAT > group PAT. Pearl FY26: 270 → 278; Q1 FY27: 99 → 101 [V PPT]. Kitex FY26: −13 → +6; Q1 FY27: −17 → −9 [screener]. That is why press reports of Kitex's Q1 loss conflict (₹17 Cr DSIJ/univest = group; ~₹9 Cr Craytheon = attributable) [V-snip]. **Kitex FY26 attributable EPS is +₹0.30 although the group lost money.** The minority in Kitex's Telangana arm (Kitex Apparel Parks) suggests it is not 100% owned — VERIFY.
4. **Gokaldas "revenue" in our primer is total income.** Primer ~₹4,065 Cr = revenue from operations ₹3,988 Cr + other income ₹77 Cr. Primer EBITDA ₹434 Cr (10.7%) includes other income; screener OP is ₹357 Cr (9.0%). The company's **adjusted** EBITDA (ex ₹97 Cr US-tariff discount) is ₹530 Cr (13.0%) [V PPT]. Quote the basis every time. Same for Q1 FY27: income ₹1,180 Cr vs revenue ₹1,154 Cr; EBITDA ₹139 Cr (11.8%, incl. OI) vs OP ₹112 Cr (9.7%).
5. **Raymond Lifestyle "debt-free / net cash ₹179 Cr" vs screener borrowings ₹2,186 Cr.**
   - The company figure excludes lease liabilities (store leases) and nets treasury investments: **net cash ₹179 Cr (Mar-26) → ₹154 Cr (Jun-26)**, from (₹55 Cr) net debt in Jun-25 [V PPT; primer].
   - Screener: borrowings ₹2,186 Cr − investments ₹1,037 Cr = net debt ₹1,149 Cr (lease-inclusive).
   - **Use the company net-cash figure for prose.** Show EV/EBITDA both ways (4.7× company vs 6.7× screener basis). Interest cost ₹248 Cr in FY26 (incl. lease interest) is real either way.
6. **Raymond Lifestyle history is short and odd.** Screener shows only FY24, FY25, FY26 and quarters from Sep-23. FY24 (₹6,535 Cr revenue, ₹2,645 Cr PAT) is a **pre-demerger carve-out year**: PAT is swollen by a **₹2,159 Cr exceptional gain** in other income (demerger/restructuring accounting) [screener schedules]. FY24 quarterly EPS is blank (shares not yet listed). Treat FY25 (first listed year, ₹6,177 Cr / PAT ₹38 Cr) as the first clean base. There is **no 9-month or other odd period on screener**; all three are 12-month March years. No 3-, 5- or 10-year CAGR is available.
7. **Kitex: the FY26 revenue fall (−32%) is real, not a basis change.** Screener consolidated exists for every year (FY25 ₹983 Cr consolidated). The primer's caveat ("FY23–FY25 largely standalone") is unnecessary for revenue, since the subsidiaries had no revenue before Warangal's COD in Aug-2025. **The FY26 margin collapse (OPM 21% → 4.5%) is consolidated-only:** standalone Kerala made ~₹57 Cr PAT [primer].
8. **Pearl's revenue is mostly made outside India.** FY26: ₹1,081 Cr India-manufactured vs ₹3,944 Cr from Bangladesh/Vietnam/Indonesia/Guatemala [V PPT p.10]. Q1 FY27: ₹340 Cr India vs ₹1,188 Cr rest of world. "Indian export share" and "US share" therefore mean different things for Pearl than for Gokaldas or Kitex. Pearl's US-tariff exposure is to **the tariff on its non-Indian origins** too.
