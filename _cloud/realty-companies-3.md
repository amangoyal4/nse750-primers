## Conventions used throughout

- **Tags:** **[V]** = verified this session from a page actually fetched (screener.in via the fetch script, a company filing or presentation PDF downloaded from nsearchives.nseindia.com, a broker PDF, or a news page fetched in full). **[V-press]** = fetched press or aggregator page (secondary, not a primary filing). **[K]** = known from memory, with the source named. **[E]** = estimate or my own arithmetic on [V] inputs.
- **Screener basis:** every Snapshot P&L, balance-sheet and ratio figure comes from `https://www.screener.in/company/<NSE>/consolidated/`, fetched 13-Oct-2026 [V]. Hemisphere has no consolidated page, so its figures come from the standalone page `https://www.screener.in/company/HEMIPROP/`. Cash comes from screener's balance-sheet schedule API (`/api/company/<id>/schedules/?parent=Other Assets`), also fetched 13-Oct-2026 [V].
- **Revenue and EBITDA definitions:**
  - Screener "Sales" = revenue from operations.
  - Screener "Operating Profit" excludes other income.
  - Companies (NESCO, Puravankara, TARC) usually report EBITDA on *total income*, which includes other income. Their EBITDA is therefore higher than screener OP. The basis is stated on each line.
- **Net debt:** screener "Borrowings − Investments" ignores cash and bank deposits, which sit in "Other assets". Each pack shows a screener-style figure (borrowings − cash equivalents − investments) and, where one is disclosed, the **company-reported net debt**. The company figure is preferred for developers because it uses the company's own debt and cash definitions. Those definitions can be narrower: Puravankara, for example, excludes ₹1,410 Cr of "payable-when-able" NCDs.
- **EV/EBITDA:** EV = (m-cap on 13-Oct-2026 + borrowings − cash − liquid investments) ÷ FY26 or TTM screener OP, unless stated otherwise. For developers it is shown for completeness only; Ind AS 115 revenue recognition makes it close to meaningless.
- **Pre-sales vs revenue:** Ind AS 115 recognises developer revenue on handover, so revenue lags bookings by 2–5 years. Pre-sales, collections, net debt and launch pipeline (the native KPIs) are the operating gauges.
- **Listing status:** all six companies are still listed on NSE and BSE as of 13-Oct-2026; live screener pages carry Jun-2026 (Puravankara: Sep-2026) shareholding. Corporate actions:
  - **Valor Estate** demerged its hospitality business into **Advent Hotels International** (effective 1-Jul-2025, record date 18-Jul-2025, 1:10 ratio, listed 13-Nov-2025).
  - **TARC** is itself the 2020–21 demerger of Anant Raj's real-estate business.
  - **Hemisphere** is the government's 2005 carve-out of surplus VSNL land.
  - No new merger or demerger is pending for any member.
- **Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026):** as of 13-Oct-2026 I could not find a published Q2 FY27 operational update for any of the six. TARC published its Q1 update on 7-Jul-2026, so its Q2 update is likely imminent. A PL Capital sector preview dated 8-Oct-2026 [V] still shows Sunteck's Q2 FY27 as an *estimate*. All quarterly figures below are therefore Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026).

## NESCO Ltd (NSE: NESCO · BSE: 505355)

**Archetype:** not a developer. NESCO is a debt-light **landlord and annuity owner** on a single ~61-acre freehold plot at Goregaon (E), Mumbai, on the Western Express Highway. It has five reported engines:
- **Realty:** NESCO IT Park Towers 3 and 4, about 1.78 mn sq ft chargeable.
- **Bombay Exhibition Centre (BEC):** about 0.77 mn sq ft of halls.
- **Foods:** catering and banqueting.
- **Indabrator:** legacy surface-preparation equipment.
- **Wayside Amenities (new):** highway fuel and food plazas for NHLML.

It does not sell inventory, so pre-sales KPIs do not apply. Its native KPIs are leasable area, occupancy, BEC utilisation and segment revenue.

### Story bullets

1. **The annuity is full; growth now needs concrete.** Towers 3 and 4 are 100% leased, so near-term Realty growth comes only from rent escalations. Tower 2 (2.25 mn sq ft chargeable per the deck) could roughly double the IT Park, but it is years from rent.
2. **The "never borrows" story has ended.** Borrowings went from ₹1 Cr to ₹272 Cr in FY26, with a ₹222 Cr inflow from financing. NESCO is still heavily net cash (~₹1,273 Cr), but the primer's "without borrowing a rupee" framing is outdated.
3. **Margins are diluting.** OPM fell from 60–67% (FY23–25) to 53% (FY26) and 48% (Q1 FY27) as Foods and the new Wayside highway-amenities business (₹400 Cr capex, fuel and food plazas) grow faster than rent.
4. **The valuation is cheap on cash flow but the stock is de-rating.** At 17.6× P/E and ~12× EV/EBITDA, with ~17% of m-cap in net cash, the shares sit near their 52-week low (₹996). The tempo of deploying the cash pile remains the debate.

### Story bullets

1. **Proof of concept, almost.** Bopkhel is HPIL's first major sale: ₹640.5 Cr gross against ₹131 Cr already paid as premium, roughly 20% of m-cap. Closing it and receiving the cash is the near-term catalyst; MoHUA approval is pending.
