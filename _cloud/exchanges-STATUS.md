## Forces
1. SEBI's one-weekly-expiry rule made expiry day a regulated duopoly and rebuilt BSE (options premium share ~3% FY24 → 31.5% Q1 FY27).
2. The state's take: STT hikes (Jul-2024; 1-Apr-2026 options 0.15%, futures 0.05%), SEBI FY26 loss study (87.7% of individuals lost; ₹91,685 Cr; STT 27% vs exchange fees 16% of traders' costs).
3. Premium, not notional: MCX after the bullion shock (bullion premium/notional 1.03% → 0.35%; energy 73% of options premium; Q1 FY27 revenue −21% QoQ).
4. IEX, a toll booth the regulator is redesigning: market coupling (CERC order 23-Jul-2025 → APTEL 13-Feb-2026 → draft regs 17-Apr-2026 → SC declined to intervene 3-Aug-2026; final regs not notified as of 25-Oct-2026), ₹10/kWh cap.
5. Operating leverage, cash and valuation: ~70% margins, members at 50.5× FY26 PAT; NSE listed 24-Sep-2026 as the new benchmark.
