## Forces
1. Volume compounds, price is cut — the wedge between accounts/SIP/AUM growth and revenue growth (telescopic RTA fees, KRA tariff reset 1-Apr-2026).
2. Regulated duopolies whose ceiling is SEBI, not competition (MII rules, IPF levy, MF Regulations 2026 with the registrar fee inside the base expense ratio).
3. Activity vs stock: CDSL's earnings follow market activity (FY26 PAT −13.5%); the RTAs' follow average AUM.
4. Two ways to diversify: CAMS builds (margin back to 46.4%), KFin buys (Ascent 51%, 49% put ~₹579–619 Cr).
5. Valuation at 34–54× earnings and what has to happen.
