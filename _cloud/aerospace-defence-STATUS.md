## The forces
1. Domestic demand is ring-fenced: 75% of capital acquisition is reserved for domestic industry, FY26 AoNs were ₹6.81 lakh Cr, and orders surged after Operation Sindoor.
2. Execution bottlenecks, not demand, set revenue: GE F404 engines for the LCA Mk1A and imported seekers for BDL. HAL's NWC rose from 37 to 301 days.
3. The PSU franchise is opening to private players: AMCA is proceeding without HAL, and draft DAP 2026 proposes a level playing field and 60% indigenous content.
4. Exports and the global aero supply chain are a second customer: exports went from ₹1,522 Cr in FY17 to ₹38,424 Cr in FY26, and Boeing and Airbus source from India.
5. Valuations already discount the pipeline: HAL has re-rated from 13× to 35×, BEL from 20× to 45× and BDL from 21× to 79×; private names trade at 80–175×.
