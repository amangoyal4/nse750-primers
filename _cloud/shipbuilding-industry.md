**Units, conventions and FX**
- ₹ crore (Cr) = 10 million. ₹ lakh crore = ₹1 trillion. All three annual reports present their accounts in **₹ lakh**; every figure here has been converted to ₹ Cr (÷100).
- FY26 = Apr-2025 to Mar-2026. Q1 FY27 = Apr–Jun 2026. Q2 FY27 = Jul–Sep 2026. All three members have a March year-end.
- **FX: ₹94.9 = US$1** (19-Oct-2026). It is used for all USD conversions unless a deal's own USD/₹ pair is quoted. KRW 1 tn ≈ ₹6,400 Cr [E, at ~KRW 1,480/$].
- **Tonnage terms.** **DWT** = deadweight (cargo-carrying capacity). **GT** = gross tonnage (enclosed volume). **CGT** = compensated gross tonnage: GT weighted by ship-type complexity. CGT is the standard measure of shipyard output and market share. **Displacement** (tonnes) is the warship measure. A 7,500-t destroyer and a 7,500-DWT cargo ship are completely different jobs.
- Naval jargon used below:
  - **AoN** = Acceptance of Necessity by the Defence Acquisition Council (DAC), the first formal step.
  - **RFP** = request for proposal. **L1** = lowest bidder.
  - **CNC** = contract negotiation committee. **CCS** = Cabinet Committee on Security (final approval for big deals).
  - **DPSU** = defence PSU. **MoD** = Ministry of Defence. **MoPSW** = Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways.

## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **"Order book" means unexecuted balance, not contract value.**
   - MDL's order-book table lists *project value* (e.g. P-17A ₹27,257 Cr; P-75 ₹29,981 Cr) next to *balance* (₹7,587 Cr; ₹1,678 Cr) [V MDL Q1 deck]. Only the **balance** (₹18,218 Cr at 30-Jun-2026) is the order book.
   - The balance also includes "pending work/supply of spares of delivered vessels". That is why P-15B shows ₹283 Cr with 0 ships pending.
   - Press often quotes total contract value as "order win". **Never add contract values to order books.**
2. **Order book basis differs by company and by date.**
   - **Cochin:** the standalone shipbuilding book was "~₹18,900 Cr" at Mar-26 (Defence 12,150 / Commercial-domestic 725 / Export 6,025) [V AR].
   - The **Sep-26 deck** shows "**~₹21,900 Cr**" = shipbuilding **₹20,700 Cr** (Defence 11,900 / Domestic 1,600 / Export 7,200; 78 vessels) **plus repair ₹1,200 Cr** [V deck]. The Sep-26 shipbuilding figure appears to include subsidiaries (Udupi ~₹2,100 Cr, Hooghly ~₹200 Cr) [E — inferred from the jump in domestic/export vessel counts from 39 to 67].
   - **GRSE** quotes a **total** book (₹13,596 Cr at 30-Jun-26), of which shipbuilding is ₹12,980 Cr [V transcript]. **MDL** quotes one total (₹18,218 Cr) that includes ONGC offshore EPC work (₹2,774 Cr) [V deck].
   - Compare "naval shipbuilding book" only after stripping repair, offshore and engineering.
3. **"Order inflow" is rarely disclosed — derive it.** Inflow ≈ closing book − opening book + revenue booked in the period [E]. Use **standalone** revenue where the book is standalone.
   - MDL FY26: 20,535 − 32,260 + 12,840 = **~₹1,100 Cr** [E] → book-to-bill **~0.09×**.
   - GRSE Q1 FY27: 13,596 − 15,324 + 1,815 = **~₹90 Cr** [E].
   - FY26 was a near-zero inflow year for the Navy yards.
4. **Standalone vs consolidated.**
   - MDL consolidated now includes Colombo Dockyard (51%). Goa Shipyard (47.21%) comes in as associate income.
   - Cochin consolidated includes Udupi-CSL (FY26 turnover ₹618 Cr) and Hooghly-CSL (₹123 Cr) [V deck].
   - GRSE is standalone only.
   - This file uses **consolidated for MDL and Cochin, standalone for GRSE** — the only reported basis for each.
5. **Revenue from operations vs "total income".** Company press releases often headline **total income**, which includes other income (mainly interest on deposits).
   - GRSE Q1 FY27: "total income ₹1,914 Cr" vs revenue from operations **₹1,815 Cr** [V transcript].
   - MDL FY26: "total revenue" ₹14,146 Cr vs contract revenue **₹13,006 Cr** [V deck].
   - Always use revenue from operations.
6. **"EBITDA" at Cochin includes interest income; screener's OP does not.**
   - Cochin's CFO: "EBITDA margin, normally we include the interest income also" [V transcript]. Cochin's FY26 EBITDA in its deck is **₹1,221 Cr (~24%)** vs screener OP **₹831 Cr (16.5%)**. Q1 FY27 "~24%" vs screener 18%.
   - MDL's deck EBITDA is also higher than screener's.
   - Use screener OP for cross-company tables, and say so.
7. **Cash: "cash & equivalents" vs "cash + bank balances" vs screener "other assets".**
   - Shipyards park customer advances in **bank term deposits** shown as "bank balances other than cash and cash equivalents".
   - FY26 annual reports [V]:
     - MDL consolidated: cash ₹2,731 Cr + other bank ₹10,365 Cr = **₹13,096 Cr**. ₹950 Cr of FDs are liened against an overdraft facility.
     - Cochin consolidated: ₹105 Cr + ₹2,301 Cr = **₹2,406 Cr**.
     - GRSE: ₹17 Cr + ₹3,494 Cr + ₹123 Cr (deposits >12 months) = **₹3,634 Cr**.
   - **Our Cochin primer's "~₹10,600 Cr of cash & investments" is screener's "Other assets" line** (₹10,667 Cr). That line includes inventories, contract assets and receivables. It is **wrong by ~4×** (see §3.4).
8. **Customer advances = "contract liabilities".**
   - The advance float is shown as **contract liabilities** (Ind AS 115). Cochin also has a separate small "advance received from customers" line (₹38 Cr).
   - Mar-26 vs Mar-25 [V AR]:
     - MDL ₹10,303 Cr vs ₹15,494 Cr.
     - Cochin consolidated ₹4,525 Cr vs ₹5,051 Cr (of which IAC works ₹271 Cr).
     - GRSE ₹5,751 Cr vs ₹6,900 Cr.
   - **The float shrank by ~₹6,870 Cr in one year** [E sum]. That is why all three had **negative operating cash flow in FY26** (MDL −₹2,654 Cr, Cochin −₹1,234 Cr, GRSE −₹290 Cr) [V screener].
9. **Margins are struck after "expected loss on onerous contracts" (MDL), warranty and LD provisions.** These swing reported OPM by several points.
   - **MDL FY26:** fixed-price contracts expected to overrun → total estimated loss **₹1,180 Cr**. Of this, ₹120 Cr was taken through revenue and **₹1,060 Cr held as a provision** (vs ₹521 Cr at Mar-25). The FY26 P&L charge for onerous contracts was **₹538 Cr** (FY25 ₹521 Cr) [V AR note 59 + provisions note].
   - **MDL FY25** included a **₹199 Cr reversal of liquidated damages** in other operating revenue [V AR].
   - **MDL's Q4 FY25 OPM of 3.8%** (screener) and the FY26 "margin compression" in our primer are largely these provisions, not a structural margin fall [E].
10. **"Pipeline" numbers are AoN or programme values, not the yard's share.**
    - P-17B's "₹70,000 Cr" is the DAC AoN for **seven frigates** [V GRSE transcript]. It will be split between yards (L1/L2), as on P-17A (MDL 4, GRSE 3).
    - NGC's ₹36,000 Cr AoN covers 8 ships; GRSE (L1) gets 5, "~₹25,000–33,000 Cr" [V-snip; V GRSE transcript "₹33,000 crore only for us"].
    - MCMV (12 ships, ₹36,000 Cr AoN) "will be split between two shipyards, eight and four" [V transcript].
    - **Never add a whole AoN to one yard's order book.**

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Three state-owned yards, one customer, one cash machine — and an order air-pocket.**
  - FY26 (straight sum, consolidated MDL/Cochin + standalone GRSE) [E from V screener]:
    - Revenue **₹25,030 Cr** (+17.4% YoY; 4-yr CAGR from FY22 ₹10,678 Cr = **+23.7%**).
    - Operating profit (screener OP, excl. other income) **₹3,894 Cr (15.6%)**.
    - Other income **₹1,822 Cr**, mostly interest on deposits.
    - PAT **₹4,043 Cr** (+7.3%).
  - **Other income was 35% of PBT** [E].
  - Market cap on 22-Oct-2026: **₹1,30,616 Cr** (MDL 59%, GRSE 17%, Cochin 23%). That is **32.3× FY26 PAT, 30.1× TTM PAT and 7.2× book** [E].
- **The 2023–25 rerating has been partly unwound.**
  - All three trade 33–41% below their 52-week highs (MDL ₹1,922 vs ₹2,883; Cochin ₹1,164 vs ₹1,864; GRSE ₹1,956 vs ₹3,339) [V screener].
  - Group m-cap is ~25% below our Jul-2026 primers (MDL ₹1.03 L Cr → ₹77,546 Cr; Cochin ₹39,600 → ₹30,649 Cr; GRSE ₹30,500 → ₹22,421 Cr).
  - Trailing P/Es are now 27× (MDL), 28× (GRSE) and 45× (Cochin), against ~40×/40×/55× in the primers.
- **Why the derating: execution delivered, orders did not.**
  - The order books are **shrinking**:
    - MDL ₹32,260 Cr (Mar-25) → ₹20,535 Cr (Mar-26) → ₹18,218 Cr (Jun-26) [V AR/deck].
    - GRSE ₹15,324 Cr (Mar-26) → ₹13,596 Cr (Jun-26). Its CMD: "first time in the last 10 years that our order book has dropped below ₹15,000 crore" [V].
    - Cochin ~₹21,100 Cr (Jun-25, primer) → ~₹18,900 Cr standalone (Mar-26) / ~₹21,900 Cr including repair and subsidiaries (Sep-26) [V].
  - The big naval awards have all slipped:
    - **P-75I**: six AIP submarines, ~₹70,000 Cr+; MDL with TKMS. Finance Ministry approval 29-May-2026, **still awaiting CCS** [V-snip]. It is absent from MDL's Jun-26 order book [V].
    - **3 additional Scorpenes**: ~₹36,000 Cr; described in press as "in limbo" [V-snip].
    - **NGC**: GRSE L1 since May-2025. "Delayed a bit"; signing was expected in Q2 FY27, and no signing had been seen by 22-Oct [V transcript; V screener announcements].
    - **P-17B**: RFP issued **16-Sep-2026**, award "late 2027" [V Shephard].
    - **IAC-2**: "has not reached the AoN stage" (Cochin CFO, 10-Sep-2026) [V].
- **The float is unwinding.**
  - Customer advances (contract liabilities) fell ~₹6,870 Cr in FY26 across the three; operating cash flow was negative at all three [V].
  - MDL's cash + bank is still **₹13,096 Cr**; GRSE ₹3,634 Cr; Cochin only **₹2,406 Cr**. Cochin has spent ~₹2,770 Cr on its new dry dock and ISRF and has raised borrowings to ₹1,672 Cr [V].
  - The treasury income that inflated PAT will shrink as advances are consumed, unless new contracts bring new advances [E].
- **Policy tailwind is real but slow and mostly commercial.**
  - The **₹69,725 Cr maritime package** (Cabinet, 24-Sep-2025) [V]:
    - SBFAS ₹24,736 Cr to 2036, incl. a ₹4,001 Cr shipbreaking credit note.
    - Maritime Development Fund ₹25,000 Cr (₹20,000 Cr investment fund, GoI 49%; ₹5,000 Cr interest-incentivisation fund).
    - Shipbuilding Development Scheme ₹19,989 Cr, which gives **25% capex support** for yard expansion (Cochin CFO).
  - The package targets **4.5 million GT/yr** of capacity. India is **<1%** of world shipbuilding against China's 63% of 2025 orders (CGT) [V].
  - The package mainly helps **commercial** shipbuilding: Cochin's European export book, CMA CGM LNG feeders, and the 432-vessel PSU demand aggregation. Naval margins are unaffected.
- **Margins: the Navy pays cost-plus-ish; repair pays more; interest pays the most.**
  - Cochin guides shipbuilding EBITDA at **10–12%**, repair **20–22%**, blended **~14%** (including interest) [V transcript].
  - GRSE's screener OPM is 8% in Q1 FY27 and 11% in FY26 [V].
  - MDL's 17% FY26 OPM came after a ₹538 Cr onerous-contract charge [V].
  - Post-tax interest income is roughly **29% of MDL's PAT, 25% of GRSE's and 17% of Cochin's** in FY26 [E: interest on deposits × (1−25%) ÷ PAT].

### 1.2 Value chain (inputs → customer)

1. **Requirement and approval (MoD):** Navy capability plan → DAC **AoN** (cost-ceiling set) → RFP to qualified yards → technical evaluation → commercial bids (L1) → CNC → CFA approval (CCS above ~₹3,000 Cr [K]) → contract signature → **advance payment** (typically 10–15% at signing [K]; GRSE/Cochin describe stage payments).
   - Nomination (no bid) was the norm for DPSUs until ~2020. NGC, NGOPV, ASW-SWC and P-17B are **competitive among Category-A yards** [V Shephard; V CSL "we are a category A shipyard"].
2. **Design:** Navy's Warship Design Bureau designs most surface combatants. Submarines and some platforms come from a foreign OEM under transfer of technology (Naval Group for Scorpene; TKMS Type-214-derived for P-75I) [V-snip]. Yards do detailed and production design.
   - First-year revenue on a new warship is only design: "**5–10% of project cost**" (GRSE CMD) [V].
3. **Procurement (the biggest cost):**
   - Steel (DMR-249A warship steel from SAIL) [K].
   - Propulsion (GE LM2500 gas turbines via HAL; MTU/Wärtsilä/Cummins diesels) [K].
   - Weapons and sensors: BrahMos, MF-STAR radar (IAI/BEL) [K]. **Many are "owner-furnished" or nominated** — GRSE: engines "are all nominated by the customers" [V].
   - MDL FY26: materials ₹5,700 Cr + base & depot spares ₹1,639 Cr = **56% of revenue** [V deck].
4. **Construction:** modular block fabrication → hull erection on slipway/dry dock → launch → outfitting at wet basin → harbour and sea trials → delivery → warranty (guarantee repairs provision).
5. **Revenue recognition:** percentage-of-completion (Ind AS 115, input/cost method) [V AR note 59]. Expected losses on fixed-price contracts are provided upfront (onerous contracts).
6. **After-sales:** refits, MRLC, spares, guarantee repairs; ship repair yards.

### 1.3 Where value is captured
- **In a naval contract, the yard is a systems integrator capturing a thin slice.**
  - Cochin: shipbuilding EBITDA **10–12%** (including some interest) [V].
  - GRSE: OPM 8–11% [V screener].
  - MDL: OPM 15–19% in FY24–26, but with heavy provisions [V].
  - Weapons, sensors and propulsion OEMs (BEL, BrahMos, HAL, foreign suppliers) capture much of the ship's value [E].
- **Indigenous content** has risen from **42% on P-15 (Delhi class) to 75% on P-17A** [V MDL deck]. GRSE claims "80% plus" overall on its warships [V]. More of the value now stays in India — but with vendors (often MSMEs; MDL's MSME procurement was 33.5% in FY26 [V]), not necessarily the yard.
- **Repair captures twice the margin of newbuild** (20–22% vs 10–12%, Cochin) [V].
- **Float income:** the customer pays ahead of cost, and the yard earns 6–7% on the deposits [E]. In FY26 interest on bank deposits was **MDL ₹1,007 Cr, GRSE ₹246 Cr, Cochin ₹164 Cr** [V AR].
- **Commercial export ships:** Cochin calls cash flow "tail-ended" — owners pay 60–70% at delivery, so working capital is positive. Margins are competitive and Cochin bears FX and fixed-price risk [V transcript].

## 4. Supply side and cost structure

### 4.1 Key inputs and their price drivers
- **Bought-out equipment and materials** dominate.
  - MDL FY26 consolidated cost lines as % of revenue [V deck; E %]:
    - Materials consumed **₹5,700 Cr (43.8%)**.
    - Base & depot spares **₹1,639 Cr (12.6%)**.
    - Employee **₹983 Cr (7.6%)**.
    - Other expenses (sub-contract, power & fuel, provisions) **₹2,419 Cr (18.6%)**.
    - Depreciation ₹97 Cr; finance cost ₹72 Cr.
  - Materials + spares rose from **~49.7% of revenue (FY25: 4,531 + 1,152 = 5,683 on 11,432)** to **56.4% (FY26)** [E from V deck]. This matches the primer's "material costs rose from ~50% to ~56%".
- **Steel:** warship-grade DMR-249A/B from SAIL. Hull steel is only ~10–15% of a warship's cost [K/E]. It matters more on commercial bulk ships.
- **Imported systems:** gas turbines, diesel engines, gearboxes, weapons, combat systems. Exposed to FX (₹94.9/$; ₹ has fallen ~8–10% in 12 months [E]) and to Western export licences. Fixed-price naval contracts usually have some FX/PVC (price variation) clauses [K]; exports are fixed-price in USD/EUR.
- **Labour:**
  - Regular manpower is small and increasingly fixed-term [V AR]:
    - MDL **5,770** (2,988 fixed-term).
    - Cochin **4,013** (1,882 on contract) + 1,301 trainees.
    - GRSE **1,595** on regular payroll.
  - Most production labour is sub-contracted. Sub-contracting sits in "other expenses" (MDL ₹2,419 Cr).
  - Revenue per regular employee: MDL **~₹2.25 Cr**, Cochin **~₹1.25 Cr**, GRSE **~₹4.4 Cr** [E]. GRSE's high figure reflects heavy outsourcing — its wage bill is not comparable.
- **Capex intensity:** low for MDL/GRSE historically (fixed assets ₹2,062 Cr and ₹576 Cr) [V screener]. Cochin is the outlier: fixed assets ₹3,164 Cr after the ₹1,800 Cr dry dock and ₹970 Cr ISRF [V].

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 Defence procurement
- **DAP 2020** (Defence Acquisition Procedure).
  - Categories, in order of preference: Buy (Indian-IDDM), Buy (Indian), Buy & Make (Indian), Buy & Make, Buy (Global) [K].
  - **Strategic Partnership (SP) model** for submarines → P-75I (MDL selected with TKMS; L&T–Navantia not compliant) [K/V-snip].
  - **DAP 2026** was in draft/finalisation through 2025–26 (shorter timelines, more private-sector access) [K — FLAG: final notification not verified this session].
- **Positive Indigenisation Lists (PILs):** MoD has issued five PILs for weapons/platforms and five for DPSU items [K]. MDL has submitted **1,024 items** to MoD for its PIL and has indigenised **55 of 174 submarine equipment items**; 29 project sanction orders issued [V MDL deck].
- **Defence Acquisition Council (DAC) AoNs** are the leading indicator. Examples:
  - P-17B ₹70,000 Cr AoN [V].
  - MCMV ₹36,000 Cr AoN [V].
  - NGC ₹36,000 Cr [V-snip].
  - LPD ~₹33,000–35,000 Cr [V].
- **Nomination vs competition:** since ~2020 most surface-ship contracts are L1-competitive among DPSUs (and sometimes private yards). Submarines (P-75I under SP) and carriers remain strategic.
- **Liquidated damages (LDs):** typically capped at **5–10% of contract value** [K]. They are levied for delay and often waived or reversed when delays are customer-caused. MDL reversed **₹199 Cr** of LD in FY25 [V AR] (P-75 delay history: six submarines due Dec-2017, commissioned by Jan-2025 [V PRS]).

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Revenue(t) = Σ over contracts [Contract value × Δ% completion(t)] + repair throughput × ₹/job**

**PAT(t) = [Revenue × contract margin − provisions (onerous/LD/warranty) + interest on (float + equity cash)] × (1 − 25% tax)**

where **float ≈ contract liabilities**, which **rise with new signatures** (advance 10–15% [K]) and **fall with execution**.

### C.1 Worked example — one P-17B-type frigate contract won by a DPSU yard [E composite]
- Assume the yard wins **2 frigates at ₹10,000 Cr total** (₹70,000 Cr AoN ÷ 7 ships ≈ ₹10,000 Cr each; yard gets 2 of 7 as L2 — illustrative).
- Duration 7 years. Recognition profile (GRSE curve):
  - Year 1: 7% → ₹700 Cr.
  - Years 2–3: +18% cumulative → ₹900 Cr a year.
  - Years 4–6: +65% → ~₹2,170 Cr a year.
  - Year 7: +10% → ₹1,000 Cr.
- Contract EBITDA margin 10% (fixed-price L1) → ₹1,000 Cr over 7 years, ~₹143 Cr a year on average.
- **Advance 10% = ₹1,000 Cr at signing**, held for ~3–4 years at 6.9% → **~₹69 Cr a year of interest**, ≈50% of the average contract EBITDA in years 1–3.
- If costs overrun by 8% (₹740 Cr on ₹9,000 Cr of cost), the yard books an **onerous-contract provision** for the expected loss and the margin turns to −1.5%. This is the MDL FY26 pattern (₹1,180 Cr expected loss across "certain ongoing projects") [V AR].

### C.2 Worked example — MDL FY26 reconciliation [E from V]
- Revenue ₹13,006 Cr × OPM 17.4% = **OP ₹2,268 Cr** (after ~₹538 Cr onerous-contract charge, inside "other expenses").
- + Other income ₹1,139 Cr (of which interest on deposits ₹1,007 Cr).
- − Depreciation ₹97 Cr − interest ₹72 Cr (screener ₹74 Cr) = **PBT ₹3,237 Cr**.
- × (1 − 25%) = **PAT ₹2,578 Cr**.
- Ex-interest PAT ≈ (3,237 − 1,007) × 0.75 = **₹1,673 Cr** → **ex-cash P/E ≈ (77,546 − 13,096 + 447) / 1,673 ≈ 38.8×** vs headline 30.1× on FY26 PAT [E].

### C.3 Worked example — Cochin blended margin (management's own arithmetic) [V transcript; E]
- Shipbuilding ~70% of revenue × 10–12% + repair ~30% × 20–22% = **13–14.5% blended EBITDA (including interest)**.
- FY27 revenue guidance: +12% (12–15%) on ₹5,022 Cr → **~₹5,625–5,775 Cr**. At 14% → **EBITDA ~₹790–810 Cr**, against FY26 company EBITDA ₹1,221 Cr. **A guided ~35% fall in company-basis EBITDA**: FY26 itself had higher-margin carrier refits; check the basis [E — FLAG to the writer: CSL's FY26 "EBITDA ₹1,221 Cr" includes ₹409 Cr other income; on the same basis 14% of ~₹5,700 Cr = ₹800 Cr].
- TTM PAT ₹680 Cr on m-cap ₹30,649 Cr = 45× [V screener].

### C.4 Worked example — GRSE Q1 FY27 [V transcript/screener]
- Revenue ₹1,815 Cr (+39%) × OPM 8.2% = OP ₹149 Cr; + OI ₹100 Cr; − D&A ₹14 Cr − interest ₹4 Cr = PBT ₹232 Cr → PAT ₹173 Cr (+44%).
