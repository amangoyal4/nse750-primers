## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **"Home Care" means four different things in this file.**
   - **This report's sub-sector (and the NSE "Household Products" bucket):** fabric care (detergent powders, bars, liquids, post-wash whiteners and conditioners), dishwash, household insecticides, surface/toilet cleaners, air fresheners.
   - **HUL "Home Care" segment:** Fabric Wash (Surf excel, Rin, Wheel, Comfort) + Household Care (Vim, Domex, Sun liquid, Cif, floor cleaners). It has **no insecticides and no air fresheners** [V HUL AR 2025-26].
   - **GCPL "Home Care" (standalone):** household insecticides (Good Knight, HIT), air fresheners (aer), fabric care (Godrej Fab liquid detergent, Godrej Zap), Godrej Spic (dishwash/home cleaning) and Godrej Rizz (liquid dishwash, launched Q2 FY27). It has **almost no detergent powder or bars** [V GCPL Q2 FY27 PR].
   - **Jyothy "Home Care" (new segment from Q1 FY27):** dishwash + HI + incense sticks/sambrani + floor/toilet cleaners. **Fabric care is a SEPARATE Jyothy segment** [V]. To compare Jyothy with HUL Home Care, use **Jyothy Fabric Care + Home Care** (FY26 ₹2,624 Cr, 89% of sales).
2. **Jyothy's FY26 numbers include Pril and Fa; FY27 does not (from 1-Jun-2026).** Management now quotes growth "excluding Pril and Fa". Q1 FY27 reported revenue was +3.0%; ex-Pril/Fa it was +8.1% value and +5.3% volume [V]. Every FY27 comparison needs one of two labels: reported, or ex-Pril/Fa.
3. **Jyothy's "Dishwashing" no longer exists as a reported segment.** FY26 Dishwashing was ₹959 Cr with a ₹151 Cr result (old structure) [V]. The new Home Care segment adds HI and incense/cleaners (₹1,215 Cr / ₹150 Cr result in FY26) [V]. From Q1 FY27 the dishwash number is not reported separately.
4. **Jyothy is NOT the dishwash leader.** The company says **Exo is the #2 brand in dishwash bars** and Jyothy is #2 in dishwash "in value terms" [V Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 presentations; AR 2025-26]. HUL's Vim is the leader [K]. Exo bar ~14% share and Pril liquid ~13% share [V-sec Finshots]; screener's profile says 13.7% [V screener]. **Our primer calls Jyothy the dishwash "leader … ahead of HUL". That is wrong** (Part 3.4).
5. **Gross margin vs EBITDA.** Jyothy reports gross margin, A&P and "operating EBITDA" in its presentations; use those [V]. Screener OPM is close but not identical (FY26 screener OP ₹450 Cr = company ₹449.9 Cr). **Never use 100 − screener material cost % as the gross margin** for Jyothy: screener's "expenses" line does not split materials.
6. **Jyothy: standalone = consolidated from FY26.** The consolidated screener page stops at Mar-2025 and shows a stale P/E of 18.8; the standalone page carries FY26 and TTM (P/E 24.3) [V screener]. The laundry-services subsidiary, **Jyothy Fabricare Services Ltd (JFSL), was amalgamated** — the AR shows an "Amalgamation Adjustment Deficit Account" for JFSL [V AR 2025-26]. FY24/FY25 company presentations are labelled "consolidated" (FY25 ₹2,847 Cr) while screener standalone shows ₹2,844 Cr. The difference is immaterial; this file uses the standalone FY22–FY26 series.
7. **HUL FY26 has a discontinued operation.** The ice-cream business (Kwality Wall's) was demerged in FY26. Continuing segment revenue is **₹64,468 Cr**; discontinued revenue ₹1,257 Cr; profit from discontinued operations **₹4,407 Cr** (mostly the demerger gain). Reported PAT is ₹15,040 Cr vs continuing ₹10,652 Cr [V HUL AR]. Screener's FY26 PAT of ₹15,059 Cr and its Dec-2025 quarter PAT of ₹6,603 Cr include that gain. Our HUL primer quotes "turnover ₹63,763 Cr", which is a different line (turnover excluding other operating income) [primer HUL; K].
8. **"Price cuts" in home care are often invisible.** Grammage increases (more grams at the same MRP), price-offs and bonus packs reduce realisation without changing the MRP. Jyothy's FY26 dishwash: **+6% volume, −1.3% value** [V Q4 call]. When a company says "volume growth", ask what happened to realisation per kg.
9. **Volume growth is company-defined.** Jyothy and HUL report "volume" or UVG on internal tonnage or mix-adjusted bases. They are not comparable to NielsenIQ consumer-panel volumes. NIQ shows HPC volume **−4.3% in Apr–Jun 2026**, while Jyothy (+5.3% ex-Pril), HUL (UVG 5–7%) and GCPL (UVG 7–8%) report growth [V-sec Daily Pioneer; V]. Sell-in vs offtake, the organised-vs-unorganised mix and channel inventory explain part of the gap. Do not reconcile these naively.
10. **Household insecticides: the legal category is smaller than the market.** Registered HI products (coils, LVs, aerosols, CIB&RC-approved incense sticks) pay **18% GST**. Unregistered mosquito incense sticks, often laced with unapproved molecules such as dimefluthrin or meperfluthrin, are sold under ordinary agarbatti codes at **5%** [V-sec The Week, citing the EY–HICA study, Apr-2026; V-snip state crackdowns]. "HI category decline" in company commentary often means **share loss to illegal sticks**, not lower mosquito-control spending.

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Indian home care is a ~₹75,000–85,000 Cr (manufacturer-value, ex-GST) industry growing roughly with nominal consumption, dominated by HUL, and Jyothy is a ~3.5% slice of it** [E composite, §2].
  - HUL Home Care alone was **₹23,672 Cr in FY26** (EBIT ₹4,521 Cr, **19.1%** margin) [V HUL AR].
  - The unlisted heavyweights are large: **RSPL (Ghadi) ₹5,793 Cr revenue in FY24** (EBITDA 19.2%) [V CRISIL]; Nirma, P&G Home Products (Tide/Ariel), Reckitt India (Harpic, Lizol, Mortein) have no verified current revenue.
  - Category sizes, triangulated: **HI ₹8,138 Cr (2025)** [V-sec EY–HICA]; **liquid dishwash ~₹3,000 Cr** at mid-teens household penetration [V GCPL Q2 FY27]; total dishwash ~₹7,000–8,500 Cr [E]; fabric care ~₹45,000–52,000 Cr [E].
  - Jyothy FY26: **₹2,944 Cr revenue, of which ₹2,624 Cr (89%) is fabric + home care** [V].
- **2026 delivered the worst input-cost shock in the industry's recent memory, and the small specialist took it hardest.**
  - The West Asia war (from 28-Feb-2026) pushed crude past $100. **LABSA (the main surfactant) rose 60–65% from January to early May; HDPE/PP packaging (15–20% of purchases) rose 50–55% in March–April** (Jyothy CFO, 4-May-2026) [V Q4 call].
  - Jyothy's raw-material basket inflation was "30–35%", worth **15–16 points of gross margin before pricing**. It took only ~3% price in Q1 (4–4.5% cumulative) [V Q1 call].
  - **Jyothy Q1 FY27: gross margin 38.5% (vs 48.0%), EBITDA margin 8.4% (vs 16.5%), PAT −51%** [V].
  - HUL and GCPL took ~5% price in Q1 [V Q1 call, analyst question]. Their diversified portfolios kept consolidated margins near 19–23% [V screener]. **HUL's Home Care segment margin fell to ~16% in Q2 FY27 from 19.1% in FY26** [V-sec Univest; V AR] — even the leader felt it.
- **Volume is not the problem; realisation is.**
  - Jyothy grew volumes 6% in FY26, while value grew 3.5% [V].
  - HUL Home Care ran **high-single-digit UVG with USG of only 4% in FY26** (price cuts during the deflationary first half), then **USG +14% in Q1 FY27 and +22% in Q2 FY27** as pricing came back [V HUL AR; V-snip/V-sec].
  - GCPL Home Care **+19%** in Q2 FY27 [V]. The giants are now growing home care fastest, through price plus liquids. Jyothy's Q2 (due 4-Nov) is the test of whether a specialist can do the same.
- **The category map is shifting under everyone's feet.**
  - **Powders → liquids** in laundry: Jyothy liquids "nearly doubled" in FY26 [V Q4 call]. Liquids are now priced **below powders per wash** after the price war [V Q1 call, CMD].
  - **Bars → liquids** in dishwash: GCPL calls liquid dishwash "~₹3k crore, growing double-digit, mid-teens penetration" [V].
  - **Coils → LVs and incense sticks** in HI: Jyothy's LV share of HI rose to ~55% from 50%; coils keep declining [V Q4 call]. Goodknight sticks are now "the largest branded stick" [V GCPL].
  - **GT → MT/e-com/QC**: Jyothy's MT + e-com + QC grew **26% in FY26** and e-com/QC grew **25–30%** in Q1 FY27 [V].
- **Policy helped personal care, not home care.**
  - GST 2.0 (effective **22-Sep-2025**) cut soap, toothpaste, shampoo and hair oil from 18% to **5%**. **Detergents and household insecticides stayed at 18%** — "inadvertently left out", "surprising" (Deloitte, Nuvama) [V-sec Upstox].
  - HI makers (HICA, with EY) are lobbying for 5% and a dedicated HSN code (3808 91 91) [V-sec The Week].
- **Rural is a 2026 risk again.** The 2026 south-west monsoon ended **~12.6% below LPA (759.4 mm vs 868.6 mm), the weakest since 2015** [V-snip superkalam]. GCPL cites "subdued/weak monsoon … intensifying El-Niño" [V]. NIQ rural FMCG volume fell **−5% in Apr–Jun 2026** [V-sec Daily Pioneer]. Jyothy said "rural resilient, urban subdued" in Q1 [V]. These views conflict (trap 9).
- **Jyothy: a cash-rich, family-controlled specialist whose valuation has already de-rated.**
  - **₹6,897 Cr m-cap at ₹188** (52-week range ₹171–319; >55% below its all-time high per Finshots) [V screener; V-sec].
  - TTM P/E **24.3×** on depressed TTM PAT of ₹284 Cr. ~**13.4× FY26 EBITDA / 15.5× TTM EBITDA on net cash of ~₹850 Cr** [E].
  - Peers: HUL 40.8×, GCPL 39.8×, Dabur 33.9× P/E [V screener 3-Nov-26].
  - Promoter **62.89%** [V].

### 1.2 Value chain (inputs → shelf)
1. **Petrochemical surfactants.** Kerosene-derived n-paraffin + benzene → **LAB** (Reliance, Indian Oil, Tamil Nadu Petroproducts; plus imports from West Asia) [K] → sulphonated to **LABSA** by detergent makers or job-workers → mixed into powders, bars, liquids and dishwash.
   - LAB/LABSA prices track **benzene and kerosene, and therefore crude**, with a 1–3 month lag [K; V Q4 call].
   - Second surfactant family: **SLES/AOS** (ethoxylates from ethylene oxide, or palm-derived fatty alcohols) for liquids and shampoos [K].
2. **Inorganic builders and fillers.** **Soda ash** (Tata Chemicals, GHCL, Nirma, RSPL, DCW): ~15–18% of Indian soda ash demand goes to detergents [V-sib]. Plus sodium sulphate (filler), sodium silicate, zeolite/STPP, calcite (bars).
   - Soda ash has been in a deep downturn: China light grade ~RMB 1,156/t in Jun-2026, Indian MIP ₹20,108/t, a DGTR safeguard probe due ~16-Nov-2026 [V-sib].
   - **Nirma and RSPL are backward-integrated into soda ash** (RSPL 0.5 mtpa at Dwarka, expanding by 700 TPD) [V CRISIL; V-sib]. That is a structural cost edge in economy powders.
3. **Oleochemicals.** Palm oil / palm kernel oil → fatty acids → **soap noodles** (bars, personal care) and fatty alcohols (SLES) [K]. Jyothy flagged soap-noodle inflation "from June onwards" [V Q1 call]. GCPL called its palm/crude inflation impact "approximately high-single-digit" [V].
4. **Actives for HI.** Pyrethroids (transfluthrin, prallethrin, allethrin, cypermethrin), mostly imported or made by agrochemical firms; each formulation needs **CIB&RC registration under the Insecticides Act, 1968** (multi-year safety data) [K; V-snip]. GCPL owns **Renofluthrin (RNF)**, a patented in-house molecule [primer GCPL].
5. **Packaging.** HDPE/PP bottles, laminates/pouches (multi-layer plastic), cartons. **15–20% of Jyothy's material purchases** [V Q4 call]. Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for plastic packaging applies [K].
6. **Manufacturing.** Low capital intensity. Jyothy runs **23 plants** with **2,970 permanent employees** (Mar-2026) [V PPT; V AR]. Third-party conversion ("conversion charges") is common.
7. **Distribution.** Company → C&F agents → **distributors (10,000+ channel partners for Jyothy)** → wholesalers/retailers. Jyothy: **~4 million outlets reached, ~1.4 million direct** (up ~100k in FY26); HUL: **9 million+ outlets** [V Jyothy PPT/AR; V HUL AR].
   - Much of Jyothy's GT business runs on **advance payment**; distributor stock is 15–20 days [V Q1 call].
   - MT, e-commerce and QC (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart) are the growth channels.
8. **Consumer.** Repeat purchase every 2–6 weeks, highly price/pack sensitive; LUPs dominate rural and lower-income baskets.

### 1.3 Where value is captured
- **Brand owners capture the spread between commodity inputs and branded MRP.** Jyothy's gross margin moved between **38.5% and 51.3%** in eight quarters [V]. HUL Home Care EBIT margin is ~16–19% [V].
- **Backward-integrated economy players (RSPL, Nirma)** capture value in soda ash and bulk scale: RSPL EBITDA 18–19% on ₹5.8–6.7k Cr revenue [V CRISIL]. That is comparable to HUL Home Care, from the opposite end of the price ladder.
- **Retailers and channels:** GT margins ~8–12%, distributor ~4–6%, MT/QC margins and listing fees higher [K/E]. QC is extracting more trade spend [V Jyothy AR: "tension with traditional retailers"].
- **Licensors:** Henkel was paid a **royalty of ₹4.57 Cr in FY26 (₹4.62 Cr FY25)** for Pril/Fa/Henko-related licences [V AR, Note: Royalty 457 lakh]. That is a tiny cash cost; the value lost with Pril is the brand franchise, not the royalty.

## 3. Demand drivers and end-markets

1. **Household formation and per-capita consumption.** Home care is a consumption staple with ~100% penetration for laundry and dishwash cleaning agents (branded or unbranded) and ~92–99% urban / 64–73% rural penetration for mosquito control [V-sec EY–HICA]. Growth comes from **upgrading formats and per-capita usage**, not first-time users.
2. **Washing-machine penetration → liquids and "matic" powders.** Rising semi/fully-automatic machine ownership [K] drives HUL's "rapid format upgradation to liquids" and Jyothy's liquids doubling [V HUL AR; V Q4 call]. Per-kg prices are higher, but per-wash liquids are now priced **below powders** after promotions [V Q1 call].
3. **Dishwash: bar → liquid.** Liquid dishwash ~₹3,000 Cr at mid-teens penetration, growing double-digit [V GCPL]. Each household converting raises spend per household [E].
4. **Mosquito pressure and disease.** Weather drives HI. Extended summer and delayed rains hurt HI in Q1 FY27 [V Jyothy]. GCPL gained share "despite weak monsoon"; Indonesia benefited from El-Niño-driven infestation [V]. Dengue/malaria seasons (Jul–Oct) are the HI peak [K].
5. **Rural incomes.** The monsoon, MSP and government transfers matter because economy detergent bars and powders, Exo bars and coils are rural-heavy.
   - 2026 monsoon **−12.6% vs LPA** [V-snip].
   - NIQ: rural FMCG **volume −5%, value −2.9% (Apr–Jun 2026)**; HPC value +1%, volume −4.3%, price +5.4% [V-sec].
   - Earlier NIQ (calendar 2025): urban volume +4.6%, rural +3.6%, total +4.1% (vs 4.7% in 2024) [V Jyothy AR citing ET/NIQ].
   - JFM-2026 NIQ deck: all-India volume +0.9% [V-snip].
6. **Affordability: GST and LUPs.** GST 2.0 lowered soap/personal-care prices, not detergents. Companies used grammage and price-point packs (₹5/₹10) [V-sec; V Q4 call]. Price points matter more than per-kg prices for half of India.
7. **Channel shift.**
   - MT +17.5% and e-com +34.8% volume in Apr–Jun 2026 (NIQ, as reported) [V-sec].
   - Jyothy: MT/e-com/QC +26% (FY26), e-com/QC +25–30% (Q1 FY27); "home care categories are not growing at the same pace as food and grocery on these platforms" [V].
   - Quick-commerce TAM: US$57 bn by 2030 (industry estimate in Jyothy AR) [V AR].
8. **Hygiene awareness.** Post-COVID disinfectant/floor-cleaner usage gave Reckitt and HUL Domex a step-up [K].

**End-market split of Jyothy's revenue (FY26, old structure) [V]:** Fabric Care 45.7% · Dishwashing 32.6% · Personal Care 10.9% · HI 6.7% · Others 4.1%. **Q1 FY27 (new structure):** Fabric Care 52% · Home Care 36% · Personal Care 12% [V].

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 GST (the biggest live policy variable)
- **GST 2.0, effective 22-Sep-2025** (56th GST Council; announced 3-Sep-2025; notifications 9/2025-CT(R) to 17/2025-CT(R), 17-Sep-2025) [V-sec Upstox; V-snip Taxguru/Vyapar]:
  - **Cut 18% → 5%:** toilet soap bars, shampoo, hair oil, toothpaste, toothbrushes, face powder [V-sec Upstox].
  - **Unchanged at 18%:** **detergents (HSN 3402: powders, liquids, washing preparations)**, **household insecticides (HSN 3808)**, hair dye, skin care, cosmetics [V-sec Upstox, citing Nuvama; V-snip Busy.in for 3402].
  - **Ambiguous:** laundry soap bars and dishwash bars. Soap (HSN 3401) moved to 5%; synthetic-detergent bars/dishwash cakes are usually classified 3402 (18%) [E/K — FLAG; confirm against the CBIC schedule]. Jyothy's FY26 commentary implies the GST cut helped **Personal Care** (Margo) and not detergents [V Q4 call: "GST rate cuts also helped improve demand particularly in Personal Care"; an analyst noted "detergents did not have that"].
  - **Agarbatti 5%** vs registered HI 18% gives illegal mosquito sticks a ~13-point tax advantage [V-sec The Week].
  - Industry ask: HI to 5% with a dedicated HSN (3808 91 91) (EY–HICA, Apr-2026) [V-sec].
- **History [K]:** at GST launch (1-Jul-2017) detergents were placed at 28%, then cut to 18% in Nov-2017 (23rd GST Council). Pre-GST, excise + VAT on detergents was ~25–27% [K — FLAG, verify].
- **Transition disruption:** "nearly 60% of the FMCG portfolio underwent GST rate revisions" (Jyothy AR). Trade destocking and repricing hit Q2–Q3 FY26 (NIQ called it a "GST transition" dip) [V AR; V-snip].

### 7.2 Household insecticides
- **Insecticides Act, 1968 / Insecticides Rules, 1971:** every molecule and formulation needs **CIB&RC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee) registration** [K]. Illegal sticks use unregistered molecules (dimefluthrin, meperfluthrin) and bypass the "mandatory three-year safety testing" [V-snip Gujarat Samachar].
- **2025–2026 enforcement:** Maharashtra acted against the 'Comfort' brand; ₹69 lakh of 'Sleepwell' sticks were seized in Vijayawada (Nov-2025) [V-snip]. Enforcement is state-led and episodic.
- A Pesticide Management Bill (to replace the 1968 Act) has been pending for years [K — FLAG].

### 7.3 Other rules
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules:** MRP, net quantity, unit sale price per kg/L (mandatory since 2022 [K]). This makes grammage games visible on pack but not to shoppers' eyes.
- **Plastic Waste Management Rules / EPR (2022 onward):** brand owners must collect/recycle and use recycled content in rigid plastics [K]. HUL reports progress on plastic neutrality [V AR mentions]. This is a cost line for pouch-heavy home care.
- **BIS standards** for detergents (IS 4955 etc.) are voluntary/partly mandatory [K — FLAG].
- **Environmental:** phosphate (STPP) limits are being debated; LAB is biodegradable vs older branched ABS [K].
- **No price controls** and no PLI scheme for home care [K].
- **Trade remedies on inputs:** soda ash MIP (₹20,108/t) and a safeguard probe; anti-dumping history on LAB [V-sib; K]. Duties on inputs raise detergent makers' costs.

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

- **1959–1990s: Surf vs Nirma.** HUL launched Surf (1959) [K]. Karsanbhai Patel's **Nirma** (1969) created the economy powder market at a fraction of Surf's price [K]. HUL answered with **Wheel (1987–88)**, the classic "fighting brand" case study [K].
- **1983: Ujala** is founded by M.P. Ramachandran in Thrissur with ~₹5,000 [V AR/PPT]. Fabric whitener becomes a branded category. **Ghadi** (RSPL, Kanpur, launched 1987 [K]) builds a North/Central economy franchise.
- **1990s–2000s:** P&G enters with Ariel (1991) and Tide (2000) [K]. The 2004 HUL–P&G detergent price war cut Wheel/Tide prices sharply [K]. Jyothy adds Maxo (coils) and Exo (dishwash bar, ~2000s) [primer].
- **2007:** Jyothy Laboratories lists (BSE 532926) [primer; K].
- **2011: Henkel India.** Jyothy buys **50.97% of Henkel India for ₹118.7 Cr** (plus an open offer and assumed liabilities) [V-sec Finshots]. Henkel India is merged into Jyothy, bringing Henko, Mr. White (lifetime licences), Pril and Fa (15-year licences from 31-May-2011), and Margo, Neem and Chek (owned) [V-sec; V AR]. Goodwill of ₹100.38 Cr from the Henkel acquisition remains on the balance sheet [V AR: "Goodwill of ₹10,038 lakhs"].
- **2012–2017:** Jyothy deleverages (borrowings ₹534 Cr in FY14 → ₹97 Cr FY21 [V screener]). The company is renamed Jyothy Labs. Bonus 1:1 in 2018–19 (equity capital ₹18 → ₹37 Cr) [V screener].
- **2017:** GST launches (detergents 28% → 18% in Nov-2017) [K].
- **2020–21:** COVID. Hygiene categories spike (Lizol, Domex, Dettol); Jyothy's FY21 EBITDA reaches 16.8% [V].
- **2021–22: commodity super-spike.** Crude, palm and LAB rise. Jyothy OPM falls to **11% (FY22)**; GM ~42% (FY23 42.3%) [V].
- **FY24: disinflation windfall.** Jyothy GM 49.1%, EBITDA 17.4%, PAT +54% to ₹369 Cr [V]. Peak share price (~₹595 in 2024 [K]).
- **FY25–FY26: price war.** Deflationary commodities let leaders cut prices and add grammage. HUL Home Care USG falls to 4% in FY26 despite high-single-digit UVG [V AR]. Jyothy dishwash value −1.3% on +6% volume [V].
- **22-Sep-2025: GST 2.0** — personal care cut, detergents/HI not [V-sec].
- **28-Feb-2026: West Asia war.** Crude above $100, LABSA +60–65%, packaging +50–55% [V].
- **9-May-2026:** Henkel does not renew Pril/Fa; exit 31-May-2026; Jyothy litigates [V].
- **Jul-2026:** Jyothy launches Maxo incense sticks (CIB&RC-approved, transfluthrin) [V].
- **Q1 FY27:** Jyothy EBITDA 8.4% [V]; HUL Home Care USG +14% [V-snip].
- **Q2 FY27:** HUL Home Care +22%, GCPL Home Care +19%; 2026 monsoon deficient [V/V-sec/V-snip]. **4-Nov-2026: Jyothy Q2 + buyback board** [V].
- **Cycle pattern:** Jyothy's EBITDA margin follows crude-linked input costs with a 1–2 quarter lag: **16.8% (FY21) → 11.3% (FY22) → 12.7% (FY23) → 17.4% (FY24) → 17.6% (FY25) → 15.3% (FY26) → 8.4% (Q1 FY27)** [V]. Trough-to-peak swings are 6–9 points, about 2–3× HUL Home Care's swing [E].

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert leads with)

1. **Crude is the hidden cost of a "brands" business.** Fifty to sixty per cent of a home-care maker's inputs (LABSA, packaging, solvents) are crude-linked. The 2026 West Asia shock (LABSA +60–65%, packaging +50–55%) took Jyothy's gross margin from 48% to 38.5% in a year [V]. Brand strength decides how much of a shock can be passed on. Portfolio breadth decides how much of the P&L is exposed. Jyothy is ~90% exposed; HUL ~37% [V; primer HUL].
2. **Price leadership belongs to HUL (and, in economy powders, Ghadi/Nirma); everyone else follows.**
   - In deflation (FY25–H1 FY26) the leaders cut prices and added grammage, compressing followers' realisation: Jyothy dishwash −1.3% value on +6% volume [V].
   - In inflation (H1 FY27) followers must wait for the leader to price up. Jyothy took ~3% vs HUL/GCPL ~5% in Q1, "at par with competition SKU to SKU" [V].
   - The CMD: "the moment JLL … launches … there is enough big competition who brings down their prices" [V Q1 call].
3. **Format migration is the growth engine (powder → liquid, bar → liquid, coil → LV/sticks).** Liquids doubled at Jyothy in FY26. Liquid dishwash is ~₹3k Cr at mid-teens penetration. LV is 55% of Maxo [V]. The prize: higher value per household. The catch: liquids are a price-war zone ("liquid detergents are now lower than powders in terms of pricing … and margin") [V Q1 call].
4. **Distribution and channel are moving from GT reach to omnichannel salience.** Direct reach (Jyothy 1.4 mn, HUL 9 mn+) still decides rural availability. MT/e-com/QC (+26% at Jyothy) decide urban premium adoption [V]. A mid-cap must fund both.
5. **Category legality and policy (HI-specific).** Illegal incense sticks with unapproved molecules undercut registered coils on both price and tax (5% vs 18% GST). Branded players respond with approved sticks (GCPL, then Jyothy in Jul-2026) and lobby for a GST cut [V/V-sec].

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Revenue = Σ categories [households reached × penetration × usage (kg or units per household per year) × realisation per kg]**, where realisation = MRP × (1 − GST) × (1 − trade margin) × (1 − promotions/grammage).

**EBITDA = Revenue × gross margin − A&P − staff − other overheads.** Gross margin = 1 − (input basket cost per kg ÷ net realisation per kg).

### C.1 Worked example: Jyothy Labs FY26 vs Q1 FY27 annualised [V inputs; E arithmetic]

| Line (₹ Cr) | FY26 | Q1 FY27 × 4 (run-rate) | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 2,944 | 3,092 | Q1 includes 2 months of Pril/Fa |
| Gross profit | 1,384 (47.0%) | 1,190 (38.5%) | −9.5 pts |
| A&P | 228 (7.7%) | 202 (6.5%) | Cut |
| Staff (~11.5%) | ~340 | ~352 | [V Q1 11.4%] |
| Other expenses (~12.4%) | ~366 | ~377 | [V Q1 12.2%] |
| **Operating EBITDA** | **450 (15.3%)** | **259 (8.4%)** | [V] |
| D&A | 61 | ~64 | [V screener] |
| Other income | 67 | ~72 | Treasury on ~₹850–1,000 Cr |
| PBT | 451 | ~262 | |
| PAT (~74%) | 333 | ~190 | Q1 × 4 = 190 [V Q1 PAT 47.6] |

**Sensitivity [E]:** each **1 pt of gross margin ≈ ₹30 Cr EBITDA ≈ ₹22 Cr PAT ≈ ₹0.6 EPS** (on ~₹3,000 Cr sales, 36.7 Cr shares). Returning from 38.5% to the 47% FY26 level adds ~₹255 Cr EBITDA.

### C.2 Worked example: one 1-kg pack of mid-tier detergent powder [E composite]
- **MRP ₹120/kg.**
  - Less GST 18% → ₹101.7 ex-GST.
  - Less retailer margin ~10% and distributor ~5% → ~₹87 net realisation to the company.
  - Less promotions/grammage ~5% → **~₹83**.
- **Inputs (pre-shock):**
  - LABSA ~15–20% of formula at ~₹110–130/kg → ~₹20.
  - Soda ash ~25–30% at ~₹25–30/kg → ~₹8.
  - Sodium sulphate/fillers ~40% at ~₹8–10/kg → ~₹4.
  - Enzymes/brighteners/perfume → ~₹4–6.
  - Packaging → ~₹4–5.
  - **Total materials ≈ ₹40–43 → gross margin ~48–52%.**
- **Shock (LABSA +60%, packaging +50%):** +₹12 + ₹2 → materials ₹54–57 → **gross margin ~32–35% at an unchanged price.** Holding the rupee gross profit per kg (~₹41.5) needs a ~₹14 (≈17%) net price increase; restoring the 50% margin would need ~34%. In practice the industry took ~4–5% [V]. This is why Q1 FY27 gross margins collapsed.

### C.3 Worked example: household insecticides, coil vs LV [E composite]
- **Coil:** pack of 10 at ~₹35 MRP → ~₹26 net to the company. Low gross margin (~35–40%); price-attacked by illegal sticks; Jyothy calls coils "margin dilutive" [V Q4 call].
- **LV:** machine + refill (₹80–90 MRP, refill 45 nights) → recurring refill revenue, gross margin ~55–60% [E/K].
- Mix shift: Maxo LV 50% → 55% of HI moved the segment from a −₹25 Cr loss (FY25) to −₹5 Cr (FY26), +₹4 Cr in Q4 [V].

### C.4 Reference: HUL Home Care FY26 [V]
- Revenue ₹23,672 Cr × EBIT 19.1% = ₹4,521 Cr. Implied EBIT per outlet (9 mn outlets) ≈ ₹5,000 a year [E].
- Jyothy FY26 segment result ₹414 Cr on 1.4 mn direct outlets ≈ ₹3,000 per direct outlet [E].

## 4.5 New insights the first pass missed

1. **The economy end of laundry is in turmoil, and that explains the "volume boom" in branded powders and bars.**
   - RSPL (Ghadi) lost 21.6% of revenue in FY25 after a product recall and revamp. Its margin fell from 19.2% to 13.1%, CRISIL moved it to Negative outlook, and it still had no liquid detergent.
   - Nirma is demerging its consumer and chemicals undertakings into a private vehicle, Ocular, while its US soda-ash arm sits in Chapter 11.
   - Over the same quarters, HUL reported bars and powders "sustained their step-up", and Jyothy's Fabric Care posted +17.8% volume (Q4 FY26) and +10% (Q1 FY27), with powders and bars at double digits.
   - **Part of the branded volume growth is share taken from a wounded economy leader, not category growth.** Expect RSPL's recovery (CRISIL expects 5–8% growth from FY27) to cap this tailwind.
2. **Value moved upstream in 2026.** The domestic LAB maker TPL's EBITDA margin rose from 11.2% to 15.9% in the same quarter Jyothy's fell from 16.5% to 8.4%. Nirma, which owns both LAB and soda ash, sits on both sides of that spread. For a formulator, "brand" did not protect against a supplier squeeze in a shortage. This is the cleanest data point for the "commodity or brand?" debate the CMD fought on the call.
3. **Jyothy's dishwash margin halved before the war.** The dishwash segment margin went 20.2% → 17.2% → 14.5% → 10.2% through FY26, all before LABSA spiked. The AR admits "calibrated MRP adjustments to ensure price parity". The 2026 cost shock landed on a category already in a price war with HUL Vim, and now GCPL (Spic, Rizz) is entering too. **This, more than Pril, is why the Home Care segment earned 2.9% in Q1 FY27.**
4. **The leader is buying volume with margin.** HUL Home Care: margin 19.1% (FY26) → 17% (Q1 FY27) → 16% (Q2 FY27) while USG went 4% → 14% → 22%, with explicit "market development" and "calibrated pricing" in liquids. When the leader runs a share strategy, a price-follower's H2 margin recovery depends on HUL's pricing as much as on crude. **Jyothy's "H2 substantially better" guidance needs that caveat.**
5. **The South got the worst of the 2026 monsoon.** South Peninsula rainfall was 76% of LPA, the 4th lowest since 1901, against 87% all-India. Jyothy is South-skewed: Ujala Detergent is the largest brand by volume in Kerala, and Keerthy Suresh fronts the southern Exo campaign. Jyothy said "rural resilient" in Q1 (Apr–Jun), before most of the shortfall (August 84%, September 92%) was known. **Q3–Q4 FY27 rural demand in the South is a specific risk.**
6. **GST 2.0 was a portfolio-mix gift, not a category gift.** The Gazette confirms every home-care line at 18%; only toilet soap, toothpaste, shampoo and hair oil went to 5%. Jyothy's stated diversification into Personal Care (Margo) is therefore also a tax-advantaged move. Separately, the 13-point HI-vs-agarbatti tax gap is now documented from the primary text.
7. **Governance and capital allocation into the buyback.**
   - The promoter side received ~63% of the ₹128.5 Cr FY26 dividend.
   - CMD pay rose ~59% (commission ₹5.1 Cr vs ₹0.6 Cr) in a year PAT fell 10%.
   - New family trusts (Apr-2025) are part of succession planning.
   - A buyback (4-Nov) under the post-Oct-2024 deemed-dividend tax rule is a tax-neutral alternative to a special dividend for the family, but it removes ~₹160–400 Cr of the M&A war-chest that management said it was "aggressively" deploying.
   - The writer should frame the buyback as a signal that no large deal is imminent [E; verify on 4-Nov].
8. **Royalty arithmetic reframes the Pril loss.** Jyothy paid Henkel only ₹4.6 Cr a year, while P&G Home Products pays its parent ₹410 Cr. The licence was extraordinarily cheap, so losing it costs the franchise (shelf, liquid consumers), not a fee. The saved royalty is irrelevant to margins (CFO: "a small amount").
