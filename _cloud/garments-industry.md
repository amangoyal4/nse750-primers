**Units, conventions and FX**
- ₹ crore (Cr) = 10 million. ₹ lakh crore = ₹1 trillion. "mn pcs" = million pieces. A **piece** is one finished garment, the industry's unit of capacity, output and orders.
- **Cotton units:** one **candy = 355.6 kg** of lint (used for Indian spot prices, e.g. Shankar-6 / ICS-105 in ₹/candy). One Indian **bale = 170 kg** of lint (used for crop and consumption, in lakh bales). One US bale = 480 lb (~218 kg). ICE futures and the Cotlook A index are quoted in **US cents per lb**. Conversion: ₹68,300/candy ÷ 355.6 = ₹192/kg = $2.02/kg at ₹94.9 = **~91.8 US c/lb** [E]. Never compare candy prices with bale counts.
- FY26 = Apr-2025 to Mar-2026. Q1 FY27 = Apr–Jun 2026. Q2 FY27 = Jul–Sep 2026. All five members have a March year-end. Bangladesh's fiscal year runs Jul–Jun (its "Q1 FY27" = Jul–Sep 2026). US OTEXA, EU Eurostat and WTO data are calendar years (CY).
- **FX: ₹94.9 = US$1** (19-Oct-2026; record low near ₹96.8). It is used for all USD conversions of current figures. Historic series are left in the currency of record. FY26's average rupee was much stronger (~₹87–89/$ [E]). A FY26 ₹ revenue converted at ₹94.9 therefore **understates** its dollar value by ~7–8%. CAI used ₹95.78→₹94.96/$ for 12→16-Oct-2026 [V CAI basis sheet]. Taka ≈ Tk122/$ [E]. £1 ≈ ₹125 [E].

## READ THIS FIRST: twelve definitional traps

1. **"Garments & Apparels" holds two different businesses: exporters and domestic brands. They should never be averaged.**
   - **Contract exporters (B2B, private label):** Pearl Global, Gokaldas, Kitex, and Raymond Lifestyle's garmenting segment. Revenue is driven by pieces × FOB price. EBITDA margins are 8–13%, ROCE 10–20%. They are hit directly by US tariffs.
   - **Domestic brands (B2C):** Page (Jockey, Speedo licensee), and Raymond Lifestyle's branded textile and branded apparel. Revenue is driven by distribution × throughput × price/mix. Page earns a 20–22% EBITDA margin and **64% ROCE** [V screener]. They are hit by GST, Indian consumer demand and channel inventory, not tariffs.
   - Page alone is **64% of the group's ₹60,869 Cr market cap** but only **24% of FY26 revenue** [E: screener sums]. Any "sector P/E" is a Page number in disguise. In aggregates, show "Page" and "the other four" separately.
2. **Page is a licensee, not a brand owner.**
   - Page holds the exclusive Jockey licence (from Jockey International, Kenosha, Wisconsin). The original licence was signed 1-Apr-1994 and has been extended to **31-Dec-2040** [primer; K: extension announced 2018]. Royalty is ~5% of relevant sales [primer, FLAG — not re-verified].
   - Territory is India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, Oman, Qatar, Maldives, Bhutan, UAE, **Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Bahrain** [V Page Q1 FY27 press release]. The primer's list omits Kuwait/Bahrain (added FY25 per screener) [V screener].
   - Speedo (Pentland Group) licence for India only, ~1% of revenue [primer; V press release].
   - Page cannot sell Jockey outside the territory. It is **not an exporter** in this sub-sector's sense.
3. **Gokaldas's FY25 growth was largely inorganic.**
   - Revenue from operations went ₹2,379 Cr (FY24) → ₹3,864 Cr (FY25), +62% [V Gokaldas presentation]. Most of that came from consolidating **Atraco Group** (Dubai-headquartered, factories in **Kenya and Ethiopia**; acquired for ~US$55 mn, closed in early 2024 [primer; K]) and **Matrix Design & Industries** (Indian outerwear/fabric, 2024) [primer].
   - Organic growth on a like-for-like basis was a fraction of that [E].
   - Since FY26 the company reports **"India" and "Africa" segments**:
     - Q1 FY27 income: India ₹941 Cr, Africa ₹239 Cr.
     - Volume: India 9.2 mn pcs, Africa 5.1 mn pcs.
     - ASP: **India ₹871/pc vs Africa ₹467/pc** [V presentation].
   - The **Ethiopia** unit has not been AGOA-eligible since 1-Jan-2022 [K]. Kenya is eligible (AGOA renewed in Feb-2026; see trap 11).
4. **Pearl Global is a multi-country network, so "Indian exporter" is only about a quarter true.**
   - Production is ~**26% India : 74% non-India** (Bangladesh, Vietnam, Indonesia, Guatemala) [primer FY26].
   - In Q1 FY27 Indian operations grew 27.4% and overseas 23.6% [V PL note].
   - US tariffs on Pearl's goods depend on the **country of origin of the garment**, not Pearl's domicile. Bangladesh-made Pearl goods are eligible for the Sec 301 US-cotton TRQ (see §7). India-made goods are not.
   - Pearl's revenue is therefore a poor proxy for "India apparel exports". Its India-made share is roughly ₹1,300 Cr of ₹5,025 Cr [E: 26%].
5. **Kitex: concentration, the Telangana capex, and calendar mismatches.**
   - Kitex (Kizhakkambalam, Kerala) is an infantwear (0–24 months) specialist with **~80–92% of revenue from the US** [primer; V-snip apparelresources].
   - Its customers (Carter's, Walmart, Target, Amazon, The Children's Place, Gerber [primer]) buy for the **US retail calendar** (back-to-school in Jul–Aug, holiday in Oct–Dec). Shipments for holiday land in Jul–Sep (Q2 FY), so Indian fiscal quarters cut the US season in half. Compare Kitex on **half-years or trailing-12-months**, not single quarters [E].
   - Since Aug-2025 it also consolidates the debt-funded Telangana subsidiary, **Kitex Apparel Parks**:
     - Warangal began commercial production ~29-Aug-2025 [V-snip BS]. It sits in the **Kakatiya Mega Textile Park, the first operational PM MITRA park** [V-snip].
     - Sitarampur (Hyderabad) is pending.
     - Committed capex is ~₹3,550 Cr; borrowings ~₹1,199 Cr at Mar-26 [primer].
   - FY26 consolidated revenue fell to ₹667 Cr (from ₹983 Cr) with a loss of ₹13 Cr [V screener]. A standalone vs consolidated split matters (some trackers show a ~₹9 Cr Q1 FY27 loss, others ₹17 Cr) [V-snip].
   - Screener flags "might be capitalizing the interest cost" and other income of ₹76.7 Cr [V screener].
6. **Raymond Lifestyle: four segments, and EBITDA has three definitions.**
   - FY26 segment revenue: branded textile ~₹3,435 Cr, branded apparel ~₹1,812 Cr, garmenting ~₹1,066 Cr, plus high-value cotton shirting (HVCS) [primer].
   - FY26 EBITDA is **₹804 Cr company-defined** [primer] vs **₹678 Cr screener OP** [V screener]. Company EBITDA includes other income.
   - Q1 FY27 has a third measure, **"operating EBITDA" ₹135 Cr (8.6%) vs reported EBITDA ₹89.8 Cr (5.9%)** [V apparelresources]. Quote the definition every time.
   - Its garmenting segment (suits, jackets, trousers for global brands, largely US/EU; ~₹296 Cr in Q1 FY27, +50% YoY) is the only exporter-type business inside RLL [V apparelresources].
   - Wedding-season timing (Hindu auspicious dates) moves branded textile between Q3 and Q4, and sometimes between fiscal years [K]. Q4 FY25 OP margin was 0.9% and Q4 FY26 7% [V screener]. Do not annualise a single quarter.
7. **FOB vs CMT vs CIF: which "price" a number is.**
   - **CMT** (cut-make-trim) = the buyer supplies fabric and the factory earns a conversion fee (~25–35% of FOB) [E].
   - **FOB / full package** = the factory buys fabric and trims and sells the finished garment at the export port. This is the basis of Indian export statistics and of all members' revenue [K].
   - **CIF/landed** = FOB + freight + insurance + US import duty. This is what US retailers compare across countries, and what the tariff hits.
   - A 10% tariff is levied on the **customs value** (≈FOB for US imports) [K]. So a 10-point tariff ≈ 10% of the exporter's own invoice. That is why **discounts of 3–7% of FOB** were enough to share the burden (Gokaldas: ₹97 Cr "US tariff impact" in FY26 ≈ 2.4% of revenue, or ~5% of US revenue [V presentation; E]).
8. **Exports in US$ vs ₹, and calendar vs fiscal.**
   - AEPC/Commerce Ministry report RMG exports in **US$ by Indian fiscal year** (e.g. FY25 ~$16.0 bn).
   - OTEXA reports **US imports from India by calendar year** at US customs value (2025: $4.93 bn) [V csdronline]. WTO reports calendar-year world exports (2024 India $16.36 bn) [V-snip].
   - With the rupee moving from ~₹84 (FY25) to ₹94.9 (Oct-26), a flat dollar export number is **+10–13% in rupees**. Company ₹ growth for exporters is flattered by FX.
9. **HS 61 vs HS 62, and "textiles" vs "apparel".**
   - HS chapter **61 = knitted or crocheted apparel** (T-shirts, polos, innerwear, babywear: Kitex, Page's products, Tiruppur). Chapter **62 = woven apparel** (shirts, trousers, suits, outerwear: Gokaldas, RLL garmenting, much of Pearl). Chapter 63 = made-ups/home textiles (not apparel).
   - Government "textiles & apparel" totals (~$36–37 bn) include yarn, fabric, made-ups, jute, handicrafts and carpets [K MoT]. **RMG (ready-made garments) is ~43–45% of that** [E]. A "textile export" headline is not an apparel number.
   - US MFN duties on apparel range from **0% to 32%** (e.g. synthetic-fibre knits at the top end) [V csdronline]. Averages cited are ~12–16% [V-snip tariffstool: "16.5%" for Indian clothing].
10. **"Tariff on India" means different things on different dates.** Always state the date and the stack (MFN + IEEPA reciprocal + Russia penalty, or MFN + Sec 122, or MFN + Sec 301). The full timeline is in §7.1 and the history in §8. Since 24-Jul-2026, Indian apparel pays **MFN + 10% (Sec 301)**. China and Vietnam pay **MFN + 12.5%**. Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia and Malaysia pay **MFN + 10%, or 0% within a US-cotton TRQ** [V csdronline; V PL; V Gokaldas].
11. **AGOA (Africa) is a separate regime** that matters to Gokaldas (Kenya).
   - AGOA lapsed 30-Sep-2025. A one-year reauthorisation to **31-Dec-2026** (retroactive) was signed in early Feb-2026 [V-snip the-star 5-Feb-26].
   - A two-year extension to Dec-2028 passed the Senate 90–6. One Kenyan outlet reports it was signed 4-Sep(-2026); another says House concurrence was still needed [V-snip — CONFLICT].
   - Gokaldas credits "AGOA renewal" for Africa +45% in Q1 FY27 [V].
12. **Cotton: candy vs bale, crop year vs fiscal year.**
   - The Indian cotton season runs Oct–Sep (CAI "2026-27" season started 1-Oct-2026) [V CAI]. Crop numbers are in lakh bales of 170 kg. Prices are in ₹/candy (355.6 kg).
   - MSP is in ₹/quintal of **kapas** (seed cotton, ~33–35% lint outturn), not lint [K]. MSP ₹8,267/qtl kapas ≈ ₹24,000–25,000/qtl lint-equivalent after seed credit [E]. Do not compare MSP with ₹/candy directly.

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Five members, two industries, one sub-sector label.**
  - **Three pure exporters plus a large export segment inside Raymond Lifestyle:**
    - Pearl Global (₹5,025 Cr FY26, OP 10%).
    - Gokaldas (₹3,988 Cr, OP 9%).
    - Kitex (₹667 Cr, OP 4.5%).
    - RLL garmenting (~₹1,066 Cr).
  - **One branded staple:** Page (₹5,247 Cr, OP 22%).
  - **A hybrid:** Raymond Lifestyle (₹6,888 Cr, OP 10%).
  - FY26 combined revenue **₹21,815 Cr (+6.6% YoY), OP ₹2,702 Cr (12.4%), PAT ₹1,167 Cr**. Market cap **₹60,869 Cr** on 21-Oct-2026 [E: sums of screener].
  - Q1 FY27 combined revenue **₹5,776 Cr, +12.6% YoY**. Growth was led by the exporters (Pearl +24.5%, Gokaldas +21%), with Page +7.9%, RLL +5.9% and Kitex −19.5% [V screener; V company].
- **The US tariff roller-coaster (Apr-2025 → Jul-2026) is the defining event of the decade for Indian apparel exporters.**
  - **50% + MFN** from 27-Aug-2025 (25% reciprocal + 25% Russia-oil penalty) [V-snip AEPC/DH] versus **19–20%** for Bangladesh, Vietnam, Cambodia, Indonesia and Sri Lanka [K]. That gap cost India orders.
  - **US apparel imports from India fell 28.0% in Jan–Apr 2026 and 25.75% in Jan–Jul 2026** (to $2.45 bn) while the US total fell ~9–12% [V Shenglu; V-snip textilesresources/OTEXA].
  - India's apparel exports fell **12% YoY in Q1 FY27** [V Gokaldas, citing MoC] and **6.2% in Apr–Sep 2026 to ~$7.2 bn**. September 2026 alone was **+13.7% to $1.1 bn** — the first clear post-tariff rebound [V-snip AEPC via textilesresources 20-Oct-26].
  - The sequence that ended the crisis:
    - An India–US interim framework (announced early Feb-2026, reciprocal rate to be cut to 18%) [V-snip].
    - **SCOTUS struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20-Feb-2026** [V-snip].
    - A **Sec 122 10% global tariff for 150 days from 24-Feb-2026** [V-snip ianslive].
    - Since **24-Jul-2026, a Sec 301 (forced-labour) tariff of 10% on India vs 12.5% on China and Vietnam** [V csdronline; V PL; V Gokaldas].
  - For the first time since 2025, **India has a tariff edge over China and Vietnam** and parity with Bangladesh/Cambodia (except their US-cotton TRQ) [V Gokaldas].
- **The companies absorbed part of the shock.**
  - Gokaldas booked a **₹97 Cr "US tariff impact" in FY26** (discounts to US customers). Its adjusted EBITDA margin was 13.0% vs reported 10.7% [V presentation].
  - Kitex's FY26 revenue fell 32% and it swung to a loss [V screener].
  - Pearl's multi-country network let it route orders and grow 11.5% [V screener].
  - IEEPA refunds are now being processed in the US, but "customers are largely not passing them on to exporters" (Pearl, Aug-26) [V PL note].
- **Trade agreements flipped from handicap to edge in Europe.**
  - **India–UK CETA entered into force 15-Jul-2026**. It removes UK duties of up to 12% on Indian apparel (1,143 textile lines at zero) and puts India at **parity with Bangladesh/Vietnam and 12 points ahead of China** in the UK (a $28.8 bn T&C import market where India is the 4th supplier) [V-snip drishtiias/fashionunited; V Gokaldas].
  - **India–EU FTA concluded 27-Jan-2026** with apparel at zero duty from day one, but it is **not yet in force** (ratification; expected 2027) [V-snip].
- **Bangladesh is the competitor to watch: still huge, but carrying three risks.**
  - Bangladesh RMG exports were **$10.58 bn in Jul–Sep 2026 (+6.1%)** [V-snip EPB/UNB]. It is #2 in world apparel (6.9% share 2024) and has overtaken China as #2 US supplier in Jan–Jul 2026 [V-snip].
  - The three risks: (1) post-2024 political transition (BNP's Tarique Rahman sworn in 17-Feb-2026) [V-snip]; (2) **LDC graduation scheduled 24-Nov-2026** (deferral of 3 years requested on 18-Feb-2026; outcome not found) [V-snip]; (3) wage pressure (Tk 12,500/month minimum since Dec-2023; unions want Tk 23–25k) [V-snip].
  - India also restricted Bangladeshi RMG imports via land ports (May-2025) [V-snip tribune: RMG imports from BD −27% Apr–Sep].
- **Cotton has turned from tailwind to headwind.**
  - Indian cotton (ICS-105 29 mm Gujarat ≈ Shankar-6) was **₹68,300/candy on 21-Oct-2026** [V CAI], up ~25% YoY [V-snip], after testing ~₹68,700 in May-2026 [V CAI weekly].
  - ICE Dec-26 is **80.88 c/lb** and Cotlook A **~91 c/lb**. Indian cotton trades near import parity [V CAI].
  - MSP for 2026-27 rose to **₹8,267/qtl (medium staple) and ₹8,667 (long staple)** [V-snip]. The government waived the **11% import duty** in both Aug–Dec 2025 [K] and Jun–Oct 2026 [V csdronline].
  - Polyester/MMF inputs spiked with crude after Feb-2026 [V-sib]. The QCOs on PTA/MEG/PSF/polyester yarn were **rescinded on 12-Nov-2025**, which lets garment makers import cheaper fibre [V-snip PTI/Crisil].
- **Domestic branded demand is steady but split by price point under GST 2.0.**
  - From **22-Sep-2025**, garments ≤₹2,500/piece pay **5% GST** (threshold raised from ₹1,000) and >₹2,500 pay **18%** (from 12%) [V-snip PIB/FinMin]. This helps Page (average realisation **₹232/pc** in Q1 FY27 [E: ₹1,420 Cr ÷ 61.2 mn pcs]).
  - It hurts premium suits, blazers and ethnic sets above ₹2,500 (Raymond, Ethnix), while a saree stays at 5% at any price as a "textile" [V-snip The Wire].
  - Page's volumes are recovering after the FY23–FY25 channel destock: FY26 228.4 mn pcs (+3.9%), Q4 FY26 +10.8%, Q1 FY27 61.2 mn (+5.7%), FY27 guide double-digit [primer; V press release; V-snip sahi].
- **Valuations price two very different stories** (screener 21-Oct-26):
  - **Page 49.8× P/E**, 25.9× book, ROCE 64% — de-rated from 60–90× [V; primer].
  - **Pearl 35.9×**, 7.7× book, ROCE 19.9% — re-rated on volume growth; stock +66.5% over 12 months to early Aug [V; V PL].
  - **Gokaldas 48.7×** on depressed FY26 EPS, ROCE 7.65% (company ROCE 12–13%) [V screener; V presentation].
  - **Raymond Lifestyle 22.6× on trailing profit, 0.35× book** [V].
  - **Kitex loss-making, 2.2× book** [V; E].
