- **Net cash is wrong:** "~₹1,470 Cr net cash" and "~₹1,517 Cr cash & investments" — company cash & equivalents were **₹853 Cr (31-Mar-2026)** and **₹943 Cr (30-Jun-2026)**; debt ₹47 Cr → net cash ~₹806 Cr (Mar-26). ₹1,472 Cr is **net worth** (CARE/CRISIL). Fix the cover stat, §06 "cash fortress" (incl. "a fifth of the company is money in the bank" → ~14%), §09 and bull/bear bullets.
- **Valuation stale:** m-cap "~₹7,600 Cr" and "~37×" → **₹5,853 Cr, P/E 28.3** (screener 3-Nov-2026); stock near its 52-week low (₹4,398).
- **Make-vs-buy wrong for sanitaryware:** primer says sanitaryware is "mostly in-house". Company: **60% of sanitaryware and 46% of faucets outsourced (Q4 FY26)**; ~59% of revenue from outsourced manufacturing; tiles and wellness 100% outsourced (CareEdge). Sanitaryware outsourcing is "mostly from Morbi".
- **Capacity figures stale/unsupported:** "~36 L sanitaryware + ~18.5 L faucet pieces" → **33,750 MTPA sanitaryware** and **48 lakh faucet pieces p.a. (4 lakh/month), rising to 60 lakh (5 lakh/month) from Q4 FY27** (CareEdge, Q4 FY26 call).
- **Brand ladder incomplete:** add **Polipluz** (value brand; ₹8.5 Cr FY26 → ₹30–35 Cr target; 102 distributors/1,120 dealers → 200/2,000). Senator: ₹10.5 Cr FY26 → ₹40–45 Cr target.
- **Network:** "~20,000 dealer touch-points" → **7,000+ dealers and 29,000+ retailers**; formats: 13 ECs, 272 CSGs, 292 Style Hubs, **1,613 Style Centres already**, with 1,400+ more planned (the primer reads 1,400 as the total target from "a few hundred").
- **Revenue definition change (Q1 FY27):** turnover discounts now netted from revenue (−2.5% to −3%); FY26 comparatives restated; add to the "what's new" box.
- **Q1 FY27 not reflected:** revenue ₹486 Cr (+19.5%), EBITDA 10.1%, PAT ₹45 Cr; gas ₹48.43/m³; brass ~₹900/kg; price hikes 12% SW / 16% FW; one kiln shut Mar–May; Baliga (CBO) resigned; A&P ₹85 Cr budget; Kriti Sanon.
- **FY23 revenue** "~₹1,760 Cr" → ₹1,803 Cr (screener).
- **Peer table:** "HSIL / Hindware ~₹2,800 Cr" conflates HSIL (now AGI Greenpac, glass) with Hindware Home Innovation (FY26 ₹2,510 Cr consolidated; bathware ₹1,520 Cr); mention the NCLT-sanctioned split (14-Oct-2026). Kajaria ₹4,830 Cr, Somany ₹2,790 Cr (FY26).
- **Subsidiaries:** primer implies tile JVs; Cera divested its only subsidiaries (51% of Packcart Packaging LLP and Race Polymer Arts LLP) on 29-Sep-2025 and now reports standalone only.
- **Gas detail:** quantify — GAIL ~64–70% / Sabarmati ~30–36%; Sabarmati cut to half in Mar-2026 and restored; Cera's gas cost ~half of Morbi's ₹82–90/SCM.
