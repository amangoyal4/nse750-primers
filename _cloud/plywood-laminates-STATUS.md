## h1
Plywood &amp; laminates: <em>a brand moat beside a board glut</em>

## Forces
1. Formalisation via the BIS QCOs (wood-based boards 11-Feb-2025; plywood 28-Feb-2025) plus the carpenter/dealer brand moat: CY2025 imports plywood −43%, MDF −36%, PB −50%; branded plywood grows 3–5× the market (Century plywood volume +28.8% Q1 FY27, EBITDA 16.9%).
2. MDF capacity glut: 4.0–4.5 mn CBM vs ~3.0 mn CBM demand; more lines H2 FY27/2027; Century MDF 7.0% EBITDA Q1 FY27 vs Greenply 17.3%.
3. Input costs that cannot be hedged: timber (local) and resins (methanol +~182% YoY May-26, CRISIL); plywood +7% hike stuck, MDF +15% largely rolled back (Rushil the exception).
4. Capex and ROCE dilution: FY26 segment capital plywood ₹1,055 Cr at ~41% pre-tax vs MDF ₹1,526 Cr at ~7% and PB ₹700 Cr at −4.6% (AR note 43); FY31 ₹12,000 Cr aspiration needs ~₹2,500 Cr more capex.
5. Laminate exports: freight, not the 10% US Section 301 tariff, is the binding cost (Stylam 65% export sheets).
