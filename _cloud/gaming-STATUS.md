## Forces

2. Tax as the precursor shock — 28% GST on face value; DGGI v Gameskraft upheld it retrospectively (27-May-2026); 91 SCNs >₹1.44 lakh Cr.
3. The surviving market is small, global-IP-led and IAP-led — India 0.74% of a US$201.6bn world market; Lumikai US$1.5bn CY25, 555m gamers, 25% payers.
4. M&A-led platform building + UA arbitrage — Nazara's Bluetile/BestPlay (US$303m; 85% UA), Moonshine ₹1,058.5 Cr written off.
5. Accounting complexity and currency — four PATs; 87.3% of Nazara revenue from outside India.
