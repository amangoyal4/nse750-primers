## Forces
1. Free-to-paid conversion (14.4 mn paid subs, ~3% of internet users; Spotify cut to ₹139).
2. Content-cost inflation vs catalogue yield (members' content investment ₹142 Cr FY22 → ₹399 Cr FY26).
3. Platform power and consolidation (Resso, Wynk and Hungama exits 2024–25; digital licensing +2% in 2025; fixed-fee Shorts/Reels).
4. Accounting shapes perception (Tips expenses 100% of content at release; Saregama amortises and reports adjusted EBITDA before the content charge).
5. Diversification vs purity (Saregama flywheel: artiste mgmt, live, video, Carvaan, Bhansali; Tips asset-light), plus copyright/AI as moat and risk.
