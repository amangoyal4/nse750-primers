## Forces
1. Import competition and the policy toolkit (BIS QCOs, anti-dumping duties) deciding category economics — China ~70% of imported glass/plastic/steel houseware; flask imports −87% after the QCO.
2. Input-cost pass-through (resin, steel, soda ash, gas) and the 2026 West Asia shock — 7–20% price hikes, furnace gas +80%.
3. Distribution reach and the channel shift — 1.8 lakh retailers; online 10.5% (FY26) → 16.3% (Q1 FY27).
4. Fixed-cost manufacturing bets where utilisation sets profit — Falna glass ~60% vs Daman opal 80–85% vs La Opala 37% OPM.
5. Brand ownership and promoter control — CPIW owns the marks; 73.50% promoter; Wim Plast merger; Bain Capital report.
(Also: GST 2.0 asymmetry, pens as a second engine, appliance vs non-electric divergence.)
