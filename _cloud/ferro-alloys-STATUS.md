## h1
Indian ferro alloys: <em>ore, power and a Zambian power plant</em>

## Forces
1. **Ferrochrome price** = stainless demand (China/Indonesia) against South African supply. Eskom's tariff deal cuts power from R1.36 to R0.62/kWh, so SA smelters are restarting. The Tsingshan tender peaked at RMB 8,495 and was cut in Oct-26.
2. **Captive ore + captive power are the only durable moat.** MMDR 2015/2021; IMFA's leases run to 2049/2055; converters sit higher on the cost curve than integrated producers.
3. **Nava is a Zambian coal-power IPP.**
   - Maamba: US$151.4 Mn EBITDA in FY26 at 89.6% PLF.
   - ZESCO arrears down to US$13.4 Mn; kwacha drives deferred tax.
   - Phase II (300 MW) due ~Jun–Jul 2027; about 24% of profit goes to the minority.
4. **IMFA's scale-up against the cost curve:** 384k → 484k → 534k tpa; FY27 guide cut to ~380 kt; FY28 475–500 kt.
5. **Trade policy:**
   - EU safeguard Reg. 2025/2351 sets SiMn/FeMn quotas and does not cover FeCr, while CBAM covers FeCr/FeMn.
   - US tariffs; India's steel safeguard; India crude steel 168.4 mt in FY26.
