## Forces
1. The price stack — LME + regional premia (US Midwest record $1.19/lb; MJP $195→$395→$255 in 2026) + the rupee: set abroad, not in India.
2. The cost curve — alumina, power, coal: integration is the moat; NALCO's bottom-of-curve alumina (₹20–22k/t) and the 2024–25 alumina spike/normalisation.
3. Novelis — a conversion-spread, recycling business: Oswego fires (16-Sep and 20-Nov-2025; restart Jun-2026), Bay Minette ~$5bn, Section 232 50%, leverage 4.5×.
4. Supply has stalled (China 45 Mt cap reached; Gulf smelters hit by West Asia war) while India uses 6.12 Mt vs 4.26 Mt primary output (2.03 Mt scrap imports; duties 7.5%/2.5%).
5. Carbon — EU CBAM definitive from 1-Jan-2026 vs coal-based Indian smelters (~19 tCO2e/t).
