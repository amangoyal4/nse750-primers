# Construction Equipment — primer issues

## From Construction Equipment research (2026-10-25, construction-equipment-industry.md §3.4)

**beml.html**
- **Margin numbers are Q4, not full-year.**
  - The snapshot, "whatsnew", §08 chart and §10 show "EBITDA margin ~25.6% → ~15%" and "EBITDA ~₹650 Cr".
  - Screener consolidated full-year OP was **₹506 Cr (12.6%) in FY25 and ₹299 Cr (6.9%) in FY26**. 26% and 15% are the **Q4 FY25 and Q4 FY26** quarterly OPMs.
  - Company FY27 guidance ("13–14%, a return to FY25 levels") confirms FY25 was ~13%.
  - Fix the stat strip ("~15% EBITDA" → "~7% EBITDA (FY26)") and the "margin halved from 25.6%" narrative. It halved from ~13%. (§00, §08, §10)
- **Disinvestment chronology is wrong.**
  - The primer dates the in-principle approval to **Dec-2019**. The CCEA approved strategic disinvestment of 26% with management control in **Oct-2016** [K, PIB].
  - DIPAM's PIM/EoI was **3-Jan-2021** (bids to 22-Mar-2021) [V-snip Business Standard]. "Pending since 2019" should read "approved 2016; EoIs 2021; dormant since".
  - The timeline entry "2016 — the government reduces its stake via a market offering… retains ~54%" is unverified. The 54.03% holding appears long-standing [FLAG: verify, or delete]. (§06 timeline, §07)
- **BEML Land Assets:** "incorporated 2021" and "separately listed" (2023–25) — give the exact demerger/listing dates [K — verify]. (§06, §07)
- **Segment mix missing.** The primer says segment splits are "illustrative".
  - Add FY26 revenue mix **M&C 41% / Defence 35% / Rail & Metro 24%** (FY25 54/27/19).
  - Add the order-book mix **~68% R&M / 25% Defence / 7% M&C** (Q3 FY26).
  - §02 calls mining "still one of its largest arms". It is now the smallest forward driver. (§01–§05)
- **Q1 FY27 omitted (refreshed 18-Jul):**
  - Revenue ₹820 Cr (+29%); EBITDA +₹3 Cr (first positive Q1 in ten years); net loss ₹27 Cr.
  - Inflow ₹1,181 Cr (M&C 589 / Def 581 / R&M 11); **order book ₹16,284 Cr**.
  - FY27 guidance: revenue +30%, EBITDA 13–14%, inflow ₹20,000 Cr, pipeline ₹40,000 Cr.

- **Valuation stale:** m-cap ~₹14,660 Cr → **₹15,419 Cr**; P/E ~99× → **86.4×**; 52-week high ₹2,364 → ₹2,268; add P/B 5.26.

**action-construction-equipment.html**
- **Revenue/EBITDA basis mislabelled.**
  - Snapshot "Revenue ~₹3,390 Cr" and "~18.3% EBITDA" are **standalone total income (₹3,395 Cr) and EBITDA including other income (₹622 Cr = 18.33% of total income)**, per the Q4 call.
  - Consolidated revenue from operations is **₹3,280 Cr (−1.4%)**, OP **₹504 Cr (15.4%)**, PAT ₹415 Cr [screener; ICICI].
  - The §10 table and "record ~18.3% margin" should be relabelled. On the screener basis the margin was **flat** (15.2% → 15.4%), not a record expansion. (§00, §04, §06, §10)
- **FY26 volume decline omitted.**
  - CMCE volumes fell **19% to 10,853 units** (Q4 3,458, −14%) after the FY25 CEV-V pre-buy. The old-generation Hydra segment (~60% of cranes) "shrank" after the Tier 3 → Stage V jump (+12–15% price).
  - The primer frames CEV-V only as a "replacement catalyst". Add the pre-buy/dip mechanics. (§06 "CEV-V wedge", §07)
- **Market-share conflict.** "~63% mobile cranes… the rest split among far smaller players" vs Escorts Kubota's own claim of **41.4% of pick-and-carry cranes in FY26 (ICEMA), "India's number one"** [V-sib tractors.html]. State both with bases; do not present 63% as uncontested.
- **Rivals table outdated.** It lists "Escorts, TIL" as crane rivals.
  - **TIL, Tadano Escorts India, Kobelco India, ABG Cranes and Tata Hitachi have shut crane manufacturing** (industry, via ET/Outlook).
  - Escorts Kubota CE (₹1,686 Cr, P&C cranes) is the real domestic rival.
  - In heavy cranes the Chinese (SANY, XCMG, Zoomlion) hold ~95%.
- **Segments:** the primer describes four segments and puts forklifts in both "Construction Equipment" and "Material Handling".
  - ACE reports **two segments (CMCE ~90%, agri ~7.7%) plus "others" ₹76.55 Cr**.
  - The "cranes ~65–70%" figure is an estimate. Label it.
