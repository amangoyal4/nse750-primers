## Units, conventions and FX
- ₹ crore (Cr) = 10 million; lakh = 100,000; ₹ lakh Cr = ₹1 trillion.
- FY26 = Apr-2025–Mar-2026. Q1 FY27 = Apr–Jun 2026. Q2 FY27 = Jul–Sep 2026. All five have a March year-end.
- **FX ₹94.9 = US$1** (19-Oct-2026) for every USD conversion in this file.
- Gas units:
  - scm = standard cubic metre.
  - mmscmd = million scm per day. MMSCM = million scm over a period (1 mmscmd × 365 ≈ 365 MMSCM; × 91 ≈ 91 MMSCM for a quarter).
  - TBTU = trillion BTU (Petronet throughput). mmbtu = million BTU.
  - Rule of thumb: **1 mtpa LNG ≈ 52 TBTU/yr ≈ 3.6–3.8 mmscmd** [K].
- **EBITDA/scm** = standalone EBITDA ÷ standalone volume (scm) for the same period. It is the CGD master KPI. Unless stated, the EBITDA used is screener standalone operating profit or the company-reported EBITDA, which differ by <₹15 Cr for these three.
- **Screener "Operating Profit" (OP)** = sales − expenses, **before other income**. It is the EBITDA proxy in all tables. Where company EBITDA differs materially it is shown beside it.
- **PAT bases:**
  - "PAT reported" = screener consolidated net profit, **including minority interest** (Aegis only is material).
  - "PAT attributable" = profit attributable to owners of the parent.
  - **No member reports an exceptional-items line for FY26 on screener.** Known one-offs are listed in trap 9.

## READ THIS FIRST: eleven definitional traps

1. **Gross vs net-of-excise revenue (CGDs).** Central excise is levied on CNG; the companies report revenue both gross and net.
   - **MGL Q1 FY27:** gross ₹2,597.89 Cr vs net ₹2,371.71 Cr (factor 1.095) [V-snip]. Screener shows the **net** figure (₹2,372 Cr SA) [V].
   - **ATGL Q1 FY27:** company "revenue" ₹1,910 Cr (gross) vs screener SA ₹1,743 Cr (net; factor 1.096) [V].
   - **IGL Q1 FY27:** news "₹5,040 Cr" (gross) vs screener ₹4,583 Cr SA / PL ₹4,580 Cr (net) [V].
   - **Our ATGL primer (₹6,415 Cr) and MGL primer (₹9,185 Cr) quote gross FY26 revenue.** Screener net: ATGL ₹5,894 Cr cons., MGL ₹8,246 Cr cons. The IGL primer (~₹16,451 Cr) is close to, but 1.8% above, screener net SA ₹16,167 Cr.
   - **Use net revenue in every table; never mix.** EBITDA margin % differs by ~1.5 pp between the two bases, which is another reason to quote EBITDA/scm instead.
2. **Attributable vs pre-minority PAT (Aegis only; material).**
   - FY26: **₹1,107 Cr reported vs ₹898 Cr attributable.**
   - Q1 FY27: **₹545 Cr vs ₹484 Cr** (EPS ₹13.80 × 35.1 Cr shares).
   - Q1 FY26 base: ₹175 Cr vs ₹131 Cr.
   - **Screener's P/E (37.1) uses TTM attributable EPS (₹35.65).** FY26 attributable P/E = 51.7×.
   - For the other four, reported = attributable.
3. **Consolidated vs standalone — what differs materially.**
   - IGL PAT (+13% from JVs).
   - Petronet PAT (+1.8%).
   - ATGL PAT (+3%).
   - MGL (−0.7%, UEPL losses).
   - Aegis everything (AVTL inside).
   - At the EBITDA line consolidated ≈ standalone for all four non-Aegis names, because JV profit sits below EBITDA.
   - **CGD operating KPIs (volume, stations, connections) are standalone** unless labelled "incl. JV".
4. **EBITDA/scm basis.** Standalone EBITDA ÷ standalone volume, same period.
   - FY26 from screener SA OP: **ATGL ₹10.49** (company EBITDA ₹1,225 Cr → ₹10.81), **IGL ₹5.45**, **MGL ₹8.66** [E].
   - Q1 FY27: **ATGL ₹9.27** (company EBITDA ₹281 Cr ÷ 303 MMSCM; screener OP ₹268 Cr → ₹8.84), **IGL ₹3.35–3.4**, **MGL ₹7.91** [E; IGL/MGL match company/PL].
   - The **MGL primer's "~₹8.3"** does not reproduce. ₹8.66 is the FY26 figure on its own EBITDA ₹1,451 Cr and 4.59 mmscmd.
5. **MGL Q1 FY26 base contains a one-off.** Reported Q1 FY26 EBITDA ₹500.7 Cr (₹12.3/scm) vs **PL-adjusted ₹390 Cr (₹9.6/scm)** [V PL]. Q1 FY27's "−31.5% YoY EBITDA" is therefore **−11.6% adjusted**, and PAT −39% is **−18% adjusted**.
6. **Petronet's revenue is mostly pass-through, and the Qatar FM gutted it.**
   - Q1 FY27 sales ₹5,558 Cr cons. (−53%), yet OP ₹1,535 Cr (+32%) and PAT ₹1,137 Cr (+35%) [V screener].
   - Q1 EBITDA includes **₹300 Cr trading gain + ₹190 Cr inventory gain** [V PL].
   - "Sales volume" (Petronet's own LNG: Qatar back-to-back + spot) fell to **31 TBTU** (vs 118). "Regas" (tolling) volume rose to **176 TBTU** (vs 102) [V PL].

   - **ATGL:** investments ₹1,690 Cr are mostly the **IOAGPL JV** (equity-accounted). Treasury-only net debt = borrowings ₹2,255 Cr − cash ₹505 Cr = **₹1,750 Cr net debt**, vs screener-basis ₹60 Cr [E].
