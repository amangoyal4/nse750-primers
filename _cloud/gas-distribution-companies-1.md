## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **Consolidated vs standalone: which members differ materially?**
   - **IGL:** PAT differs by ~₹180 Cr (13%) because of the MNGL/CUGL JV share. Revenue/EBITDA barely differ. The primer's headline (₹16,451 Cr revenue, ₹1,850 Cr EBITDA, ₹1,364 Cr PAT) is **standalone**. **Consolidated PAT is ₹1,543 Cr** [primer]. Use consolidated PAT for P/E.
   - **ATGL:** standalone PAT ₹637 Cr vs consolidated ₹656 Cr [primer]. The JV (IOAGPL) volumes are excluded from standalone operating KPIs. **Station and PNG-home counts "incl. JV" (1,169 stations; 13.1 lakh homes) are NOT ATGL-standalone KPIs.**
   - **MGL:** consolidated adds Unison. FY26 revenue: consolidated ₹9,185 Cr vs standalone ~₹8,240 Cr [primer]. PAT barely differs.
   - **Petronet:** standalone ≈ consolidated. FY26 PAT: standalone ₹3,843 Cr, consolidated ₹3,809 Cr [primer].
   - **Aegis:** consolidated includes AVTL (44.71% owned) — see trap 2.
2. **Attributable vs pre-minority PAT (Aegis only; material).**
   - FY26: ₹1,107 Cr pre-minority vs **₹898 Cr attributable**. Q1 FY27: **₹545 Cr** pre-minority (the investor deck's "net profit", +212% vs ₹175 Cr) vs **₹484 Cr** attributable (+269% vs ₹131 Cr) [V-snip sahi/kotakneo/filingreader].
   - Our primer quotes ₹1,107 Cr as "net profit" and builds a ~50× P/E on it.
   - **Use attributable PAT for P/E and EPS. Use pre-minority PAT only alongside consolidated EBITDA/EV.**
   - In Part 3 I show both. The summary grid's headline basis is **reported consolidated PAT incl. minorities** (one basis for all five), with an attributable column next to it.
3. **Excise duty in CGD revenue.**
   - CNG attracts central excise (and state VAT). IGL/MGL/ATGL report "revenue from operations" **gross** and also **net of excise**. Example: MGL Q1 FY27 standalone revenue was **₹2,597.89 Cr gross** vs **₹2,371.71 Cr net of excise** [V-snip sahi/indianchemicalnews].
   - Screener usually shows gross "Sales" for CGD names. Margins (EBITDA/revenue) therefore differ by ~1–1.5 pp depending on the basis.
   - **Always quote EBITDA/scm, not EBITDA margin %, for CGD.** Revenue is mostly gas cost passed through.
4. **Screener "operating profit" vs company EBITDA.**
   - **Aegis:** "normalised EBITDA" (₹1,599 Cr FY26; ₹727 Cr Q1 FY27) excludes one-offs/FX, and the reported EBITDA differs (Q1 FY27 reported ₹715 Cr vs normalised segment ₹727 Cr) [V-snip investywise].
   - **Petronet:** EBITDA is not in our primer and I could not fetch it. My FY26 estimate is ~₹5,350 Cr, built up from PAT + tax + D&A + interest − other income [E]. Check against screener.
   - **MGL:** the primer's "EBITDA/scm ~₹8.3" does not reproduce from its own consolidated EBITDA: ₹1,451 Cr ÷ (4.59 mmscmd × 365) = **₹8.66/scm** [E]. The ₹8.3 is probably standalone EBITDA or a quarterly average.
5. **JV share of profit sits below EBITDA (ATGL, IGL).** Their EBITDA/scm uses standalone EBITDA ÷ standalone volume. Adding JV profit to EBITDA, or JV volumes to the denominator, breaks the KPI. IGL's MNGL/CUGL profit (~₹180 Cr) is ~12% of consolidated PAT.
6. **Net cash includes bank deposits and mutual funds.**
   - All three CGDs and Petronet are net-cash. Screener's "investments" line can miss fixed deposits (shown under "other assets"/cash) and can include strategic JV stakes (IGL's MNGL/CUGL; Aegis's investments).
   - Company figures are what I have: IGL "~₹4,000 Cr+ cash" [primer]; Aegis "~₹4,150 Cr debt vs ~₹6,000 Cr cash & investments" [primer] — much of that cash is AVTL IPO money inside a 44.71%-owned subsidiary.
   - **MGL, Petronet and ATGL net cash/debt are n/s this session.** I show [K]-approximate values clearly marked.
7. **Petronet's revenue is mostly a pass-through, and Q1 FY27 is distorted by the Qatar halt.**
   - About half of Dahej throughput is QatarEnergy long-term LNG, which Petronet **buys and resells back-to-back**. When no Qatar cargo arrived (Q1 FY27, Hormuz disruption), revenue fell **from ~₹11,880 Cr to ~₹5,554 Cr (−53%)** [V-snip psuconnect/investywise].
   - Yet PAT **rose 33%**, helped by ~₹301 Cr of trading gains and ~₹193 Cr of inventory gains [V-snip multibagg].
   - **Never compute Petronet margin % across quarters.** Use TBTU and ₹/mmbtu.
8. **Petronet utilisation denominator changed on 31-Mar-2026.** Dahej went from 17.5 to **22.5 mtpa**. Q1 FY27 Dahej utilisation of ~65.6% is on 22.5 mtpa; the "92% a year earlier" was on 17.5 mtpa [V-snip]. On the old base, Q1 FY27's 192 TBTU is ~84% [E: 192 ÷ (17.5 × 52 ÷ 4) ≈ 0.84].
9. **CGD "volume" scope.**
   - ATGL quotes MMSCM per period: FY26 1,133 MMSCM = **3.10 mmscmd** [E]. Q1 FY27 303 MMSCM standalone = 3.33 mmscmd; **496 MMSCM incl. the IOAGPL JV** [V-snip].
   - IGL and MGL quote mmscmd. IGL FY26 = 9.39 mmscmd = **3,427 MMSCM** [V-snip: "3,427.21 million scm" — one news summary mislabels this as a Q4 figure; it is the full year].
   - Convert everything to mmscmd for the peer table.
10. **One-offs in the FY26 and Q1 FY26 CGD bases.**
    - IGL's FY26 includes a ~₹114 Cr one-time reversal of an OMC trade margin [primer].
    - MGL's Q1 FY26 base (EBITDA/scm ₹12.35) includes a one-time trade-margin item; another source restates it as ₹9.6 [V-snip Quartr vs indianchemicalnews].
    - **Q1 FY27 YoY declines at MGL (−39% PAT) are therefore overstated** vs the underlying trend. QoQ (+47%) is the cleaner read.
