# Luggage — primer issues

### From Luggage research (2026-10-25, luggage-industry.md §3.4)

- **safari-industries.html — rank wrong:** the primer calls Safari "India's second-largest branded-luggage maker … behind only VIP Industries". **FY26 consolidated revenue: Safari ₹2,047 Cr vs VIP ₹1,858 Cr** (screener; VIP AR), and Q1 FY27 ₹589 vs ₹578 Cr. Safari's own AR cites Euromonitor: **"No. 1 Luggage Brand in India" for the third successive year** (2025 data, value RSP). Rewrite the "clear #2" framing (snapshot, §02 chart "FY26 clear #2", §08, bottom line).
- **safari-industries.html — Carlton omitted:** there is no mention of the **18-Feb-2026 20-year Carlton licence** from Carlton Retail Pvt Ltd: 5% royalty (minimum ₹45 lakh); **₹99.5 Cr refundable deposit**; super-premium launch in FY27 (~Oct-26); initially imported from China. The brand-ladder table (§03) and the "Safari Select fights VIP's Carlton" line are now wrong: Carlton will be Safari's own top rung. VIP was restrained from using CARLTON in India (Delhi HC 1-Jul-2025; SC sell-off to 31-May-2026).
- **safari-industries.html — Samsonite brand list:** "Samsonite (Samsonite, American Tourister, Tumi, **Delsey**)". **Delsey is not a Samsonite brand** (it is an independent French company). Samsonite's group brands are Samsonite, Tumi, American Tourister, Gregory, High Sierra, Lipault, Hartmann and Kamiliant. Kamiliant (Samsonite's value brand) should be named, because MOSL puts Aristocrat + Kamiliant at ~30% of the mass segment.
- **safari-industries.html — BIS / anti-dumping claims unverified:** "Mandatory Indian quality standards for luggage" and "anti-dumping and duty actions on imported soft luggage". This session found **no luggage QCO and no luggage ADD**. Polymer QCOs (PP/PC inputs) were rescinded in Nov-2025. Remove the claims or source them.
- **safari-industries.html — market shares inconsistent with the VIP primer:** this primer gives VIP ~28% / Safari ~20% / Samsonite ~13%; the VIP primer gives 38% / 26% / 22%. Neither is sourced. Replace both with MOSL's CY24 segment shares (mass: Safari 16%; mid: VIP + Skybags 21%, AT 17%; premium: Samsonite 20%, Carlton 8%; top-3 ≈ 33% of the total market) and label the basis.
- **safari-industries.html — net cash missing:** "borrowings ~₹100–120 Cr" is correct gross, but the company holds **~₹346 Cr cash + investments → net cash ~₹230 Cr (Mar-26)**. Say "net cash".
- **safari-industries.html — Halol "early 1980s" / plant structure:** the AR shows 3 units — 1 in the parent (Halol GIDC) and 2 in the subsidiary **Safari Manufacturing Ltd** (Halol highway; **Jaipur, commissioned Q4 FY25**). Add Jaipur capacity (6.5 lakh pieces/month) and the standalone-vs-consolidated profit split (Q1 FY27 standalone PAT ₹28.9 Cr vs consolidated ₹47.8 Cr).
- **safari-industries.html — valuation stale:** m-cap ~₹7,250 Cr / ~44× → **₹6,006 Cr / 36.4×** (25-Oct-26). The stock is at its 52-week low (₹1,216; high ₹2,507).
