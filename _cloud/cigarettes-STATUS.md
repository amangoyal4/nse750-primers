## Forces
1. The 1-Feb-2026 tax reset: 40% GST on retail sale price plus specific excise by stick length (₹2,050–8,500 per 1,000 effective, ₹2,700–11,000 statutory, Notification 03/2025-CE = G.S.R. 955(E)); compensation cess ended; budgeted basic excise ₹45,780 → ₹90,810 Cr. GPI excise per stick ₹0.57 → ₹4.67.
2. Pricing power vs volume: price hikes of +43% to +51% on ITC packs, GPI's phased pricing, GPI volume −2% in Q1 FY27, the FY14–17 precedent.
3. Illicit trade (~26%, industry estimate) as the ceiling on price.
4. GPI as a Philip Morris hybrid: own brands + Marlboro supply to PMITPL (30.1% of standalone revenue) + leaf exports (about two-thirds to PMI affiliates, incl. Russia) + 24.8% of PMITPL (₹1,142 Cr profit, CY2025).
5. Leaf glut and governance/capital allocation (AP auction ₹248.86 → ₹209.83/kg; 2026-27 crop cut to 81 mn kg; Modi family pay ₹159 Cr; 27.4% institutional dissent).
