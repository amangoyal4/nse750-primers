## Forces
1. **Wood cost and the farm-forestry lag.** JK's delivered wood cost rose from ₹7,542/t (FY23) to ₹14,460/t (FY26). Wood explains about two-thirds of the per-tonne margin loss (labelled illustrative). Relief is expected in FY27–29.
2. **Imports and the trade-remedy toolkit.** Imports hit a record 2.05 mt in FY25. The ₹67,220/t minimum import price (MIP) on virgin multi-layer board now runs to 31-Mar-2027. DGTR recommended an anti-dumping duty (ADD) of US$261/t for RAPI, nil for APP/Indah Kiat and US$376/t for others. The Finance Ministry had not notified it by 23-Oct-2026.
3. **Board capacity wave and consolidation.** Domestic virgin-board capacity is 16.9 lakh t, run by six producers at about 100% utilisation. ITC bought Century's Lalkuan mill on 1-Aug-2026.
4. **Demand split.** Writing & printing paper (W&P) is about 7 mt, growing 2–3%. Packaging is about 65% of volume, growing about 8%.
5. **The cycle.** FY23 peak → FY26 trough → the Q1 FY27 turn (rupee, freight, the MIP floor, easing wood).
