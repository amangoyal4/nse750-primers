## h1
Indian travel services: <em>a toll, two marketplaces, a forex desk</em>

## Forces
1. IRCTC's toll stalls: e-booking penetration ~89%, ticket counts flat, RailOne/UPI fee tiers; growth shifts to low-margin catering; Railways policy risk (fee share, GSTAT order).
2. The 2026 West Asia war and a weak rupee (~₹94.9/$) hit outbound travel: Thomas Cook outbound B2C −20% in Q1 FY27; Gulf routes disrupted.
3. Airline capacity and fares: groundings and high fares lift GTV but compress per-ticket take (ixigo flight take 7.0% vs 9.4%).
4. B2B vs B2C economics: TBO goes global and upmarket (Classic Vacations), ixigo goes deeper into India (trains, buses, hotels).
5. Forex, float and resorts: Thomas Cook's high-margin forex (45.8% EBIT) and Sterling inside a low-margin travel business; composite scheme pending NCLT.
