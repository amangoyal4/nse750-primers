**Members (2, Tier C):** Asahi India Glass (ASAHIINDIA · 515030) and Borosil Renewables (BORORENEW · 502219).
- The two share a furnace, not a market. AIS sells **automotive safety glass plus architectural float glass**. Borosil sells **textured, tempered, low-iron solar glass** to module makers.

**Units, conventions and FX**
- ₹ crore (Cr) = 10 million. ₹ lakh = 100,000; the companies report results in ₹ lakh, so divide by 100 for ₹ Cr.
- **tpd** = tonnes per day of furnace **melting (pull) capacity**. Annual tonnage = tpd × 365 × utilisation. A 1,000 tpd furnace melts ~365,000 t/yr, but packed saleable output is lower after cutting/edge losses (see trap 4).
- **mt** = million tonnes. **sq m** = square metre. **GW** = gigawatt of modules (DC unless stated). MMBtu = million British thermal units.
- **Solar-glass price unit:** Borosil quotes realisation in **₹ per mm of thickness per sq m** ("₹160.3/mm"). A 2 mm sheet at ₹160.3/mm is ~₹320/sq m. Chinese prices are in **RMB per sq m for a given thickness** (e.g. 2.0 mm coated = RMB 8–10.5/sq m).
- FY26 = Apr-2025 to Mar-2026. Q1 FY27 = Apr–Jun 2026. Q2 FY27 = Jul–Sep 2026. Both companies have a March year-end.
- **FX: ₹94.9 = US$1** (19-Oct-2026; record low near ₹96.8). RMB 1 ≈ ₹13.1 [E, at ~7.25 RMB/$]. €1 ≈ ₹108–110 [E]. Earlier-period conversions are flagged.

## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **AIS's reported segments are "Automotive Glass", "Float Glass" and "Others", not "auto vs architectural".**
   - The Float Glass segment's **gross** revenue includes float sold internally to the auto plants. **Inter-segment revenue jumped from ₹95 Cr (FY25) to ₹516 Cr (FY26, standalone)** after Soniyana, a float plant "built specifically to supply the automotive segment" [V AR; V results].
   - So **float segment gross revenue (FY26 ₹1,754 Cr, +31.6%) overstates external architectural sales.**
   - The annual report's SBU split is different again: **Auto SBU ₹3,369.31 Cr; Architectural SBU ₹1,261.95 Cr** (FY26) [V AR].
   - The primer's "~60% auto / ~35% architectural / ~5% consumer" is roughly right on SBU revenue (67% / 25% / ~8% on ₹5,031 Cr consolidated gross revenue [E]). Quote the SBU split for "mix"; quote segment EBIT for margins.
2. **AIS standalone vs consolidated: close, but not identical.**
   - FY26 consolidated revenue ₹4,982 Cr / OP ₹910 Cr / PAT ₹345 Cr; standalone ₹4,668 / ₹865 / ₹330 Cr [V screener].
   - The difference is the consumer-glass/distribution subsidiaries ("Others": consolidated gross ₹803 Cr vs standalone ₹99 Cr) and associates.
   - The primer's "₹4,990 Cr revenue / ₹918 Cr OP" is ~₹8 Cr above screener, probably because it includes other operating income. Immaterial; use screener in tables.
3. **Borosil: standalone (India) ≠ consolidated (India + the German tail), and the primer conflates them.**
   - **FY26 standalone:** revenue **₹1,534.83 Cr**, EBITDA **₹491.68 Cr (32.0%, incl. other income)**, PBT before exceptional ₹390.71 Cr, **exceptional −₹359.78 Cr**, **reported PAT ₹20.74 Cr** [V Borosil Q1 FY27 PPT/results].
   - **FY26 consolidated:** revenue **₹1,555.84 Cr**, EBITDA **₹465.96 Cr (29.9%)**, exceptional −₹213.40 Cr, **reported PAT ₹127.40 Cr** (owners ₹129.08 Cr) [V].
   - **The primer's headline "₹381 Cr net profit (FY26, consolidated) after German one-offs" is wrong on two counts.** ₹381 Cr ≈ **standalone PAT before the exceptional** (₹20.74 + ₹359.78 = ₹380.5 Cr [E]). It also equals the standalone **TTM to Jun-26**, which screener shows as ₹381 Cr.
   - The primer's "₹1,608 Cr revenue (FY26, consol)" is screener's **standalone TTM to Jun-26**.
4. **tpd (melt) ≠ tonnes sold; tonnes ≠ sq m.**
   - Borosil: 1,000 tpd melt → **12.5 million sq m net production in Q1 FY27** [V call] → at ~2.0 mm × 2.5 t/m³ ≈ **~5 kg/sq m** ≈ **~63 kt per quarter ≈ ~700 t/day packed** → ~70% pull-to-pack yield [E].
   - Float lines yield higher (~80–85% [K]), but auto processing scraps more.
   - **Never multiply tpd × 365 × price.**
   - Borosil's own rule of thumb: **1,000 tpd ≈ 6.5 GW of modules a year; 1,600 tpd ≈ 10.5 GW** [V PPT]. The industry rule: **62 GW ≈ 11,000 tpd** (≈177 tpd per GW because of glass-glass/bifacial) [V PPT].
5. **"Exceptional" items sit inside screener's "Other income".**
   - **Borosil:** screener shows FY26 consolidated other income **−₹188 Cr** and standalone **−₹338 Cr**. These are the German write-offs, not operating losses. Screener's "OP" (₹440 Cr consolidated, ₹470 Cr standalone) **excludes other income**; the company's EBITDA (₹466 / ₹492 Cr) **includes** it.
   - Borosil's **Q4 FY26 PAT of ₹169 Cr exceeds PBT of ₹120 Cr** because of a **₹54.75 Cr tax credit** on the write-off [V PPT]. Do not annualise Q4 FY26.
   - **AIS:** labour-code past-service cost of ₹6.54 Cr standalone / ₹11.74 Cr consolidated in FY26 [V]; FY25 consolidated had a **+₹31.83 Cr** exceptional gain [V].
6. **Borosil's realisation includes a temporary fuel surcharge.**
   - Q1 FY27 realisation **₹160.3/mm** includes **~₹9.5/mm of fuel surcharge** levied from **10-Mar-2026** after the West Asia war. Management is "already reducing" it as fuel costs ease [V call].
   - Underlying price ≈ ₹150.8/mm, flat on Q4 FY26 (₹150.2/mm). FY25 ₹113.4/mm; FY26 ₹146.7/mm [V AR].
7. **Borosil's capacity history is muddled in public sources (and in our primer).**
   - Company version [V PPT]: first furnace **180 tpd (2010), rebuilt to 210 tpd**; second **240 tpd**; **SG-3 550 tpd (commissioned Q4 FY23, Jan-2023)** → India **1,000 tpd**.
   - GMB Germany: **300 tpd, rebuilt to 350 tpd in Q2 CY23**; group peak **1,350 tpd** in CY23–24; GMB −350 tpd from Jul-2025.
   - **SG-4 + SG-5 = 2 × 300 tpd = 600 tpd, ₹950 Cr, firing Dec-2026, both commissioned by Mar-2027** → 1,600 tpd.
   - The primer labels SG-1 = 240, SG-2 = 210 (swapped) and GMB = 300 tpd (pre-rebuild).
8. **Float glass trade defence is now an MIP, not (only) anti-dumping.**
   - **DGFT Notification 29/2026-27 of 18-Aug-2026** put **clear float glass 4–12 mm (HS 70051090, 70052990)** under "Restricted" unless **CIF ≥ ₹34,000/t**, for one year [V notification text].
   - That is separate from the ADD on Malaysian clear float. The AIS AR says a **2026 sunset review recommended continuing it for five years**; Malaysia was **85% of clear-float import volume (~361 kt), ~18% market share** [V AR, citing DGTR].
   - The 2015 ADD on Chinese float glass ($218/t) was last seen extended to Dec-2020 [V-snip]; its later status is unverified, so treat it as lapsed unless the writer finds a renewal.
   - **Solar glass** is a different HS/product scope: textured tempered, ≤4.2 mm, ≥90.5% transmission, one side >1,500 mm, under ADD to 3-Dec-2029 [V-snip; V AR].
9. **Market-share claims for AIS vary by definition.**
   - Primer: "~72% PV OEM share, ~65% all vehicles" (FY24-era Autocar Pro interview).
   - **FY26 annual report: "~80% market share in the Indian passenger car segment"** and elsewhere "~79%" [V AR].
   - Architectural: **~12% of the domestic architectural glass market; ~26% of value-added glass** (AR FY26; primer said ~22%) [V].
   - Use the AR's ~80% "passenger car" figure with its label. It is company-claimed, not SIAM- or ACMA-audited.
10. **"Borosil = India's only solar-glass maker" is no longer true.**
    - Borosil's own July-2026 deck: **domestic solar-glass capacity 2,600 tpd (≈18 GW)**, of which Borosil is 1,000 tpd. It rises to **~7,700 tpd (≈51 GW) by Mar-2027**, "a significant portion … for captive consumption" by integrated module makers [V PPT].
    - Gold Plus's Kanagala project included a **300 tpd solar line** [V ICRA Dec-2023]. Others (Vishakha, Emmvee, Waaree/Premier, Chinese-backed lines) are unverified this session — FLAG.

# PART 1 — INDUSTRY DOSSIER

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Two members, one technology, three end-markets.**
  - **AIS:** autos (~67% of SBU revenue) and buildings (~25%), plus consumer/other.
  - **Borosil:** solar modules (~100%).
  - **Combined FY26 consolidated revenue ₹6,538 Cr (+7.7%), OP ₹1,350 Cr (20.6%), reported PAT ₹472 Cr; m-cap ₹29,070 Cr on 22-Oct-2026** [E: sums of screener].
  - AIS is **76% of revenue and 80% of m-cap**.
- **FY26 → Q1 FY27 was a margin up-cycle for both, for different reasons.**
  - **AIS:**
    - Consolidated OPM 16% (Q1 FY26) → **23% (Q1 FY27)**; PAT ₹55 → **₹149 Cr** [V screener].
    - Drivers: **PV volumes** (domestic PV +28.3% in H1 FY27 after GST 2.0 [V SIAM]), **Soniyana float filling up** (float segment EBIT margin **9.5% → 21.5%** YoY [V results]), and the **MIP on clear float from 18-Aug-2026** (helps Q2 onward).
  - **Borosil:**
    - Standalone EBITDA margin 16% (FY25) → **32% (FY26) → 35% (Q1 FY27)** [V].
    - Price-led: the **ADD on Chinese/Vietnamese solar glass (effective 4-Dec-2024, $570–664/t, to 3-Dec-2029)** lifted realisation from ₹113.4/mm to ₹146.7/mm to ₹160.3/mm (incl. surcharge) [V].
- **The market is re-pricing both down despite the earnings.**
  - **Borosil** is at ₹396 (−45% from the ₹721 52-week high; −35% since 16-Jul results at ₹612) → **5.8k Cr m-cap, 15.7× screener P/E, ~10.5× TTM EV/EBITDA** [V screener; E]. The stated worry: **domestic solar-glass capacity tripling (2,600 → 7,700 tpd by Mar-27)**, the fuel surcharge unwinding, Chinese PV glass at a loss-making RMB 8/sq m [V SMM], and equity dilution. No single trigger was found — FLAG.
  - **AIS** is at ₹914 (52-week range 775–1,074) → **₹23,241 Cr m-cap, P/E 52× TTM / 67× FY26, EV/EBITDA ~24× TTM** [V screener; E]. It is still priced as a franchise.
- **India is short of glass at the margin in both businesses.**
  - **Flat glass:** demand ~**2.75 mt in 2026** (industry estimate) vs "potential" 3.8 mt [V-snip Angel One, Aug-2026]. Clear-float imports ~0.4 mt [E from AR: Malaysia 361 kt = 85%].
  - **Solar glass:** FY26 installations **45 GW AC / 62 GW DC** need ~**11,000 tpd** vs **2,600 tpd** domestic → ~75% imported [V Borosil PPT]. Even 7,700 tpd by Mar-27 covers only ~70% of today's demand.
- **Energy is the swing cost, and 2026 was an energy shock.**
  - LNG tender prices for India rose from **$16.8–18.8/MMBtu (Mar–Jun-2026)** to **$26.4/MMBtu (Sep-2026)** (Wood Mackenzie via Business Today, 13-Oct-2026) [V-snip]; Asian spot was ~$10 before Qatar's force majeure [V-snip].
  - GAIL/IOC cut industrial gas by **10–40%** in Mar–Apr [V-snip].
  - Borosil's power & fuel was **25.2% of standalone revenue in Q1 FY27** (₹102 Cr) vs 21.8% in FY26 [V results; E]. It passed this on through the surcharge.
  - AIS uses ~**13.5 lakh MWh** of energy a year (12.5% renewable) [V AR].
- **Soda ash is the input tailwind.**
  - Chinese soda ash is at decade lows: light ~**RMB 1,156/t** end-Jun-2026; dense FOB **$185/t**; Indian ~₹30/kg [V-sib commodity-chem].
  - Glass uses ~0.2 t soda ash per t of glass [K]. This is part of why AIS's float margin doubled. Cross-link `commodity-chem.html`: what hurts Tata Chemicals/GHCL helps glassmakers. The pending **soda ash safeguard (DGTR, final determination ~16-Nov-2026)** is a cost risk for glass [V-sib].

### 1.2 Value chains

1. **Float → auto (AIS model)**
   - Sand (silica ~70% of the batch), soda ash (~20% by weight, the costliest raw material), dolomite/limestone, sodium sulphate, cullet (recycled glass, **25–30% of input** at AIS) [V AR].
   - → furnace (natural gas/fuel oil/propane + electric boost) → tin bath → annealing lehr → cut "jumbo" sheets.
   - → **auto processing:** cutting, edging, screen-printing (black frit), bending, laminating (PVB) or tempering, encapsulation.
   - → JIT delivery to OEM lines (AIS has **11 sub-assembly units-cum-warehouses** near OEM clusters) [V AR].
   - Value is captured at **design-in** (the model-life contract), processing and logistics. Float is the low-margin feeder.
2. **Float → architectural.** Jumbo sheets go to processors and fabricators (many small unorganised processors) → toughening, DGU, lamination → builders and contractors. Value sits in **coated/value-added glass** and distribution reach (AIS: 1,424+ stockists, 79 WE cities, 151 dealers) [V AR].
3. **Solar glass (Borosil model)**
   - Low-iron silica sand (Fe₂O₃ < ~0.015% [K]) + soda ash + dolomite + antimony (Borosil has a patented antimony-free route) [V PPT] → furnace → **rolling** between patterned rollers → annealing → cutting → **tempering** → AR coating → packing → module maker.
   - Module makers buy glass by sq m per thickness. Glass is ~**10–15% of module BOM cost** in China [K, industry rule of thumb; FLAG]. In India it is higher given ADD-protected prices [E].

## 2. Size and growth

### 2.1 Flat (float) glass — India
- **Volume:** flat glass market ~**2.75 mt (2026)**; industry "potential demand" ~3.8 mt [V-snip Angel One 18-Aug-2026, citing industry estimates]. 2.75 mt ≈ **7,530 t/day** of consumption [E].
- **Value:** ~2.75 mt × ~₹30,000–35,000/t ex-works for clear float [E: MIP ₹34,000/t CIF; Chinese ~₹25,000/t] ≈ **₹8,500–9,500 Cr at the commodity-float level**. With processing (tempering, laminating, auto) the downstream flat-glass market is perhaps **₹25,000–30,000 Cr** [E; FLAG — no primary source found]. USD at ₹94.9: ~$0.9–1.0 bn at float level; ~$2.6–3.2 bn downstream [E].
- **Per-capita use:** India ~2 kg/person vs China ~10–12 kg and a global average ~6–7 kg [K, industry rule of thumb; FLAG].
- **Growth:** historic ~6–8% p.a. volume [K]. Capacity grew in lumps: **FY18–FY19 +43%** (which crashed prices) [V ICRA].
- **Imports:** clear-float imports ~**0.42 mt** [E: 361 kt Malaysia ÷ 85%] ≈ **15–18% of the market** (AR: Malaysia alone "18% market share") [V AR]. India's total glass and glassware imports ~**$2.1 bn** [V-snip Angel One].
- **Domestic capacity:** ~**10,500–11,500 tpd** of float [E, §5.1].

### 2.2 Automotive glass — India
- **PV volumes (SIAM, domestic wholesale):**
  - FY21 ~2.71 mn [K]; FY22 ~3.07 mn [K]; FY23 ~3.89 mn [K]; FY24 ~4.22 mn [K]; **FY25 4.30 mn; FY26 4.64 mn (+7.9%, record)** [V Outlook Business/SIAM].
  - **H1 FY27 2.64 mn (+28.3%)**; Q2 FY27 1.36 mn (+30.7%; UVs 68%) [V Autocar Pro/SIAM 15-Oct-2026].
  - PV exports Q2 FY27 2.29 lakh (−5.2%) [V].
- **PV production** (domestic + exports) is ~0.8 mn above domestic sales [K]: ~5.0–5.1 mn FY25 [K]; ~5.4–5.5 mn FY26 [E]. SIAM's PV production series was **not fetched — FLAG**.
- **Glass per car:** a standard car uses ~4–6 sq m of glass (windshield ~1.3–1.6 sq m, backlite ~1.0, 4–6 sidelites, quarter glass), ~**18–25 kg** [K; FLAG]. A panoramic sunroof adds ~1–1.5 sq m of laminated, tinted, high-value glass [K].
- **Value per vehicle:** AIS Auto SBU revenue ₹3,369 Cr ÷ (~5.4 mn PV production × ~80% share + CV/tractor/aftermarket) → roughly **₹6,000–7,000 of AIS glass per Indian PV** [E]. Premium SUVs with panoramic roofs and acoustic/HUD glass are ~2–3× a base hatchback [primer, illustrative].
- **Market size:** auto glass ~**₹4,500–5,000 Cr** [E: AIS ₹3,369 Cr at ~70–80% share (incl. aftermarket and CV), plus Saint-Gobain Sekurit, plus imports for luxury brands].

### 2.3 Solar glass — India

**Key demand mechanics**
1. **Glass content per car is rising faster than car volumes.**
   - Sunroofs feature in "over a quarter of cars sold in India" [primer, unverified]; AIS targets 1.5 mn+ sunroof glasses a year [primer].
   - UV-cut, acoustic laminated sides, HUD-ready windshields and ADAS camera-bracket windshields (optical-grade zones) add value [K].
   - **UV share of PVs is 68%** (Q2 FY27) [V SIAM], and SUVs carry more and bigger glass [K].
   - EVs carry larger glass roofs and IR-reflective glass [K]. AIS flags "EV and ADAS-linked glass demand" as its top opportunity [V AR].
2. **GST 2.0 (22-Sep-2025)** cut GST on small cars from 28% (+cess) to 18%, and larger cars to a flat 40% without cess [K]. SIAM credits it, with repo cuts, for the FY26 record and H1 FY27 +28% [V].
   - **Risk:** the RBI hiked to 5.50% on 7-Oct-2026, and the base effect from Oct-2026 onwards (post-GST surge) will be tough [E].
3. **Architectural glass is tied to completions, not launches.** The float cycle lags real estate by 2–3 years. The 2026 LNG shock and the flood of SE-Asian "Chinese transplant" imports hurt FY26 [V AR chairman].
4. **Solar glass demand = domestic module output, not installations.**
   - A solar farm built with imported modules uses no Indian glass. ALMM-I (modules), DCR rules and now ALMM-II (cells, from 1-Jun-2026) push assembly onshore [V PPT].
   - **Bifacial / glass-glass modules** need **two sheets** (front + back), roughly **doubling glass per W** vs glass-backsheet. The thinner 2.0 mm and moves to 1.6–1.8 mm partly offset this [V SMM; V PPT citing ITRPV 2025].
   - **Shake-out risk:** management warns module makers without cell capacity or on Mono-PERC "may not be able to continue" after ALMM-II [V call]. Customer credit and volume risk rise even as aggregate demand grows.

## 4. Supply side and cost structure

### 4.1 Furnace economics (both members)
- **Float line:** economic size **550–600 tpd** minimum (modern lines 800–1,000 tpd) [V ICRA]; build time **18–24 months** [V ICRA]; capex **~₹600–1,000 Cr per 600–1,000 tpd line** [E: AIS Soniyana "₹1,400 Cr earmarked" incl. processing V-snip; Borosil 600 tpd solar = ₹950 Cr V].
- **Campaign life:** float furnaces run **12–15 years** before a **cold repair** (rebuild of refractories) [primer, K]. Solar-glass furnaces need a rebuild **every 6–7 years** [V Borosil call].
- **Cold repair** = ~**75 days down + ~15 days to restart ≈ 90 days of lost output** (Borosil) [V call]; costs ~20–40% of a greenfield line [K; FLAG].
- **Cannot be idled:** fixed costs (energy to keep the tank molten, depreciation, labour) continue. In gluts producers keep producing, which crashes prices (China 2025–26).

### 4.3 Raw materials
- **Soda ash** (dense grade) ~0.18–0.22 t per t of glass [K]; the largest single RM cost.
  - China light ~**RMB 1,156/t (end-Jun-2026)**; dense FOB Qingdao **$185/t (Jun-2026)**; India Mumbai ~₹29.5/kg (Jun-2025), ~₹30–31/kg (Q1 FY27) [V-sib commodity-chem].
  - 2022 peak ~₹53/kg (May-2022) [V-sib E].
  - **MIP on soda ash ₹20,108/t** (since 2023) and a **DGTR safeguard investigation initiated 16-Mar-2026** (final ~16-Nov-2026) [V-sib]. A safeguard would raise glassmakers' costs; AGI Greenpac, AIS, Gold Plus and Borosil are natural opponents [E].
  - Rule of thumb: ₹5,000/t soda ash move × 0.2 t = **₹1,000 per t of glass** ≈ 2–3% of float realisation [E].
- **Silica sand:** float-grade from Rajasthan/Gujarat/UP; **low-iron sand** for solar glass is scarcer [K]. Gold Plus owns silica mines [V ICRA].
- **Cullet:** AIS 25–30% of input by weight [V AR]. Each 10% cullet saves ~2.5–3% of melting energy [K].
- **PVB interlayer** (auto laminates): imported mostly from Eastman (Saflex), Kuraray (Trosifol), Sekisui [K]; priced in USD, so rupee weakness to ₹94.9/$ raises cost [E].
- **Tin** (float bath; small consumption) and **antimony** (solar refining agent): Borosil's antimony-free process is patented [V PPT].

### 4.4 Energy
- **Fuel:** natural gas (pipeline/R-LNG), fuel oil and propane/LPG, often fungible (Gold Plus lines are fungible between gas and furnace oil [V ICRA]).
- **Energy intensity:** float ~**6–8 GJ/t** of glass [K]; AIS float energy-efficiency project data show **246 MMBtu/day of gas** saved on one burner project [V AR].
- **2026 shock:**
  - Asian spot LNG ~$10 → **$24–25/MMBtu** after Qatar's force majeure (early Mar-2026); ~$17–18 in May [V-snip moneylife/Equirus].
  - India LNG tender awards **$16.8–18.8 (Mar–Jun) → $26.4/MMBtu (Sep-2026)** [V-snip Business Today 13-Oct-2026, citing Wood Mackenzie].
  - Qatar = 40–45% of India's LNG; Petronet force majeure; GAIL/IOC industrial cuts 10–40%; CGD prioritised [V-snip].
  - Brent swung $72–85 in Jul-2026 [V Borosil call].
- **Pass-through:** Borosil levied a **₹9.5/mm fuel surcharge** (~6% of price) from 10-Mar-2026 and is now unwinding it [V call]. AIS has no explicit surcharge on OEM contracts (typically quarterly or half-yearly price resets with RM/energy indexation [K; FLAG]).
- **Power:** Borosil now meets **93% of power needs from renewables** (16.5 MW wind-solar hybrid, COD Mar-2026; plus 10 MW hybrid from May-2023 and 1.5 MW wind) [V call/AR]. AIS is at **12.5% renewable**, targeting 70% by 2030; it has **on-site green hydrogen at Soniyana** (INOX Air Products, 190 tpa plant, 95 tpa to AIS in phase 1; commissioned Mar-2025) [V AR; V-snip ICN].

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

1. **1984–2003: auto glass born inside the Maruti supply chain.**
   - AIS was formed in 1984 as a JV of the Labroo family, Asahi Glass and Maruti Udyog, and began production in 1987 [V screener profile; primer].
   - Float was dominated by Saint-Gobain (Chennai, from 2000 [K]), Gujarat Guardian (1990s [K]) and Float Glass India (AGC, Taloja — merged into AIS 2001–03) [primer].
2. **2008–2016: Chinese float and the first ADDs.** India imposed ADD on Chinese float ($218/t, 2015) [V-snip]. Gold Plus entered (Line 1, Jan-2009) [V ICRA]. Borosil started India's first solar-glass line (180 tpd, 2010) [V PPT].
3. **FY18–FY19: the float glut.** Domestic capacity +43% (Gold Plus Line 2 700 tpd, AIS Taloja ~550 tpd, HNG Float) crashed prices [V ICRA].
4. **FY21–FY23: the boom.**
   - Post-COVID autos and housing, the ADD on Malaysian float (FY2021), BIS (Apr-2022) [V ICRA].
   - AIS OPM peaked at **24% in FY22** [V screener]; Gold Plus OPBDIT 32.6% [V ICRA].
   - Borosil earned **38–39% OPM in FY21–22** (standalone) on a solar-glass shortage [V screener]. Borosil bought **86% of Interfloat/GMB (Oct-2022)** [V AR] at the top.
5. **FY23–FY25: China's solar-glass overbuild and the European collapse.**
   - The earlier solar glass ADD was discontinued in **Aug-2022** [V AR]. Chinese PV glass flooded India and Europe.
   - Borosil swung to losses: consolidated PAT −₹50 Cr FY24, −₹87 Cr FY25 [V screener]. GMB lost ~€0.9 mn/month [primer].
6. **Dec-2024 → FY26: the policy reset.**
   - Provisional ADD from 4-Dec-2024 (final May-2025): Borosil standalone EBITDA ₹181 → ₹492 Cr [V].
   - GMB insolvency 4-Jul-2025; Geosphere 22-Dec-2025 [V].
   - AIS commissioned Soniyana (FY26) and refurbished Roorkee float, which hit FY26 PAT through depreciation and interest (dep. ₹192 → ₹285 Cr; interest ₹128 → ₹204 Cr) [V screener]. QIP ~₹994 Cr; debt/EBITDA 3.17× → 2.15× [V AR].
7. **2026: energy shock, policy tightening, PV boom.**
   - West Asia war (28-Feb), Qatar force majeure, LNG at $24–26/MMBtu [V-snip].
   - MIP on clear float (18-Aug-2026) [V]; Malaysia CVD on solar glass extended (2-Jun-2026) [V]; ALMM-II live (1-Jun-2026) [V].
   - PV sales +28% H1 FY27 [V]; China PV glass at RMB 8/sq m (Jun-2026) with production cuts [V SMM].

## 11. Technology and disruption risks

1. **Thinner solar glass and ultra-thin (1.6–1.8 mm).** Fewer tonnes per GW (bad for soda ash and tonnage, neutral for ₹/sq m). Borosil already makes 2 mm fully tempered (a "world first" claim) [V PPT/SMM].
2. **Glass-glass / bifacial modules** raise glass per W (back glass replaces polymer backsheet). Positive for Borosil [V PPT, ITRPV].
3. **Perovskite/tandem cells** (later decade) still need front glass; possibly TCO-coated glass (higher value) [K].
4. **Auto:** ADAS camera zones, HUD windshields, acoustic laminates, switchable PDLC sunroofs, heated/IR-reflective and **glass-polycarbonate competition** in some roof applications [K; V AR mentions PDLC and illuminated sunroofs].
5. **EVs:** bigger glass roofs, lighter thin laminated glass for range [K].
6. **Furnace decarbonisation:** oxy-fuel, electric boosting, hybrid electric melters, hydrogen blending (AIS Soniyana green H₂) and EU CBAM [V-snip; K].
7. **Disruption to Borosil's model:** captive glass lines at integrated module makers. Most of the 2,600 → 7,700 tpd build is captive [V PPT], which can shrink the merchant market even as total demand rises.
8. **Disruption to AIS's model:** Chinese "transplant" floats in SE Asia exporting at ~₹25,000/t [V-snip], and Fuyao entering India (no plant yet [K; FLAG]).

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert leads with)

1. **Trade defence sets the price; the furnace sets the cost.**
   - Both clear float and solar glass are priced at **import parity**.
   - **Solar glass:** the ADD ($570–664/t, Dec-2024 to Dec-2029) took Borosil from losses to 32–35% EBITDA on the same furnaces [V].
   - **Float glass:** the MIP (₹34,000/t CIF, 18-Aug-2026, one year) and the Malaysian ADD (sunset review 2026) do the same job for AIS's float segment [V].
   - The moat is partly "rented": expiry, sunset reviews and circumvention via third countries are the existential variables.
2. **Indian PV volume × glass content per car (AIS).**
   - Domestic PV sales hit a record 4.64 mn in FY26 and rose +28.3% in H1 FY27 after GST 2.0 [V SIAM]; UVs are 68% of the mix.
   - AIS's ~80% share and rising content (sunroofs, acoustic, ADAS/HUD) mean auto revenue outgrows car volumes. The auto segment grew +12.4% in FY26 vs PV +7.9%, and +22.1% in Q1 FY27 [V; E].
3. **Energy and soda ash.**
   - Energy is ~20–25% of revenue for a solar-glass maker (Borosil P&F 21.8% FY26, 25.2% Q1 FY27) [V] and ~25–35% of float cost [K].
   - The 2026 LNG shock ($26.4/MMBtu Sep-26) was offset by **decade-low soda ash** (China RMB ~1,156/t) [V-snip; V-sib].
   - Renewable captive power (Borosil 93%) and green H₂ (AIS) are cost hedges, not just ESG.
4. **Lumpy capacity: the cycle is a capacity cycle.**
   - Float lines come in 550–1,000 tpd blocks with 18–24-month builds; FY18–19 +43% crashed prices [V ICRA].
   - Solar glass: 2,600 → 7,700 tpd in a year (Mar-27) [V PPT], plus Borosil's own +600 tpd. Watch merchant (non-captive) supply vs demand.
5. **China's overcapacity as a permanent backdrop.**
   - PV glass operating capacity is 78,685 t/day, with prices below cash cost (RMB 8/sq m, −24% in H1 2026) [V SMM]. Chinese float transplants operate in SE Asia [V AR].
   - "Anti-involution" production cuts (Jul-2026, −6,000 t/d) [V-snip SMM] could lift global prices and ease the pressure on Indian protection.

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Float/solar producer:**
`Revenue = tpd × days × utilisation × pull-to-pack yield × realisation per saleable tonne` (or ÷ kg/sq m × ₹/sq m for solar)
`EBITDA = Revenue − (batch RM/t + energy/t + conversion/t) × tonnes − fixed costs`

**Worked example 1 — Borosil India, FY26-like year [E from V inputs]**
| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 1,000 tpd × 365 = 365,000 t melted |
| Yield ~69% | ~252,000 t packed |
| Average thickness ~2.05 mm → ~5.1 kg/sq m | ~49 mn sq m |
| Realisation ₹146.7/mm × 2.05 mm ≈ ₹301/sq m | **Revenue ≈ ₹1,480 Cr** (reported ₹1,535 Cr incl. stock reduction, specialty) |
| Materials ~22% + P&F ~22% + employees 6% + other 18% | ~₹1,045 Cr |
| **EBITDA** | **≈ ₹490 Cr (32%)** (reported ₹491.7 Cr) |
| Depreciation ₹87 Cr, interest ₹14 Cr | PBT ≈ ₹390 Cr; PAT ≈ ₹290 Cr at 25% tax [E]; reported pre-exceptional ₹380 Cr (low tax: shield) |
| Sensitivity | ±₹10/mm ≈ ±₹100 Cr revenue and EBITDA (≈ ±20% of EBITDA) [E] |
| Sensitivity | +$5/MMBtu gas ≈ +₹60–80 Cr/yr P&F (~15% of EBITDA) [E: P&F ₹334 Cr, ~40–50% of it gas-linked] |

**Auto glass producer:**
`Revenue = vehicles produced × AIS share × glass value per vehicle + aftermarket replacements × ₹/job`

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
