## Forces
1. The film slate is the demand curve; language mix decides the multiplex share.
2. Fewer visits, more money per visit (ATP + SPH premiumisation vs affordability).
3. Fixed-cost operating leverage, disguised by Ind AS 116 (FY26 pre-Ind-AS EBITDA ₹968 Cr / 14.4% vs 33.4% reported).
4. Under-screening (6.8/mn, ~10,033 screens) and the capital-light / SMART pivot.
5. Windows/OTT bargaining and the policy wedge (GST ₹100 threshold, state taxes, Karnataka cap, convenience fees, CCI).
