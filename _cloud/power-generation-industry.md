### R8. Unsigned PPA/PSA backlog and relief package
- Khaitan & Co ERGO note on MNRE OM No. 238/10/2025-P&RA dated 25-May-2026 — [V] fetched https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/Ergo_MNRE%20OM_Time%20Relief%20Package_02062026.pdf: **44.8 GW** of RE capacity holds REIA LoAs with PPAs/PSAs unsigned as on 30-Apr-2026. Proposed One-Time Relief Package (not final): Category A (demand side, 90+30-day window) — one-time **100% ISTS waiver** (or existing waiver +25%) for projects signing PSAs within 3 months; vanilla-solar buyers may add 2-hr BESS with non-solar-hour tariff under Sec 62; ISTS projects may connect to STU network; deemed RPO/RCO compliance; deemed tariff adoption if SERC silent for 45 days. Category B (developer options): exit LoA retaining connectivity (fresh SCOD ≤24 months), swap LoA for another PPA (≤30 months), or penalty-free exit with return of connectivity BGs (default). Linked CERC suo-motu petitions 11/SM/2026 (06-May-2026) and 5/SM/2026 (15-Apr-2026). Phased ISTS waiver framework applies to projects commissioned until **30-Jun-2028**.
- Prior datapoints (search snippets, [K]): MNRE — LoAs >43.9 GW with no PSA as of 30-Sep-2025 (TaiyangNews); MoP Oct-2025 review directed REIAs to resolve ~42 GW; PTI Sep-2025 ~40 GW to be rebid; IEEFA/JMK earlier ~19 GW of SECI capacity awaiting PPAs, 63% manufacturing-linked solar, 78% held by Adani, Azure, ReNew, Greenko. SECI cumulative PSAs >60 GW (Jul-2025).
- Interpretation: The binding constraint on RE build-out has shifted from **capital and modules to offtake (discom appetite for daytime-only solar) and transmission**. Discoms now want firm/dispatchable (FDRE, solar+BESS) power. This favours developers with C&I/merchant routes and storage capability, and penalises "vanilla solar" bid-winners without PSAs.
