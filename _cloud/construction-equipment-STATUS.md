## Forces
1. CE demand follows the GROWTH of infra capex, not its level (Union capex +25–37% FY22–24 vs +4.2% FY26 RE; ICEMA FY26 1,36,995 units −2%, domestic −7%; NHAI awards 3,124 km; trough Q3 FY26).
2. Stage V (CEV-V, 1-Jan-2025, all power bands): 12–15% price step, FY25 pre-buy, FY26 dip (ACE CMCE units −19%). GST on CE has been 18% since Nov-2017 — GST 2.0 did not offset it.
3. China: 71% of crane imports (FY26 US41m), ~95% of heavy cranes, none in Hydras; DGTR crane ADD recommendation never notified; ACE–KATO JV.
4. BEML = three businesses in one PSU (M&C 41% / Defence 35% / R&M 24% of FY26 revenue; order book 71% R&M at Mar-26), 421-day cash cycle, ~₹6.7 Cr cash.
5. Two opposite financial models and the valuation paradox (BEML 86× TTM P/E, ROCE 7.7%; ACE 27×, ROCE 31.6%).
