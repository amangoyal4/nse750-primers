# Primer issues — houseware

## From Houseware research (2026-10-23, houseware-industry.md §3.4)

- **cello-world.html — writing-instruments growth wrong:** the "What's new" box, §03 bar chart ("FY25 ~230 → FY26 ~370, ~+60%") and bull case say WI grew ~60% in FY26. Company FY26 presentation: **WI ₹309 Cr (FY25) → ₹369 Cr (FY26), +19.7%**. The ~60% figure is the **Q4 FY26 quarter (+64%, ₹128 Cr)**; Q1 FY27 was +52%. Also the driver is mainly the **Cello brand added from Q4 FY26**, not only "the Unomax export ramp". (§2.3, §9.5)
- **cello-world.html — Wim Plast amalgamation omitted:** the composite scheme (Wim Plast + Cello Consumer Products into Cello World) was sanctioned by NCLT Ahmedabad on 14-May-2026 and became **effective 27-May-2026 (appointed date 1-Apr-2025)**; FY25 comparatives restated, NCI eliminated, ₹409.3 Cr "shares pending issuance"; allotment completed by Sep-2026. The primer, refreshed 14-Aug-2026, never mentions Wim Plast (the listed furniture subsidiary). Add to §05 timeline and §01 structure.
- **cello-world.html — promoter holding and capital history:** "around 75% after the IPO" and "did not need external capital" are wrong/misleading. Promoters held **78.06% after the IPO**, fell to **75.00% after a ₹737 Cr QIP (2024, MPS compliance)** and to **73.50% (Sep-2026)** after the Wim Plast share issue. The QIP is fresh capital — it is part of today's ~₹730 Cr net cash.
- **cello-world.html — Cello pen brand described as a "plan" to "reacquire … through CPIW":** the deal was done in **Nov-2025** (full control Dec-2025); the listed company **leases** the "Cello" WI/stationery trademark **from CPIW (promoter firm)**. State that it is a lease from the promoters, and that the inherited range was loss-making (WI gross margin fell to 47.8% in Q4 FY26, 53.8% in Q1 FY27 vs 58.8% FY24).
- **cello-world.html — steel-bottle disruption missing:** the single biggest FY26 consumerware issue — **imported Chinese steel bottles stopped after the BIS insulated-flask QCO (2024)**; steelware sales −25% in FY26; OEM sourcing cost 5–6 pp margin; 8 in-house lines at Falna; peak ~₹300 Cr. India's vacuum-flask imports fell from US$114.6 mn (2023) to US$14.6 mn (2025) [UN Comtrade]. The primer attributes the margin dip only to glass, furniture and input costs.
- **cello-world.html — glass vs opal utilisation conflated:** primer's "~60%" is the **Falna glassware** plant; **Daman opalware (25,000 tpa) runs at 80–85%**. Also note management's Nov-2025 guidance of "80% by Q4 FY26" was **missed** (still ~60% in Aug-2026). Add peak revenue (₹250–300 Cr) and peak EBITDA (28–30%).
- **cello-world.html — EBITDA basis unlabelled:** "~22.7% EBITDA" is the company figure **including ₹55 Cr of other income**; ex-other-income it is 20.3% (screener OP 20%). The stat strip should say "incl. other income" or switch to 20%.
- **cello-world.html — Q1 FY27 not incorporated despite 14-Aug refresh:** Q1 FY27 (filed 7-Aug-2026): revenue ₹526.7 Cr (flat), gross margin 52.4%, EBITDA ₹117.1 Cr (22.2%), PAT ₹73.4 Cr (−9%); price hikes 7–20%; gas +80%; WI +52%; online 16.3%; FY27 guidance withdrawn. The primer cites "Q1 FY27 earnings-call highlights" in its sources but none of these numbers appear.
- **cello-world.html — valuation stale:** m-cap ~₹8,365 Cr → **₹7,984 Cr**; P/E ~26× → **25.1×**; stock −44% over one year, near its 52-week low (₹314). Add net cash ~₹730 Cr (EV/EBITDA ~15×).
- **cello-world.html — competitor table error:** lists **"Cera (adjacent)"** as a glassware/opalware rival — Cera Sanitaryware makes sanitaryware/faucets/tiles, not tableware. Replace with Treo (Milton), Ocean/Pasabahce imports, Corelle, Luminarc, Chinese imports.
- **cello-world.html — brand licence specifics missing:** state that "Cello", "Unomax", "Kleeno", "Puro" are owned by **CPIW**; licence fee 0.5% of revenue (cap 1%) **waived from 1-Oct-2023** (royalty ₹7.8 Cr in FY23). This is the precise related-party fact behind the primer's governance paragraph.
- **cello-world.html — plants list incomplete:** omits **Haridwar (2 units)**; Cello has 14 facilities (Daman 8, Haridwar 2, Baddi, Chennai, Kolkata, Falna).
- **cello-world.html — channel mix unlabelled period:** the "GT ~71% / online ~16% / exports ~7% / MT ~5%" chart is **Q1 FY27**; FY26 full-year was GT 74.9 / online 10.5 / exports 7.9 / MT 6.7.
- **cello-world.html — "Milton makes houseware" comparison understates Milton:** Hamilton Housewares (Milton) had **₹2,314 Cr revenue in FY24** (CRISIL) — larger than Cello's consumerware segment. Add as the category leader.
- **cello-world.html — GST 2.0 and policy absent:** no mention that plastic houseware, glass/opal, vacuum flasks, pens and furniture stayed at **18%** while steel utensils and ceramic tableware fell to **5%** (22-Sep-2025); no mention of the opal ADD (China/UAE, to ~2027) or the 24-Sep-2026 DGTR recommendation on borosilicate glassware.
- **cello-world.html — stake-sale rumour:** add the Aug-2026 "no proposal at present" clarification and the 7-Oct-2026 volume clarification as a watch item (FII holding fell 5.52% → 2.03% in two quarters).


## From Houseware deep research (2026-10-23, houseware-industry.md Part 4 §4.5)

- **cello-world.html — segments:** the three "segments" are management verticals; the audited accounts have **one operating segment**. Label charts "vertical (company presentation)".
- **cello-world.html — stake-sale rumour specifics:** Livemint 4-Aug-2026 (Bain Capital); stock +18.6% on 5-Aug; company "not privy … speculative" (6-Aug).
- **cello-world.html — QIP and steel plant:** QIP ₹737.32 Cr (3–5 Jul-2024); steel-bottle plant object ₹105.25 Cr delayed twice (deadline 30-Sep-2026).
- **cello-world.html — guidance slippage list:** glass 80% by Q4 FY26 (missed), steel full-scale by Jul-2026 (slipped), FY27 +10–12% (withdrawn Aug-2026).
