# Tractors — primer issues

## From Tractors research (2026-10-23, tractors-industry.md §3.4)


- **escorts-kubota.html — market share direction wrong (§01 levers table and §05):** "Escorts Kubota held ~10.9% of the domestic market in FY26 (up from ~9.9%)". The company's own Q4 FY26 presentation shows **11.8% in FY25 → 10.9% in FY26** (Escorts domestic +14.9% vs industry +23.5%). The FY26 annual report concedes the share "remains way below our mid-term aspirations… the domestic industry expanded at a faster pace". Fix the number and the narrative ("ahead of the broad market" in §07 is also wrong for domestic; only exports outgrew the industry).
- **escorts-kubota.html — §07 "+15.7% … ahead of the broad market":** total volume +15.7% vs industry total +21.9% (domestic +23.5%). Escorts lagged.
- **escorts-kubota.html — industry size (§04, §07):** "crossed a landmark ~10 lakh … for the first time" — correct direction but understates: FY26 domestic was **11.60 lakh (+23.5%)**, total incl. exports **12.66 lakh**.
- **escorts-kubota.html — competitor share chart mixes bases (§05):** Sonalika 12.8% is FY25 FADA retail; TAFE 11.3% looks like FADA retail for the Massey brand only; on the TMA wholesale basis CRISIL puts **TAFE at ~18%** (9M FY26) — TAFE is a clear **#2**, not "#3–4 competing with Escorts". Escorts is **#4** on wholesale (behind M&M, TAFE, Sonalika). Relabel and name the basis.
- **escorts-kubota.html — greenfield numbers (§06, §10):** "₹3,000–3,500 Cr over 4–5 years (₹5,000 Cr+ longer) … export capacity by FY28–29". CFO (Aug-2026): **overall greenfield capex ₹2,000 Cr; FY27 ₹450–500 Cr on land; first commercial production 2029-30** (Feb-2026 call). The larger figure is the DPR's "indicative" number.
- **escorts-kubota.html — valuation stale:** m-cap ~₹32,500 Cr (Jun-26) → **₹29,390 Cr** (23-Oct-26; ₹2,627; 52-wk ₹3,999–2,550). "~15× reported, ~23× continuing" → screener P/E **20.7× TTM** (the RED quarter has rolled out of TTM; TTM PAT ₹1,383 Cr).
- **escorts-kubota.html — ROE 19% is flattered:** screener's 18.7% includes the ₹1,004 Cr RED gain; company standalone continuing ROE **11.8%** (FY26). Say so in the metrics table.
- **escorts-kubota.html — Nanda holding:** "~11–12%" → non-Kubota promoter group **13.97%** (Har Parshad & Co alone 9.59%) [AR FY26]. Kubota **54.07%** (not "~54%" via "share cancellations" alone — the Sep-2024 allotment of 13,79,493 shares under the KAI/EKI merger took it from ~53.5% to 54.07%; the earlier treasury-share capital reduction also helped).
- **escorts-kubota.html — Kubota-brand volumes:** the primer treats "Kubota-branded" tractors as a separate competitor ("CNH (New Holland), VST, Kubota-branded — niche") — since the 2024 amalgamation **Kubota-brand India tractors are inside Escorts' own numbers** (three-brand architecture). Remove Kubota from the competitor list.
- **escorts-kubota.html — Q1/Q2 FY27 missing (refreshed 10-Jul):** add Q1 FY27 (cons. revenue ₹3,207.6 Cr +28%, EBITDA 11.1% vs 12.9%, PAT ₹385.9 Cr; material cost 72.7%; ~5% commodity cost hit; exports −18.9%; share 10.5%); Sep-2026 volumes −16.7% (GST base, festive shift, weak monsoon); Q2 board 2-Nov-2026; 2026 monsoon ~87% of LPA.
