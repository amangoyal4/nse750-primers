# Primer issues — music-labels

## From Music Labels research (2026-11-01, music-labels-industry.md §3.4)

- **saregama.html — Pocket Aces date wrong:** says "majority-acquired in 2022" (§03 subsidiaries and the 2022 timeline entry). The deal was **announced Sep-2023**: 51.8% for ~₹174 Cr plus ₹15 Cr primary, with a second tranche of ~41% within 15 months at pre-agreed multiples [V-snip storyboard18/yourstory/exchange4media]. Move the timeline entry to 2023 and add the stake.
- **saregama.html — catalogue size understated:** "more than 150,000 songs" vs the company's **1,80,000+ songs** (Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 presentations).
- **saregama.html — EBITDA basis not stated (apples vs oranges with Tips):** the primer treats "music EBITDA ~63%" and "group adjusted EBITDA ~41%" as operating margins and compares them with Tips' 73% (§10, §09 table, Tips row). Saregama's adjusted EBITDA is **before the music content charge** (₹139.9 Cr FY26). Add: EBITDA after content charge ₹264.8 Cr (26.9%); music net margin ₹376.8 Cr (46%, +28%). Tips expenses 100% of content, so its 73% is after content.
- **saregama.html — Q4 FY26 music revenue basis:** "₹200.4 Cr, +19.2%" (from a press article) conflicts with the company's own segment chart, **₹242.9 Cr** (music = licensing + artiste management + retail). FY26 ₹814 Cr is on the company basis; keep one basis.
- **saregama.html — content-payback device uses the FY27 budget, not FY26 spend:** "₹325 Cr" is FY27 guidance midpoint; FY26 actual investment in content was **₹340.1 Cr** (₹235.4 Cr new music + ₹104.7 Cr catalogue purchases). Ratio still ~2.8×. Also note the cash-flow "spent on new content" line (₹265.3 Cr) and the unreconciled ₹186 Cr figure raised on the Q1 FY27 call.
- **saregama.html — films business status stale:** Yoodlee/film production is presented as an ongoing studio. Management announced (Feb/May 2026) a **wind-down of own film production**; film music will come via Bhansali Productions; video revenue fell 52% in Q1 FY27 "by design".
- **saregama.html — Bhansali terms incomplete:** add 9,960 CCPS, ~28% initial stake rising to 28–49.9% on conversion in 2028 (performance formula), option to 51% by 31-Mar-2030, and exclusive sale of all future Bhansali film music at a pre-agreed formula; first releases Q4 FY27 (*Love and War*, Jan-2027).
- **saregama.html — Q1 FY27 and guidance omitted (refreshed 20-Jul):** revenue ₹263.6 Cr (+27%), adjusted EBITDA ₹112.4 Cr (+69%), PAT ₹51.9 Cr (+42%), music ₹230.6 Cr (+39%); guidance music +20–23% p.a., music EBITDA 60–65%, ₹1,000 Cr content over FY25–27 tempering from FY28; 25–30% target share of new music; 60% of FY26 music revenue from post-2000 music.
- **saregama.html — live events understated:** "not yet financially material" — FY25 live revenue was **₹285.2 Cr** (Diljit Dosanjh tour, 24% of group revenue) and explains most of FY26's −16% revenue. Say so in §08.
- **saregama.html — valuation and ownership stale:** m-cap ~₹8,930 Cr → **₹10,578 Cr**; P/E ~44× → **46.4×**; promoter 59–61% → **60.84%**; add FII 11.88% (down from 16.82% a year earlier) and DII 9.36%.
- **saregama.html — net cash unquantified:** company treasury net cash **₹181.1 Cr (Jun-26)** after the Bhansali payment; FY26 FCF after content was negative (CFO ₹155.3 Cr − content ₹265.3 Cr). The "heavy net cash" description in §03 is now overstated.
- **saregama.html — platforms:** "JioSaavn (inside the Reliance-Disney media orbit)" — JioSaavn sits in **Jio Platforms** (listed 28-Oct-2026), not JioStar. Add the DSP shutdowns (Resso Jan-2024, Wynk Aug-2024, Hungama Apr-2025) that management cites for the FY25 slowdown.

- **tips-music.html — catalogue size:** "~30,000 songs" (cover, §02, §03, §08) → company now says **38,000+ songs in 25+ languages** (Q2 FY27 deck).
