# Primer issues — telecom-towers

## From Telecom Towers research (2026-11-14, telecom-towers-industry.md §3.4)


- **indus-towers.html — Airtel tower purchase misdated and overstated:** the timeline (2024) says "Indus also buys ~16,000 towers from Airtel/Hexacom", and §4 repeats "~16,000 Airtel/Hexacom sites". Per the Q2 FY27 Quarterly Report (note 4) the Business Transfer Agreement was signed **7-Feb-2025** and completed **24-Mar-2025** for **10,093 macro towers + 2,179 lean co-locations** (initially booked 10,380 + 2,226), consideration ~₹1,982 Cr (AR FY26 related-party note). Move it to FY25 (Mar-2025) and correct the count. Also, FY25's +29.6k tower additions include ~10.4k acquired towers.
- **indus-towers.html — competitor table wrong:** lists "Reliance Jio infra (Brookfield/Jio InvIT)" and "Summit Digitel / others — smaller; the market is effectively Indus + Jio's captive estate" as separate rows. **Summit Digitel is the Jio tower estate** held by Brookfield's InvIT, which also bought **ATC India (renamed Elevar) on 12-Sep-2024** and is now **Altius Telecom Infrastructure Trust: 258k+ sites, 315k+ tenancies, FY26 adjusted revenue ₹12,262 Cr, cash EBITDA ₹8,301 Cr** (Altius investor presentation, 11-May-2026). Altius is a near-equal by site count, not "smaller". ATC's exit is not mentioned anywhere in the primer.
- **indus-towers.html — FY25 EBITDA margin wrong:** the FY26-vs-FY25 table shows FY25 EBITDA margin "~59%*". Company FY25 EBITDA ₹2,08,447 Mn on ₹3,01,228 Mn revenue = **69.2%** (QR). The "~₹20,900 Cr" EBITDA figure is right; the margin is not.
- **indus-towers.html — contract terms:** "typically ~15-year, MLA-based contract" → contracts are **MSAs** (Master Service Agreements) with per-tower service contracts; management says new and renewed contracts are **minimum 10 years**, and the weighted average residual life is **5.89 years** with minimum lease payments receivable ₹1.19 lakh Cr (Sep-26).
- **indus-towers.html — "net cash… a rarity" needs the lease caveat:** net cash ~₹4,900 Cr is **ex-lease liabilities**; including ₹20,206 Cr of lease liabilities (Mar-26) net debt was ₹15,294 Cr (₹17,516 Cr at Sep-26; 0.97× LTM EBITDA). After the ₹3,693 Cr dividend, net cash ex-leases is ₹3,436 Cr (Sep-26). Add a line on Ind AS 116: reported EBITDA 55.3% vs **lease-adjusted EBITDA 42.7%** of revenue (₹13,863 Cr FY26).
- **indus-towers.html — energy pass-through described as neutral:** §1 calls energy "largely a pass-through". Indus's energy margin is **negative** (−3.6% Q2 FY27, −4.6% Q1 FY27, −4.8% Q2 FY26; ~−3.9% FY26). Add it, and the 2026 diesel shock (bulk diesel +₹22/litre on 20-Mar-2026; retail ₹87.67 → ₹95.20 in May-2026).
- **indus-towers.html — Jio renewal risk missing:** the primer says Jio is "a modest and reluctant tenant" but omits that **a major customer's master contract has expired** (identified on the Q1 FY27 call as Jio), that Jio is ~12–13% of revenue (MOSL), and that many 2016–17 contracts roll over late-2026–early-2027 (Jefferies). This is now the main bear point alongside Vi.
- **indus-towers.html — ownership:** "Airtel ~48–50%" → promoter (Airtel) **51.26%** (Sep-26; 51.03% Sep-25). FII 21.71%, DII 22.93%. Say Indus has been an Airtel subsidiary since **19-Nov-2024** (Ind AS 110).
- **indus-towers.html — customer mix can be quantified:** AR FY26 related-party note shows Airtel ₹19,583 Cr + fellow subsidiaries (Hexacom etc.) ₹1,411 Cr of GST-inclusive revenue → **~55% of revenue ex-GST** [E]. Replace "indicative skew, not disclosed" with this estimate (flagged as derived).
- **indus-towers.html — stale operating and market data (refreshed 02-Jul):** towers 2,64,500 → **2,70,480**; co-locations 4,28,000 → **4,40,153**; tenancy 1.62 → **1.63** (first rise in ~6 quarters, Vi-led); solar 42,400 → **49,001** sites; 5G BTS "500,000+" → **5,93,244**; P/E ~16× → **14.7×**; price ₹390–430 band → ₹396 (13-Nov), 52-week ₹356–482.
- **indus-towers.html — Q2 FY27 omitted:** revenue ₹8,515 Cr (+4.0%), core ₹5,441 Cr (+3.9%), EBITDA ₹4,638 Cr (54.5%; +5.2% ex write-back), PAT ₹1,798 Cr (−2.2%; +6.4% ex), FCF ₹1,377 Cr, +2,869 towers, +7,903 co-locations, 472 exits; first Africa revenue (Zambia, Uganda); Nigeria from Q3; dividend ₹3,693 Cr paid; **new CFO Abhishek Maheshwari (from 19-Aug-2026)**.
- **indus-towers.html — Africa milestones:** add board approval date (2-Sep-2025; stock −5% next day on payout worries), licences in all three markets (Q1 FY27), first deployments (Q2 FY27), Airtel Africa as sole anchor, and competition (IHS, Helios, ATC).
- **indus-towers.html — rent flat despite escalators:** explain why sharing revenue per co-location has been ~₹41k/month for five years (₹44,264 FY22 → ₹41,036 Q2 FY27): ~2.5% escalators offset by **renewal discounts** and **revenue-equalisation** drag (Q1 FY27 call). The primer implies escalators flow through.
- **indus-towers.html — dividend history wording:** "paused dividends for three years (FY23–FY25)" is right (screener payout 0% FY23–FY25); add that FY22 payout was ~47% and that there is **no formal minimum payout** — the board decides annually on FCF (Q4 FY26 call).
- **telecom-operators.html — consistent; one cross-check:** "Airtel took control of Indus in November 2024" matches (19-Nov-2024). Its Vi cash-waterfall line "tower and site lease payments ~₹10,500 Cr, mostly to Indus" is plausible against Vi ≈ 28–30% of Indus revenue (~₹9,000–9,800 Cr incl. energy) [E] — no change, but avoid implying all of it goes to Indus.
- **General:** neither the primer nor the operators report mentions **Altius** by name, the **ATC exit (Sep-2024)**, or the **RoW Rules 2024 (effective 1-Jan-2025)**.
