## Forces
1. The toll pool compounds (₹33,929 Cr FY22 → ₹70,278 Cr FY26) and WPI indexation turns from headwind to tailwind (April-2027 hike INDICATIVE 6.6–7.8%).
2. The award cycle is broken for builders but not for owners (~7,000 km FY26; NHAI 107 km in Q1 FY27; EPC group revenue −15.7%).
3. NHAI monetisation and InvIT recycling are the business model (₹1,71,065 Cr cumulative; NHAI debt ₹3.48 → ₹2.40 lakh Cr; IRB bids at 15–16% IRR and sells to its InvIT at 12–12.5%).
4. Rates are the valuation lever (repo 5.50% from 7-Oct-2026; 10y 7.2–7.4%; InvIT cash yields 8.5–10.7%, ~38% of DPU is return of capital).
5. Policy is the tail risk (WPI linking factor cut then reversed 8-Jul-2026; annual pass; MLFF/GNSS; plaza rules).
