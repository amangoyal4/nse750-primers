Indian pharmacy retail: <em>the chemist's counter goes corporate</em>

## Forces
1. Formalisation: ~8–12 lakh chemists → chains (11.6% of IPM 2023 → 17.4% / ₹72,832 Cr 2030E, IPA/Pharmarack); cluster density as moat.
2. Mix not markup: private label is the only big GM lever, with a trust ceiling — MedPlus PL fell 23.3% (Q4 FY25) → 20.0% (Q1 FY27) → 21.4% (Q2 FY27, re-based).
3. Labour and rent vs capped prices: employee cost 12.7% of income FY26; KA +60% / TG +25% minimum wages from 1-Jun-2026; Q2 FY27 GM 25.1% (−110 bps), op. EBITDA 4.8%.
4. Channel war: online ~3–3.5% of IPM (indicative), losses narrowing (Apollo 24|7 breakeven, PharmEasy FY26 loss ₹520 Cr), quick commerce; GSR 817 never notified, Madras HC status quo, AIOCD strike 20-May-2026.
5. The state as price-setter and competitor: NPPA 30%-of-MRP trade-margin cap on 137 cancer drugs (9-Nov-2026); Jan Aushadhi 20,149 kendras, FY26 sales ₹2,252 Cr.
