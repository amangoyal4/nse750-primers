## Forces
1. Two policies, one wall — BIS QCOs (still enforced; 12-Jun-2026 amendment S.O. 3037/3038(E) moves the legacy-stock deadline to 31-Jul-2027) + GST 2.0 (5% up to ₹2,500/pair from 22-Sep-2025) + ~38.5% customs → formalisation of a ~₹1.93 lakh Cr, ~35%-organised market (indicative).
2. Pairs versus price — Relaxo Q1 FY27 pairs −2.3%, realisation +9.9% to ₹166 after 15–18% hikes; Campus pairs +11.7% at flat ASP ₹674.
3. The margin that isn't there — Bata 20.6% post-Ind AS 116 vs 9.7% pre-116 (₹342 Cr; AR lease payments ₹364.2 Cr); Hush Puppies licence; parent fees ~₹92 Cr.
4. Redtape's model — ~96% traded goods, ~75% imported; ~79% of other income is marketplace/supplier rebates; inventory; Sep-2025 IT search with auditor emphasis of matter.
5. Premiumisation into the GST ₹1,000–2,500 sweet spot (Campus), plus channels, 2026 polymer/wage cost shock, exports/US tariffs and the valuation reset vs Metro Brands.
