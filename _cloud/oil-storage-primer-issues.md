# Primer issues — oil-storage

### From Liquid & Gas Terminals research (2026-11-13, oil-storage-industry.md §3.4)

- **aegis-vopak-terminals.html — FY25 not restated; growth overstated:** the revenue chart shows FY25 ₹621 Cr and PAT ₹127 Cr. The company restated FY25 to **₹789.2 Cr revenue / ₹224.8 Cr PAT** after the common-control consolidation of Hindustan Aegis LPG (presentation footnote). So "FY26 revenue +~49%" should read **+17%**. The FY23–FY24 bars (₹356/₹570 Cr) are **total income**; revenue was ₹353.3/₹561.8 Cr. Fix the chart and the snapshot sentence.
- **aegis-vopak-terminals.html — LPG capacity wrong:** "~2.78 lakh MT static LPG" (stat strip, §01, §04) is not a company figure. Company: **225,800 MT** today (Kandla 48,000; Pipavav 70,800; New Mangalore 82,000; Haldia 25,000) and **355,100 MT incl. announced JNPA (129,284 MT)**. Ammonia 36,000 MT is separate.
- **aegis-vopak-terminals.html — capacity multiples mislabelled:** "liquid 3.75×, LPG 4.5× since the JV" — the company's charts give **3.84× and 5.3× including announced capacity** (vs a Nov-2021 base of 556,000 cbm / 67,000 MT). On installed capacity only: ~3.0× and ~3.4×.
- **aegis-vopak-terminals.html — port table errors:**
  - **Kochi is a liquid terminal** (82,545 cbm petroleum products; +49,577 cbm under way, early FY28), not "LPG storage serving the southern cooking-gas market".
