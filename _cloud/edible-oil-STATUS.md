## h1
Indian edible oils: <em>a refining business run by customs</em>

## Forces
1. The crude–refined duty differential (~19 pts since May-2025, kept by the 24-Sep-2026 cut, Notification 31/2026-Customs); refined imports down to 5%; Nepal/SAFTA 682 kt = 92% of refined imports.
2. Inventory and hedging swing the P&L more than demand (EBITDA/t ±30–50%; Q1 FY27 gains, Q3 FY27 loss risk).
3. ~56% import dependence: Indonesia/Malaysia/Argentina/Black Sea, B50 (live 1-Jul-2026), rupee ~₹94.9/$, West Asia freight.
4. Value migrating to adjacencies (Patanjali FMCG/HPC + oil palm; AWL foods; Gokul biodiesel/exports).
5. Working-capital/LC financing as the hidden balance sheet.
