**Forces:**
1. Crude price and the 2026 West Asia shock: the Indian basket averaged $120.09 in Oct-26 to the 21st (PPAC), and the rupee is at ~₹94.9/$.
2. The state's claw-back toolkit: subsidy sharing 2003–15 (ONGC ₹56,384 Cr in FY14), SAED 2022–24 (₹27,618 Cr paid by the two companies), the APM gas cap ($6.75 → $7.00), royalty plus cess (19.7% of ONGC revenue), and dividends. In 2026 there was no crude windfall tax; the government levied a fuel-export duty and cut excise instead.
3. The volume problem: domestic decline vs KG-98/2 (~21k bpd; gas 1.5–1.7 MMSCMD), Mumbai High with bp, and OIL's growth. RRR is 1.17 for ONGC and 1.02 for OIL.
4. The two-layer consolidation trap: in Q1 FY27 the E&P layers earned ₹19,904 Cr while the subsidiary layers lost ₹9,323 Cr (HPCL −₹12,265 Cr).
5. Policy reset and capital allocation: OALP/HELP, the Oilfields Amendment Act 2025, PNG Rules 2025, Samudra Manthan ₹84,084 Cr, dividends and capex.
