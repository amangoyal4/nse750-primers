**Bases and conventions (read first)**
- "Screener EBITDA" means screener.in "Operating Profit" (Sales − Expenses), which excludes other income. Company "EBITDA" is labelled separately. Nuvoco's company EBITDA, for example, is struck on *total income*, so it includes other income.
- Net debt (screener basis) = Borrowings − Investments. This ignores bank deposits and cash held outside "Investments", so I also give the company-reported net debt wherever the company discloses it, and I use the company figure for EV.
- EV = market cap (13 Oct 2026) + the latest company net debt (basis stated). EV/EBITDA uses FY26 screener EBITDA, with TTM (Jun-26) in brackets.
- Market caps and prices are screener.in's "Market Cap" and "Current Price" on 13 Oct 2026.
- Shareholding: screener showed the **Sep-2026** quarter only for Birla Corporation. For the other four, the latest pattern on screener was **Jun-2026**, because the Sep-26 filings were not yet reflected.
- **Q2 FY27 (Sep-26 quarter): no company in this batch has reported.** Every screener quarter table ends at Jun-2026. Nuvoco has dated its results to **Thu 22 Oct 2026**, with the concall on 23 Oct [V: BSE letter dated 9 Oct 2026, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=904039a0-8982-4200-b54e-6ecdf15638d7.pdf]. Prism Johnson's trading window closed on 1 Oct "until 48 hours after Q2 FY27 results" [V: BSE letter dated 25 Sep 2026]. The other three have closed trading windows, but I found no board-meeting date in the screener announcement list.
- Industry context: GST on cement fell from 28% to 18% from 22 Sep 2025 (56th GST Council, 3 Sep 2025) [K: CBIC notification]. The West Asia conflict from Feb-2026 sent petcoke to ~US$160/t CFR before it eased to ~$134/t, and coal (G7) to ~₹4,628/t [V: Nuvoco Q1 FY27 presentation chart, citing Argus and the Ministry of Coal]. ### 1h. Story bullets
- **The margin reset is the story.** EBITDA/t rose from ~₹717 to ~₹922 in FY26 and to ~₹1,079 in Q1 FY27, on cost work (fuel ₹1.52/Mcal, AFR, zero petcoke at Arasmeta) plus premiumisation (43% of trade).
- **Westward pivot.** Vadraj gives Nuvoco its first Gujarat and coastal (jetty) platform. Surat came in ahead of schedule. Kutch clinker is the next milestone, in H2 FY27.
- **Deleveraging is now slow.** All-in net debt is still ~₹4,450–4,600 Cr (~2.4× EBITDA), because Vadraj absorbed the FY26 cash. Interest cost is falling, however: ₹398 Cr in FY26 against ₹496 Cr, and ₹70 Cr in Q1 FY27.
- **Cheap on assets, mid on earnings.** EV/t is ~₹5,900, about half of replacement cost. EV/EBITDA is ~8.5× and P/E ~27.5×.

### 1i. Risks
Fuel-price spikes (West Asia) and INR at ~₹95/$. Election and labour disruption in the East. Execution of the Kutch restart (an asset idle for years). Gujarat price wars with UltraTech, Adani and Shree. A thin PAT base: tax was 40–42% in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27. GST show-cause notices.
