## The forces
1. The deposit war — SA share of system deposits 34.6% (Mar-22) → 28.7% (Mar-26); TDs 55.2% → 61.6%; HDFC CASA ~44% pre-merger → 31.6% (Sep-26); FCNR(B) swap window inflates Q2 FY27.
2. Rate-cycle whiplash — 125 bp of cuts (Feb–Dec 2025) compressed EBLR-heavy, CASA-rich banks (Axis −34 bp, HDFC 3.26% record low) and widened TD-funded/fixed-rate lenders (KVB +48 bp, Federal +39 bp); first hike to 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026 starts the reversal.
3. Credit cycle turning — MFI PAR 31–180 6.3% → 2.0%; provisions falling at IDFC/Bandhan/RBL/Yes; ECL from 1 Apr 2027.
4. Ownership & leadership reset — ENBD 60% of RBL, SMBC ~24.99% of Yes, Blackstone 9.99% of Federal, Fairfax reported for IDBI; new CEOs at HDFC (Bagchi, 27 Oct 2026), Kotak (Saha, 1 Jan 2027), IndusInd, Yes; HDFC chairman resignation.
5. Two-speed sector — ICICI RoA 2.49% vs HDFC ~1.8% vs Yes 0.9%; southern old-private banks (TMB, KVB) at 2.1%+.
