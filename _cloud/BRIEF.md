## 2. Phase 1 — research dossier → `_cloud/<slug>-industry.md` + `_cloud/<slug>-companies-<n>.md` (via subagents, see §1)
Sourcing: every hard number carries its source inline (publisher + year, URL where possible); tag [V] verified this session (fetched), [K] known from memory with source named, [E] estimate/composite. Prefer primary sources (ministries, regulators — RBI/SEBI/CEA/DGTR/MoRTH etc., industry bodies, rating agencies CRISIL/ICRA/CARE/India Ratings, company presentations and concall transcripts, reputable press). Where sources conflict give both and say which is more credible. Budget ~35–45 searches/fetches for tier A (industry + every company), then write.
Cover (checklist, not headings): what the sub-sector does and the value chain, where value is captured; size and growth (₹ and USD, CAGR, domestic vs export, India's global share); demand drivers with numbers; supply side and cost structure, capacity, utilisation, a "where ₹100 of revenue goes" breakdown ([E] if composite); industry structure, concentration, unlisted players/MNCs, imports and China; business-model archetypes and how their economics differ; regulation and policy; history and cycles with dates; native KPIs with definitions, formulas, good/average/weak ranges; global lens; technology/disruption; what to watch.
Also: A the 3–5 defining forces with reasoning; B 2–4 deep-dive themes; C the revenue/profit equation with a worked ₹ example; D a 6-cell stat strip in ₹ ONLY with sources; E 8–12 chart-ready datasets; F KPI glossary with ranges; G a 15–25-event timeline; H figures you could not source.
Company packs (one per member, in the company-pack files): from screener.in (`https://www.screener.in/company/<NSE>/consolidated/`, fall back to standalone): FY26 revenue, prior year and growth, EBITDA and margin, PAT, ROCE, ROE, borrowings, cash/investments, net debt/cash, m-cap (today, with date), P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA; Q1 FY27 (Jun-26 quarter; check whether Q2 FY27 is out); segment mix; capacities; ownership (latest shareholding); concentration; capex/expansion; guidance; 3–4 story bullets. **Lenders** (banks/NBFCs/HFCs): loan book/AUM and growth, deposits & CASA, NII, NIM, cost of funds, opex/assets, credit cost, GNPA/NNPA/PCR, PAT, ROA, ROE, CRAR/CET1, LDR, m-cap, P/B, P/E.
Data-quality lessons: screener net cash often misses bank deposits — show the company figure too; 100 − material cost % is only a gross-margin proxy; screener operating profit can differ from company EBITDA (other income, one-offs) — state the basis; use the latest shareholding; confirm each company is still listed and flag any merger/demerger; m-caps in primers are stale — always re-pull.
End with: a COMPANY-KPI APPENDIX (summary grid, aggregates with stated basis), and a **PRIMER ISSUES** section: for each member primer, read it (cover strip, "whatsnew" box, first sections, then grep for key figures) and list every error or stale figure vs your verified research (wrong numbers, wrong names, stale m-cap/P/E/ownership, missing major events, wrong regulatory status).
