## The forces
1. Consolidation into a duopoly-plus: UltraTech (200.1 MTPA domestic / 205.5 MTPA global) and Adani (109 MTPA) together hold ~43% of an estimated ~720 MTPA.
2. The capacity race holds utilisation near ~70%, caps price and keeps EBITDA/t below the decade average.
3. Fuel and freight decide cost/t: they are 45–55% of costs, the 2026 petcoke shock added ₹150–300/t, and green power and shorter lead distances are the offsets.
4. Demand comes from housing and government infrastructure, 85–90% of the total.
5. Policy has turned: GST cut from 28% to 18% (22 Sep 2025); the SC mining-levy ruling (Jul 2024) followed by MMDR Amendment 2026 Section 9D; carbon-credit targets.
