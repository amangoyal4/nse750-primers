## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **Billings (collections) vs revenue: the single most important trap in this sub-sector.**
   - All four members sell **prepaid subscriptions/packages** (Naukri Resdex/job postings, 99acres builder packages, IndiaMART Silver/Gold/Platinum, Justdial campaigns). Cash is collected up front and recognised over 3–12+ months.
   - Info Edge reports **"billings"**; IndiaMART and Justdial report **"collections from customers"**; all three disclose **deferred revenue** (contract liabilities). CarTrade's model (ads, leads, auction commissions) has very little deferral.
   - **Billings lead revenue by 1–3 quarters.** Example: Info Edge FY26 standalone billings ₹3,177 Cr (+10.3%) vs standalone revenue ₹3,052 Cr (+15%). The revenue growth was the tail of FY25 billings [primer; V-snip scanx "FY26 billings ₹31,775 Mn"]. Q1 FY27: billings +14.4%, revenue +12.0% [V co-PPT]. The cycle is **turning up** in billings first.
   - IndiaMART's **collections are seasonal** (Q4 is the biggest quarter: Q4 FY26 collections ₹595 Cr vs Q1 FY27 ₹463 Cr) [V co-PPT]. Always compare year on year, never quarter on quarter.
   - **Basis trap:** IndiaMART reports collections and deferred revenue on both a **standalone** basis (FY26: collections ₹1,674 Cr, deferred ₹1,832 Cr) and a **consolidated** basis (incl. Busy: ₹1,857 Cr and ₹1,965 Cr) [V co-PPT]. Our primer pairs **standalone** collections (+10%) with **consolidated** revenue (+13%) to argue that "collections are lagging revenue". On one basis the claim is wrong: consolidated collections grew **+14.2%** (₹1,626 → ₹1,857 Cr) against consolidated revenue +13.0%; standalone collections grew +9.7% against standalone revenue +9.3% (₹1,320 → ₹1,443 Cr) [E from V co-PPT]. See the Primer issues.

2. **Info Edge consolidated ≠ Info Edge's business. Three layers:**
   - **Standalone** = the operating classifieds (Naukri, 99acres, Jeevansathi, Shiksha) plus treasury. Q1 FY27 standalone revenue ₹824.5 Cr, operating PBT ₹333.9 Cr (40.5%) [V co-PPT].
   - **Consolidated** adds the acquired businesses (Zwayam, DoSelect, Aisle, Job Hai), the **Info Edge Ventures AIF funds** (with external LPs, including the Temasek JV), and the equity-accounted associates/JVs (Info Edge's share of their losses). Consolidated "net sales" ₹881 Cr in Q1 FY27 [V co-PPT].
   - **Consolidated net profit includes non-controlling interests** (outside LPs' share of fund gains). Screener's P/E of 52.2× on ₹76,935 Cr implies **TTM profit attributable to owners of ≈₹1,474 Cr**, against a headline TTM consolidated net profit of ₹1,910 Cr. FY26: headline ₹1,763 Cr vs **attributable ≈₹1,450 Cr** (EPS ₹22.36 × ~65 Cr shares) [E from V screener]. **Use attributable figures for P/E.**
3. **Info Edge's Eternal and PB Fintech marks go through OCI, not the P&L.**
   - The FY26 fair-value gain on Eternal (+₹3,296 Cr) and the loss on PB Fintech (−₹1,472 Cr) were taken to **other comprehensive income** [V co-PPT note 7]. So were Q4 FY26 (Eternal −₹15,874 Cr, PB −₹2,287 Cr) and Q1 FY27 (Eternal +₹4,273 Cr, PB +₹1,154 Cr). Q1 FY27 **total comprehensive income was ₹5,149 Cr** against PAT of ₹490 Cr [V co-PPT].
   - So the primer's line that "reported profit is a mirage … swollen by non-cash fair-value gains on Eternal/PB" is **only partly right**. P&L "other income" (₹1,099 Cr FY26; ₹572 Cr in Q4 FY26) is **treasury interest/MF gains plus FVTPL marks on the fund/AIF portfolio**. The two big listed stakes do not move PAT unless sold (or on a deemed disposal, as in FY22, when the Zomato IPO produced a ₹12,882 Cr PAT).
   - Search-press conflict: one secondary summary gave the Q4 FY26 combined OCI loss as "₹8,161 Cr". The company note gives **₹18,161 Cr** (₹15,874 + ₹2,287 Cr). **Use the filing figure.**
4. **Exceptional items in FY26–Q1 FY27 (labour codes and deemed gains).**
   - The new labour codes (effective 21-Nov-2025) changed the definition of "wages" for gratuity, so companies booked one-time past-service costs.
   - **Justdial:** ₹21.1 Cr exceptional in Q3 FY26 [V co-PPT].
   - **CarTrade:** ₹6.5 Cr in FY26 plus ₹3.1 Cr in Q1 FY27 [V co-PPT].
   - **Info Edge:** a ₹27.7 Cr gratuity plus ₹6.6 Cr leave-encashment impact over FY26, with a partial reversal in Q4 [V co-PPT note]. Info Edge also had a **+₹25.1 Cr exceptional MTM gain** on the Makesense–PB Fintech merger (Q2 FY26).
   - **IndiaMART:** labour-code impact not captured; its "Others" line includes exceptional items [V co-PPT footnote]. Flag.
   - All screener PAT figures below are **after** these items. Pre-exceptional PAT ≈ reported + 0.75 × the charge [E]. The differences are small (<5%) except at the quarterly level.
5. **Other (treasury) income is a large share of profit at three of the four members.**
   - FY26 other income as a share of PBT [E from V screener]: **Info Edge 52%** (inflated by fund MTM), **Justdial 51%**, **IndiaMART 31%**, **CarTrade 29%**. All four: ₹1,700 Cr of ₹3,675 Cr PBT = **46%**.
   - Treasury income is **mark-to-market on debt funds/bonds**, so it swings with bond yields. Justdial Q1 FY27 other income ₹131.5 Cr (yields −44 bp) vs Q2 ₹54.6 Cr (yields +57 bp) [V co-PR]. IndiaMART Q4 FY26 other income was **−₹34 Cr** (an FV loss) [V co-PPT], which drove the "PAT −72%" headline.
   - **Always split operating PBT from treasury.** Justdial publishes "Operating PBT" (Q2 FY27 ₹80.0 Cr). Info Edge publishes "Operating PBT" (standalone Q1 FY27 ₹333.9 Cr).
6. **EBITDA definitions differ by company. Never mix them in one column.**
   - **Screener OP** (revenue − operating expenses) is the common basis in this file's tables.
   - **IndiaMART "EBITDA"** FY26 ₹530 Cr (34%) vs screener OP ₹475 Cr (30%). The gap of ~₹55 Cr is probably classification (e.g. some cost lines screener treats as operating) [E]. IndiaMART's standalone "Adjusted EBITDA" (₹555 Cr, 38%) adds back ESOP cost [K, label in deck].
   - **CarTrade "EBITDA"** FY26 ₹257.0 Cr (33%) ≈ screener OP ₹258 Cr. But **CarTrade "Adjusted EBITDA" ₹363.0 Cr (42%) adds back other income and ESOP cost** [E: ₹363.0 − ₹257.0 = ₹106 Cr ≈ other income ₹90.5 Cr + ESOP ~₹15 Cr]. Management headlines and some press quote the adjusted figure. **Do not compare CarTrade's 42% "adjusted" margin with IndiaMART's 34%.**
   - **Justdial "Operating EBITDA"** FY26 ₹357 Cr (29.5%) ≈ screener ₹358 Cr.
   - **Info Edge** reports **"operating PBT"** (after depreciation, before other income), not EBITDA. Screener consolidated OP FY26 ₹1,186 Cr (36%).
7. **CarTrade: PAT ₹244 Cr vs ₹223 Cr is a minority-interest issue, not a "basis" mystery.**
   - CarTrade owns ~55% of **Shriram Automall India Ltd (SAMIL)**, the remarketing arm [K: CarTrade raised its stake from ~51% to ~55% over 2021–23; VERIFY]. Consolidated PAT ₹243.5 Cr (FY26) includes the outside shareholders' ~45% of SAMIL's PAT.
   - **Attributable PAT ≈ ₹222–226 Cr** (EPS ₹46.58 × ~4.8–4.86 Cr weighted/period-end shares) [E from V screener]. The primer's unexplained "~₹223 Cr on a slightly different basis" is exactly this. In Q1 FY27 the deck shows PAT ₹56.8 Cr while press quoted "consolidated net profit ₹51.2 Cr" [V-snip sahi]: the attributable figure.
   - **Use attributable PAT for P/E** (screener's 57.6× on ₹13,711 Cr implies ~₹238 Cr TTM attributable).
8. **Segment numbers at CarTrade are pre-elimination and include other income.**
   - The deck's segment revenue (Consumer ₹308.3 Cr, Remarketing ₹259.3 Cr, OLX ₹217.6 Cr; sum ₹785.2 Cr) slightly exceeds consolidated revenue (₹779.3 Cr).
   - Segment PAT includes each subsidiary's treasury income (OLX FY26 other income ₹26.3 Cr against PAT ₹82.0 Cr; OLX FY26 PAT also benefited from a ₹3.4 Cr deferred-tax credit) [V co-PPT].
   - "OLX PAT +77%" is **flattered by treasury income and tax credits**. OLX segment **EBITDA** (₹67.1 Cr FY26, 31%; Q1 FY27 ₹18.1 Cr, +76%) is the cleaner metric.
9. **"Paying suppliers" (IndiaMART) and "paid campaigns" (Justdial) are different units.**
   - IndiaMART: **paying subscription suppliers** (unique businesses); 218K at Jun-26. ARPU = quarterly revenue ÷ period-end suppliers × 4 (₹69K annualised) [V co-PPT footnote].
   - Justdial: **active paid campaigns** (a business can hold more than one; some are monthly ECS plans); **656,520** at Sep-26. Implied revenue per campaign ≈ ₹20.9K/yr (Q2 annualised ₹1,371 Cr ÷ 6.57 lakh) [E].
   - Info Edge: **billed customers** in Recruitment India B2B (~146K FY26, up ~14%, with realisation per customer slightly lower; chart values partly garbled in the PDF text) [V co-PPT, approx.].
   - **None of these is comparable across companies.**
10. **"Traffic" metrics are self-reported and inconsistently defined.**
    - Justdial "unique visitors" aggregate voice, desktop and mobile and "may not be mutually exclusive" [V co-PR note]. Q2 FY27: 198.4 Mn per quarter.
    - CarTrade quotes "~80 Mn MAU" (CarWale + BikeWale + CarTrade ~47 Mn; OLX India ~32 Mn; Q1 FY27 monthly average) [V co-PPT]. The Spinny press release separately cites "23 Mn consumers annually" for used-car buying/selling on OLX + CarWale [V-snip].
    - 99acres reports **time-share vs competitors** (SimilarWeb): web 49%, app 55%, iOS 69% (Q1 FY27) [V co-PPT].
    - IndiaMART reports **active buyers (LTM) 41 Mn** and **unique business enquiries**.
    - Treat each metric as a within-company trend series only.

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Four listed survivors of Indian Web 1.0/2.0, one model: prepaid classifieds sold to the supply side.**
  - Every member monetises **the side that wants to be found**: recruiters (Naukri), builders/brokers (99acres), SME suppliers (IndiaMART, Justdial/JD Mart), OEMs/dealers/lenders and sellers (CarTrade). The demand side (job-seekers, home-buyers, buyers, searchers) uses them free.
  - **All four: FY26 revenue ₹6,847 Cr (+13.7%), OP ₹2,277 Cr (33.3%), other income ₹1,700 Cr, reported PAT ₹2,979 Cr (+15.0%); m-cap ₹1,06,109 Cr on 23-Oct-2026 (≈35.6× reported PAT, ≈40× attributable PAT ₹2,645 Cr)** [E: sums of screener 24-Oct-26; Justdial standalone].
  - **Ex-Info Edge (CarTrade + IndiaMART + Justdial): revenue ₹3,562 Cr (+12.3%), OP ₹1,091 Cr (30.6%), PAT ₹1,216 Cr (−5.0%, treasury income fell), m-cap ₹29,174 Cr (24.0× reported PAT), treasury ₹10,988 Cr = 38% of their m-cap** [E].
- **Info Edge is 72.5% of group m-cap and 48% of revenue, and more than half of its own m-cap is two listed stakes.**
  - Its 12.43% of **Eternal** (₹3,06,302 Cr m-cap) is worth **≈₹38,070 Cr**. Its 12.12% of **PB Fintech** (₹46,969 Cr) is worth **≈₹5,690 Cr**. Together **≈₹43,770 Cr = 57% of Info Edge's ₹76,935 Cr m-cap**, on a cost of ~₹714 Cr [E: stakes as of 31-Mar-26 V-snip; m-caps V screener 24-Oct-26].
  - Add ~₹5,034 Cr of treasury (Jun-26) [V co-PPT]. The market then values Naukri + 99acres + Jeevansathi + Shiksha + the unlisted venture book at **≈₹28,100 Cr** pre-tax and pre-holdco discount (≈₹22,500 Cr after deducting ~₹5,600 Cr of unlisted portfolio marks) [E]. That is ~**21–27× FY26 core post-tax operating profit** [E, §9.4]: cheap for a ~57%-margin monopoly, which is why "holdco discount" is the debate.
- **The hiring cycle has turned up, but on GCCs, not IT services.**
  - Naukri recruitment billings: FY26 +10% (Q4 FY26 +9.5%) → **Q1 FY27 +17.5% → Q2 FY27 +12.6%** (management says ~14–15% underlying, adjusting for renewal timing) [primer; V co-PPT; V press].
  - Q1 FY27 by customer: **GCCs +31%**, Tech/IT/BPM +15%, other sectors +12%, recruitment consultants +1% [V co-PPT]. GCCs were **17.3%** of FY26 India B2B recruitment billings vs IT services' direct 18.9% [V co-PPT].
  - The **Naukri JobSpeak index** is flat: **Sep-2026 3,053 vs 3,002 (+2% YoY)**; AI/ML +20%, IT/software −4% [V lapaasvoice, 3-Oct-26]. Nasscom FY26: industry revenue $315 bn (+6.1%), **net hiring only ~1.35 lakh**, headcount +2.3% [V-snip].
  - Read: Naukri is growing billings on **pricing, SMB/tier-2 penetration, B2C paid services (+35%) and AI products**, not on IT-services volume.
- **AI search is now a measurable P&L line item at two of the four.**
  - **Shiksha billings −27.2% in Q2 FY27** (₹20.5 Cr), attributed by Info Edge to "AI-driven search reducing referral traffic" [V press]. Q1 FY27 Shiksha billings "de-grew due to AI-related traffic headwinds" [V co-PPT].
  - **IndiaMART "business enquiries delivered" fell from 610 Mn (FY21) to 370 Mn (FY26), −19% in FY26 alone; Q1 FY27 85 Mn (−13%)**. Unique business enquiries Q1 FY27 26 Mn (−11% per the KPI table) [V co-PPT]. Paying suppliers have been flat at ~218–222K for six quarters.
  - Justdial traffic was −4.7% YoY in Q4 FY26 [primer] but **+0.4% in Q2 FY27 (198.4 Mn)**, with mobile −2.1% and desktop +24.7% [V co-PR]. App downloads/day −22% [V co-PR].
  - The members' response: AI products sold to the **paying side** (Naukri AI-Rex 400+ paid customers, Talent Pulse 600+; Justdial AI CRM/lead tools and WhatsApp quotes; CarTrade VAYA AI agents; JD Mart conversational RFQ) [V co-PPTs].
- **Treasury: ₹16,022 Cr of cash across the four (latest), 15% of group m-cap.**
  - Info Edge ₹5,034 Cr (Jun-26), CarTrade ₹1,321 Cr (Jun-26), IndiaMART ₹3,553 Cr (Jun-26), Justdial ₹6,114 Cr (Sep-26) [V co-PPTs].
  - **Justdial's treasury exceeds its m-cap (₹6,004 Cr) → EV ≈ −₹110 Cr** [E]. Its business is valued at zero.
- **Valuation dispersion is extreme, and it tracks growth plus capital allocation, not margin** (screener 24-Oct-26):
  - CarTrade **57.6× P/E** (EV/FY26 EBITDA ~48×) — the "operating-leverage compounder", near its 52-week high (₹2,820 vs H ₹3,291).
  - Info Edge 52.2× P/E (meaningless; SOTP stock).
  - **IndiaMART 19.2× P/E at its 52-week low** (₹1,573 vs L ₹1,539, −34% in a year); EV/EBITDA ~11× [E]. The supplier plateau plus the AI-enquiry decline is being priced.
  - **Justdial 11.8× P/E, P/B 1.1, EV < 0.**

### 1.2 Value chain (inputs → customer)

1. **Content/supply acquisition:** listings and CVs, mostly free (users upload CVs; owners/brokers list). Field sales teams onboard paying advertisers: IndiaMART ~5,400 sales and servicing staff; Justdial's large tele/field force [V co-PPT; K]. Info Edge's 99acres verification teams.
2. **Traffic acquisition:** organic search (Google) plus brand. CarTrade claims **95% organic unique visitors** [V co-PPT]. Advertising spend is low: Justdial ₹11.6 Cr in Q2 FY27 on ₹343 Cr revenue (3.4%) [V co-PR]; CarTrade marketing ₹32.7 Cr in FY26 (4.2% of revenue) [V co-PPT]. **Google is both the main traffic source and the main competitor**, which is why AI Overviews/AI Mode are an existential variable (§11).
3. **Matching/lead routing:** search, recommendation, lead distribution (IndiaMART "BuyLeads", Justdial lead delivery, Naukri Resdex search: ~997K resume searches per day) [V co-PPT, approx.].
4. **Monetisation:** prepaid packages (12-month typical; IndiaMART also multi-year), pay-per-lead, auction commissions, display ads.
5. **Fulfilment:** happens **off-platform** (an interview, a site visit, a phone call, an order) except at CarTrade's remarketing (physical yards and online auction) and OLX/Spinny transactions.

### 1.3 Where value is captured

- **Winner-take-most at the vertical level.** Naukri (~70–75% recruitment-portal traffic share [primer]) earns a **~57–58% segment margin** (FY26 standalone recruitment segment result ₹1,277 Cr on revenue ₹2,256 Cr = **56.6%**; Q1 FY27 **58.3%**) [E from V co-PPT segment note]. Meanwhile #2/#3 verticals (Jeevansathi, Shiksha) break even, and 99acres lost ₹59 Cr in FY26 [V co-PPT segment result].
- **The supply side pays, so value capture depends on lead quality and alternatives.** An SME pays IndiaMART ~₹69K/yr (ARPU; top-10% ARPU ₹3.49 lakh) [V co-PPT]. A Justdial campaign ~₹21K/yr [E]. A Naukri B2B customer ~₹1.4–1.5 lakh/yr (realisation) [V co-PPT chart, approx.].
- **Negative working capital:** IndiaMART working-capital days −301; Justdial −182; Info Edge's deferred revenue funds operations [V screener]. Float plus margins → treasury build-up → other income (§A force 6).

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 Data protection — DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (assent 11-Aug-2023). **DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13/14-Nov-2025** with phased commencement [V-snip]:
  - Data Protection Board provisions: immediate (Nov-2025) [K/V-snip].
  - **Consent Manager framework (Rule 4): from 13-Nov-2026.** Consent managers must be Indian companies with ≥₹2 Cr net worth [V-snip].
  - **Substantive obligations** (notice, consent standards, security safeguards, breach intimation, children's data, Significant Data Fiduciary duties): from **13-May-2027** (one source says 14-May-2027) [V-snip; FLAG — check the gazette].
  - Penalties up to **₹250 Cr per breach** of security-safeguard obligations [K, Act Schedule].
- **Relevance:** Naukri (118 Mn CVs: names, phone numbers, employment history), Jeevansathi (sensitive matrimony data), Justdial and IndiaMART (phone numbers of SMEs/buyers distributed to advertisers as "leads"). The lead-sharing model must be re-papered with **explicit, purpose-specific consent**, and lead resale to multiple advertisers may need clearer notices [E]. Naukri and Jeevansathi could be notified as **Significant Data Fiduciaries** (DPO, DPIA, audits) [E].
- **Cost:** compliance spend is modest relative to margins. The bigger risk is **lead volume** if consent friction reduces shareable contacts [E].

### 7.2 IT Act, IT Rules 2021 and intermediary status
- All four operate as **intermediaries** under the IT Act 2000 s.79 safe harbour, subject to the **IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021**: grievance officer, takedown timelines, due diligence on listings [K].
- **Fake job postings/recruitment fraud** (Naukri), **fraudulent listings/scams** (OLX, Justdial, IndiaMART) and **RERA-registration display** for project ads (99acres) are the main compliance flashpoints [K]. 2025–26 amendments on **synthetically generated (AI) content labelling** were proposed by MeitY (Oct-2025 draft) [K; FLAG status].
- **Online gaming/fantasy rules do not apply.**

### 7.3 Competition — CCI and the Digital Competition Bill
- **Digital Competition Bill (draft, MCA, Mar-2024):** an ex-ante regime for "Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises" (SSDEs) with turnover/user thresholds. In 2025 MCA reportedly put it on hold pending a market study [K; FLAG — no 2026 update found this session]. **The members would be beneficiaries, not targets**: none meets the SSDE thresholds, and the Bill targets Google/Meta/Amazon-type gatekeepers (self-preferencing, data use).
- **CCI vs Google:** the Android (₹1,337.76 Cr penalty, Oct-2022; largely upheld by NCLAT Mar-2023, before the Supreme Court) [K] and Play Store billing cases set the precedent. **Matrimony.com** was one of the original complainants against Google search bias (2012 case; CCI 2018 order fined Google ₹135.86 Cr for search bias) [K]. The members' AI-search grievance (AI Overviews using their content, zero-click) has **no Indian regulatory remedy yet** [E].
- **CCI deal approvals:** acquisitions above thresholds (deal-value threshold of ₹2,000 Cr from Sep-2024 [K]) need CCI approval. Not a constraint at the members' scale.

### 7.4 Tax
- **GST:** online services are 18% (B2B, input-credit-able for paying SMEs) [K]. IndiaMART's and Justdial's customers are mostly GST-registered SMEs, so GST is largely neutral. Unregistered micro-merchants bear it as a cost [E].
- **Equalisation levy:** the 6% levy on online advertising (to non-residents, e.g. Google/Meta) was **abolished from 1-Apr-2025** (Finance Act 2025) [K]. That marginally lowers the cost of advertising with foreign platforms, slightly negative for domestic ad-funded portals [E]. The 2% levy on e-commerce operators was abolished from Aug-2024 [K].
- **Capital gains (Info Edge holdco):** listed LTCG rate **12.5%** (from 23-Jul-2024) plus surcharge/cess (~14.95% effective for a company [E]); STCG 20% [K]. A full sale of the Eternal + PB stakes (₹43,770 Cr value, ~₹714 Cr cost) would carry **~₹6,400 Cr** of tax [E]. This is one quantifiable component of the holdco discount.
- **Treasury income** at all four is taxed at the corporate rate (~25.2%) or capital-gains rates (debt MF gains taxed at slab/corporate rate for units bought after 1-Apr-2023) [K]. That lowers post-tax treasury yields to ~5% [E].

### 7.5 Sector-specific
- **Real estate:** RERA requires project registration numbers in ads; MahaRERA/UP-RERA have penalised portals for listing unregistered projects [K].
- **Employment:** the new **labour codes (effective 21-Nov-2025)** triggered one-off gratuity charges (trap 4) and may formalise more hiring (positive for Naukri over time) [V co-PPT; E].
- **Vehicle repossession:** RBI's fair-practice code on recovery agents [K] affects lenders' repossession flow into auctions (Remarketing volume).
- **MPS:** Justdial's promoter at 74.14% is just under the 75% cap; any further RRVL purchase requires a delisting route or OFS-type dilution [K, SEBI SCRR 19A].

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

1. **1996–2000 (Web 1.0 founding):** Justdial (1996, phone), IndiaMART (1996, export directory), Naukri (1997) — all founded before most Indians were online.

## 11. Technology and disruption risks

### 11.1 AI/LLM search and zero-click (the defining structural risk)
- **Evidence (global):** Chartbeat data shows **Google search referrals to publishers fell ~33% in the year to Nov-2025**. Ahrefs finds the #1 organic result **loses ~34.5% of clicks** when an AI Overview appears. A small Growth Memo sample of **Google AI Mode sessions found ~75% with no external click**. Cloudflare data shows **human traffic to finance/publishing/retail sites fell ~40% between Jun-2025 and Apr-2026** [V-snip Press Gazette/ppc.land/Calcalist]. **No India-specific zero-click statistic was found** [FLAG].
- **Evidence (members):**
  - **Shiksha:** billings −27.2% in Q2 FY27, "AI-driven search reducing referrals" [V]. The clearest casualty: generic, informational queries ("best MBA colleges in Pune") that an LLM answers directly.
  - **IndiaMART:** business enquiries delivered −19% (FY26) and −13% (Q1 FY27); unique business enquiries −11% (Q1 FY27) [V co-PPT]. Not attributed to AI by the company in what was read, but it is the most consistent with the AI/Google hypothesis [E].
  - **Justdial:** traffic −4.7% (Q4 FY26) → +0.4% (Q2 FY27). Mobile UVs −2.1%, desktop +24.7%, voice −9.2%; app downloads/day −22% [V].
  - **Naukri, 99acres, CarWale, OLX:** no visible damage. 99acres enquiries +38%, app DAUs +38%; CarTrade 95% organic, ~80 Mn MAU [V]. **Transactional, proprietary-inventory platforms are holding; informational ones are losing.**
- **Mechanism ranking (most to least exposed) [E]:** Shiksha > Justdial (local "near me" answered in Google Maps/AI) > IndiaMART (supplier discovery via AI/WhatsApp) > CarWale research content (reviews/specs are LLM-answerable; leads are not) > Jeevansathi > 99acres (live inventory, owner/broker contacts) > OLX (C2C listings) > Naukri (private CV database, two-sided transactions) > Shriram Automall (physical auctions).
- **Member AI responses (sell AI to the paying side):**
  - Naukri **AI-Rex** (AI recruiter; 400+ paid customers) and **Talent Pulse** (600+); AI job-seeker tools; B2C paid conversion up from 1.2% to 2.6% in 18 months [V co-PPT].
  - Justdial **AI CRM/lead management**, AI review responses, WhatsApp-based onboarding and quotes, **JD Mart Conversational RFQ** ("100% AI-qualified leads") [V co-PPT].
  - CarTrade **VAYA AI** agents (match-making, pricing, condition assessment); AI assistant across a 17-step new-car journey [V co-PPT].
  - IndiaMART: AI-led lead qualification/BuyLead matching, Lead Manager CRM, Busy [K/V co-PPT]. Exact AI products were not extracted this session.
- **The other side:** LLMs need fresh, verified, structured supply data. The members could **license data or become the "supply layer"** for AI agents (Naukri's 118 Mn CVs; IndiaMART's 132 Mn product listings). No licensing deals were found [E].

### 11.2 Other disruption vectors
- **LinkedIn + in-house AI sourcing** (Naukri): large employers use AI to source from LinkedIn/their own ATS. Info Edge counters with the Zwayam ATS and AI-Rex [V co-PPT; K].
- **WhatsApp commerce** (IndiaMART/Justdial): SMEs transact on WhatsApp Business. Both members now integrate WhatsApp rather than fight it [V co-PPT].
- **Full-stack used-car players** (CarTrade): Spinny/Cars24 own transactions. CarTrade's answer is partnership (Spinny) rather than competition [V-snip].
- **Builder-direct digital marketing** (99acres): large developers run Meta/Google lead campaigns directly; 99acres counters with video (99Shorts) and owner listings (606K) [V co-PPT].
- **Facebook Marketplace** (OLX): free C2C listings with social trust.

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert analyst leads with)

1. **The white-collar hiring cycle has shifted from IT services to GCCs (Naukri).**
   - Naukri is ~70% of Info Edge's standalone billings (recruitment ₹2,374 Cr of ₹3,177 Cr in FY26 [primer]; ₹613.8 of ₹822.3 Cr in Q2 FY27 [V]) and nearly all its profit.
   - Billings growth troughed at ~9.5% (Q4 FY26), jumped to 17.5% in Q1 FY27 and ran at 12.6% (≈14–15% underlying) in Q2.
   - The mix has changed. GCC billings grew +31% in Q1 FY27, while IT services' direct share is only ~25% (30–35% including consultants) [V co-PPT]. The JobSpeak headline (+2%) and IT/software (−4%) say volume is flat [V]. **Growth = price + mix + B2C + AI upsell, not hiring volume.** A GCC slowdown (US tariff/visa politics) is now a bigger risk than an IT-services slump.
2. **Real estate: plateaued sales, rising launches (99acres).**
   - Anarock Q3 CY26 sales +3% (PropEquity −6%), but launches +18% and unsold inventory +12% [V-snip]. Builders with inventory to clear advertise.
   - 99acres billings +16.5% (Q1) and **+21.2% (Q2 FY27)** [V]. The segment loss shrank to ₹2.1 Cr in Q1 FY27 (vs ₹18.7 Cr) [V co-PPT]. Management targets doubling billings to ~₹1,000 Cr in three years at 25–30% margins [primer].
   - 99acres is **Info Edge's swing factor for FY27–FY29 margins**: every ₹100 Cr of extra billings at ~60–70% incremental margin adds ~₹60–70 Cr of operating profit [E].
3. **SME demand and paying-supplier churn (IndiaMART, Justdial).**
   - IndiaMART: paying suppliers flat at ~218–222K for six quarters, −1.85K in Q1 FY27. ARPU +9% is doing all the work, and consolidated collections growth slowed to +8% in Q1 FY27 [V co-PPT].
   - Justdial: paid campaigns **+5.2%** and collections +11.7% (Q2 FY27), better than IndiaMART [V co-PR].
   - **The SME franchise is not broken, but at IndiaMART the volume engine has stalled.** The stock reflects it: −34% in a year, P/E 19.2 [V screener].
4. **AI/LLM search and zero-click: informational portals lose, transactional ones hold** (§11).
   - The clearest measurable casualties are Shiksha (−27%) and IndiaMART enquiry volumes (−13% to −19%).
   - Naukri, 99acres and OLX show no damage. The question for 2027 is whether Google AI Mode answers "find me a supplier/plumber/flat" queries in Indian languages.
5. **Used cars, OLX and remarketing: CarTrade's three-engine operating leverage.**
   - FY26 revenue +21%, EBITDA +70%, PAT +68% [V co-PPT/screener]. Q1 FY27: revenue +16%, EBITDA +45% [V].
   - OLX EBITDA +76% (Q1). The Spinny partnership monetises OLX/CarWale used-car intent **without inventory risk**.
   - The used-car market (>6 mn units FY26 → >7 mn FY27E) grows ~7–9% a year [V-snip CRISIL]. Repossession flows feed Remarketing.
   - **Risk: the valuation (57.6× P/E, ~48× EV/EBITDA) already prices continued 40%+ EBITDA growth.**
6. **Treasury: ₹16,000 Cr of cash; ~46% of group PBT is other income.**
   - Justdial's cash exceeds its m-cap, and IndiaMART's is 38% of its m-cap.
   - Capital allocation (dividends/buybacks) is the lever. IndiaMART pays the most (₹60/share FY26 incl. a ₹30 special; ~₹1,650 Cr returned cumulatively per company) [primer; V co-PPT]. **Justdial pays nothing (0% payout for a decade)** [V screener], and Reliance's intentions are unknown. CarTrade pays nothing.
   - Treasury income is **MTM-volatile**: yields up in Q2 FY27 → weak Q2 other income across the board [V co-PR Justdial].
7. **Info Edge = operating company + listed venture holdco (marks, holdco discount).**
   - 57% of its m-cap is the Eternal + PB Fintech stakes at market (₹43,766 Cr) [E].
   - PB Fintech has halved from its 52-week high, and Eternal is near its high (₹317 vs H ₹345) [V screener].
   - The implied core classifieds valuation is ~₹22,500–29,000 Cr → ~22–35× core post-tax earnings, depending on tax and unlisted-book treatment [E §9.4].
   - These marks go through OCI, so reported PAT is cleaner than the primer claims, but NCI (LP shares in the AIFs) inflates consolidated PAT by ~₹300 Cr a year [E].
8. **ESOP costs and the "adjusted" profit problem.**
   - All three founder/professional-run members (Info Edge, IndiaMART, CarTrade) issue ESOPs heavily. CarTrade's FY22 OP of −₹133 Cr reflects IPO-year ESOP charges [V screener; K attribution]. CarTrade and IndiaMART headline "adjusted EBITDA" excludes ESOP (and, at CarTrade, includes other income).
   - **Use reported EBITDA/OP and attributable PAT in all comparisons.** Info Edge allots shares to its ESOP Trust regularly (Q1 FY27 note) [V co-PPT].
9. **Network effects and winner-take-most economics vs the Google/Meta ad duopoly.**
   - Naukri's ~57% margin vs 99acres' −12% (FY26) and Shiksha's breakeven is the single best illustration that **only the #1 in a vertical earns economic rent** [V co-PPT segments].
   - Digital ads are ₹94,700 Cr (+26% CY25) and ~63% of all advertising [V-snip FICCI-EY], but most of it flows to Google/Meta. Members win only where they own **proprietary supply** (CVs, listings, auctions).
10. **Regulation: DPDP (consent for lead-sharing), IT Rules (fraud/takedowns), labour codes (one-offs, formalisation), Digital Competition Bill (dormant; would help members vs Google)** (§7). None is a P&L threat in FY27. DPDP's May-2027 deadline is the one to model for lead-gen consent friction [E].

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**General classifieds equation:**
Revenue(t) ≈ Billings(t−lag) recognised = Σ [paying customers × ARPU] spread over subscription life
Operating profit = Revenue − (sales headcount × cost per head) − marketing − tech/hosting − D&A
PAT = (Operating profit + Treasury yield × Cash) × (1 − tax) ± fair-value marks ± minority interests

**Deferred-revenue identity:** Closing deferred revenue = Opening deferred + Billings (collections) − Revenue recognised. Check, IndiaMART FY26 consolidated: 1,678 + 1,857 − 1,569 = **1,966 ≈ reported ₹1,965 Cr** [E from V co-PPT ✓].

### C.1 Worked example 1 — Naukri recruitment, Q1 FY27 [V co-PPT; E for the splits]
- Billings ₹553 Cr (+17.5%) → revenue recognised ₹612 Cr (+13.0%; revenue > billings because Q1 recognises Q4's heavy billings).
- Customers: ~49K billed corporate customers in the quarter (FY26 annual ~146K billed customers × ~₹1.46 lakh realisation ≈ ₹2,130 Cr of India B2B billings) [E from V chart, approx.].
- Costs ₹256 Cr → **operating PBT ₹356 Cr (58.3%)**. Operating leverage: revenue +13% → op PBT +25%.
- Incremental margin: Δ op PBT ₹72 Cr / Δ revenue ₹70 Cr ≈ **~100%** YoY [E: Q1 FY26 revenue ₹541.5 Cr, op PBT ~₹284 Cr].

## 4.5 New insights the first pass missed

1. **Info Edge's management states the AI-risk test in one sentence.** Where Info Edge owns both sides of the marketplace, AI helps; where traffic came through a channel it did not own (Shiksha via Google), AI hurts. Combine this with IndiaMART's admission that Google's SERP "non-links" push it below the mobile fold even when it ranks #1–2, and that it stopped reporting traffic because of bot/LLM noise. Together they make the B2 framework **management-sourced rather than analyst inference**.
2. **IndiaMART's margin is being bought with the supplier stall.** Q1 EBITDA (35%) was "elevated" *because* customer-acquisition spend fell. Management will not push gross adds while Silver churn is ~7% a month. Margin and supplier growth are therefore a trade-off, not two independent levers. FY26's margin dip was partly accounting (ESOP doubled to ₹34.7 Cr; labour code ₹8.6 Cr).
3. **CarTrade's Remarketing arm is half-owned by its biggest customer.** Shriram Finance owns 44.57% of SAMIL and is its anchor institutional seller (AGM-approved RPT cap ₹362.6 Cr). This is good for volume, but a concentration and governance point the dossier missed. It also means ~45% of Remarketing profits leak to NCI.
4. **CarTrade's own target implies ~32–40% profit CAGR** (₹1,000 Cr in 4–5 years from ₹244 Cr). The 57.6× P/E therefore prices roughly management's own plan, and the "Q2–Q4 > Q1" seasonality claim gets tested on 28-Oct.
5. **Justdial after the founder.** Both new CXOs (CEO 1-Aug-26; CFO 11-Jul-26) come from the Reliance/Flipkart network, the founder is now non-executive, and RRVL related-party business is near zero (₹1.4 Cr). The capital-allocation question (₹6,114 Cr cash, zero payout, promoter at 74.14% against the 75% MPS cap) now sits with a Reliance-appointed team. That raises the odds of either a payout or a corporate action, but nothing has been announced. Note also that **Justdial no longer hosts earnings calls** (none transcribed since Apr-2025).
6. **99acres: the competitive field changed in 2026.** Housing.com (REA-backed, burning ~₹250 Cr a year) was sold to Aurum PropTech, and 99acres' web time share rose from 32% (Jun-24) to 51% (Mar-26). Yet 99acres still has **no underlying profitable quarter** (Q4 FY26's "profit" was a ₹20 Cr accounting one-off), and builder budgets go to Facebook/Google. The breakeven story depends on FY27 execution.
7. **Naukri's growth is a third each from price, volume and new products, and the AI products are being priced per outcome** (₹3,500 per AI-Rex mandate). This is the first Indian classifieds AI SKU with a disclosed price. Cannibalisation of Resdex upgrades is acknowledged but unquantified.
8. **Global read-across:** Rightmove guides 2026 operating-profit growth (3–5%) below revenue growth (8–10%) to fund AI. Margin dilution to fund AI is now a global classifieds pattern, which is relevant to how far Naukri's 58% margin can expand.
