**Members (3, Tier C):** Redington (REDINGTON · 532805), MMTC (MMTC · 513377), Entero Healthcare Solutions (ENTERO · 544122).

**The bucket is mixed. Treat the three as archetypes, not peers:**
1. **Redington: a scaled, multi-country technology distributor.** It sells smartphones, PCs, servers, software and cloud across India, the Middle East, Turkey and Africa. It is the sector's economic reality: about 95% of member revenue and about 67% of member market cap.
2. **Entero: a pharma and medtech distribution roll-up.** It was listed in February 2024 and is a consolidation story inside a fragmented market.
3. **MMTC: a state trading house that no longer trades.** It has done no business since April 2022, on the Commerce Ministry's direction. It is a cash shell, 89.93% owned by the government, with a 26% stake in the MMTC-PAMP gold refinery and a "closure decision awaited" (its own annual report).

## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **"Revenue" is gross pass-through value at all three, and it means three different things.**
   - **Redington** books the full invoice value of hardware it buys and resells (principal, gross). For much of the software and cloud it sells, it books only the margin as revenue, because under Ind AS 115 it is an **agent** [K, accounting norm].
     - The US peer TD SYNNEX now reports both "gross billings" and revenue: Q4 FY25 billings **$24.3 bn vs revenue $17.4 bn** [V-snip SEC 8-K]. Redington publishes **no gross-billings figure**.
     - So SSG (software) revenue of ₹19,834 Cr understates the software and cloud value Redington handles, and a principal/agent reclassification can move reported revenue without changing profit [E].
     - **Entero said the same thing:** its FY27 "core organic ~12%" guidance is "adjusted for some low-ROIC business exits and **net-revenue accounting**" [V Arihant Q4 FY26 note].
   - **Entero** books the full price-to-stockist value of medicines it distributes. A ₹100 strip is ~₹90 of manufacturer cost [primer; V GM 10.3% FY26].
   - **MMTC** historically booked full canalised import/export value. Peak turnover was ~₹30,000 Cr in FY21 on screener; it was **₹23,931 Cr in FY15** and **₹29,439 Cr in FY19** [V screener].
     - Today's ₹3.41 Cr "revenue from operations" is **mostly electricity sold from a 15 MW windmill in Karnataka (₹2.63 Cr)** [V AR].
     - The primer calls that ₹2.63 Cr "residual agency & services". It is wind power.
   - **Never add revenue across the three and call it a "market".** Part 3 aggregates do so only as a straight sum, labelled.
2. **Margin percentage is the wrong scoreboard. Use rupees of gross profit, EBITDA per ₹ of capital, and working-capital days.**
   - Redington's gross margin fell from 5.47% (FY25) to **5.06% (FY26)** and to **4.90% in Q1 FY27**. Yet its EBITDA rose 67% in Q1 [V].
   - The dilution comes from mix (mobility, large data-centre deals) and from price inflation (PCs +25–50%), which raises revenue without raising rupee margin.
   - In Q1 FY27, return on capital employed (ROCE) reached **22.2%** [V].
3. **PAT has three bases. Quote the basis every time.**
   - **Redington FY26:**
     - Screener consolidated PAT: **₹1,284 Cr**, including minority interest and the full ₹152.3 Cr Arena impairment.
     - Attributable to owners (from screener EPS ₹19.06 × 78.2 cr shares): **~₹1,490 Cr** [E].
     - Company "PAT" (after minority interest, **excluding** Redington's ₹75.2 Cr share of the impairment): **₹1,565 Cr** [V Q4 deck].
     - Excluding Arena altogether: **₹1,687 Cr** [V].
     - FY25 bases: screener ₹1,821 Cr includes the Paynet gain (₹625.8 Cr gross, ₹265.1 Cr post-tax Redington share); company ex-Paynet ₹1,340 Cr [V].
   - **Redington Q1 FY27:**
     - Screener PAT: **₹453 Cr**.
     - Company PAT: **₹486 Cr**, after minority interest. Arena's ₹64 Cr loss is ~51% borne by Arena's minority shareholders (~₹33 Cr), so attributable profit is higher than the consolidated total [V].
     - The ₹486 Cr vs ₹453 Cr gap is minority interest, **not an exceptional item**. AngelOne's "pre-exceptional" label is wrong.
   - **Entero Q1 FY27:**
     - PAT **₹52 Cr** total; **attributable ₹38 Cr**. Minority interest ₹14 Cr = ~27% of pre-minority profit, because recent acquisitions are 51–80% stakes [V Arihant].
     - In Q4 FY26 attributable PAT was only ₹28 Cr of ₹45 Cr [V].
     - **Screener's P/E of 49.5× is on attributable EPS.** On total PAT it would be ~38× [E].
   - **MMTC FY26:**
     - Standalone PBT **₹463.30 Cr**, but standalone **PAT ₹212.07 Cr** after ₹89 Cr current tax and ₹162 Cr deferred tax [V AR].
     - **Our primer mislabels ₹463.30 Cr as PAT** (Part 3.4). Consolidated PAT is **₹387 Cr** [V screener].
4. **MMTC's profit is a sale, interest on that sale, and a gold refinery dividend. It is not trading.**
   - FY26 standalone exceptional income was **₹473.70 Cr** (net). Most of it is the **final ₹411.76 Cr NINL escrow release, received 4-Jul-2025**, plus ₹25.75 Cr interest on it [V AR].
   - **The three-year limitation on NINL claims expired 4-Jul-2025: "nothing is payable/receivable from NINL as on date"** [V AR]. There are no more NINL tranches.
   - Excluding exceptionals, standalone PBT was **≈ −₹10 Cr** [E: 463.30 − 473.70].
   - The consolidated line adds MMTC's equity-accounted share of **MMTC-PAMP profit, ₹175.31 Cr in FY26** (₹17.10 Cr in FY25) [V-snip ScanX via search; consistent with the investment rising ₹263.59 Cr → ₹439.04 Cr, V AR].
   - **Recurring earning power ≈ interest on ~₹1,790 Cr of bank deposits (~₹110–130 Cr a year at 6–7%) + the PAMP share − ~₹100–105 Cr of overheads** [E].
5. **Arena accounting at Redington.**
   - Redington owns its Turkish subsidiary Arena Bilgisayar through Redington Gulf FZE. It bought **49% in 2012** [V corporate deck]. It has control, so it consolidates 100% of revenue, but it bears ~49% of profit or loss: Q1 FY27 Redington share was ₹31 Cr of a ₹64 Cr loss [V].
   - Turkey has been a **hyperinflationary economy** since 2022. Redington has applied **Ind AS 29 from 1-Apr-2022** to TRY-functional step-down subsidiaries, calling the impact "not significant" at the time [V-snip Redington FY23 financial notes].
   - Arena has since **exited TRY-denominated businesses**: mobility (including the Vodafone contract) and Paynet (fintech, sold Feb-2025 to iyzico for ~US$89 m [primer]). So the IAS 29 restatement now applies to a shrinking base [V Q4 deck; transcript].
   - Arena's FY26 revenue fell to **~₹6,824 Cr from ~₹9,097 Cr** (−25%), and Q1 FY27 was **~₹1,172 Cr vs ~₹2,068 Cr** (−43%) [E: company total minus "excl. Arena"].
6. **Working-capital days have three sources and they differ.**
   - Redington FY26: screener (year-end balances) **WC days 26, CCC 34** (debtors 66, inventory 26, payables 58) [V]. Company (average basis) **30 days FY26; 32 days in Q1 FY27 vs 37 a year earlier** (inventory 31, debtors 59, creditors 58) [V].
   - Entero FY26: screener **CCC 74, WC days 35** [V]. Company **net WC 59 days at Q4 FY26, 61 days at Q1 FY27 (66 a year ago)** [V Arihant]. The primer's "98 → 66 → 60 target" uses the company basis.
   - Use company figures in prose and screener figures only for like-for-like peer tables. The computer-hardware report used year-end CCC: **Redington 34, Rashi 60** [V-sib].
7. **Redington is not promoter-less in the economic sense.**
   - SEBI classifies it as a "listed entity with no promoters" and it reports 0% promoter holding [V corporate deck; screener].
   - But **Synnex Technology International Corp (Taiwan) holds 24.12%** (30-Jun-2026) and has **two non-executive board seats** (Tu Shu-Chyuan, Chen Yi-Ju) [V corporate deck]. It was a strategic investor from 2003–06 with 36% [V].
   - Screener shows the holding inside "FIIs 63.42%". The primer's "no deep-pocketed anchor" framing is wrong (Part 3.4).
8. **Entero's growth is two numbers.**
   - **FY26:** reported revenue +29%, organic **15.6%** vs IPM ~10% [V Arihant Q4].
   - **Q1 FY27:** +38.2% reported = **17.8% organic (19.6% like-for-like) + 20.4% inorganic**. The inorganic part is entirely the calendarisation of FY26 deals; there were no new deals in Q1 [V Arihant Q1].
   - **FY27 guidance of 23% includes ~11 points of calendarisation; "core organic" is ~12%** [V Arihant Q4].
   - In the primer, "₹1,000 Cr+ of acquired revenue guided for FY27" is a misreading. The ₹1,000 Cr is the **MedTech annualised run-rate** (Anand Chemiceutics + Ace Cardiopathy + Bioaide + legacy). There is no acquired-revenue guidance; management has **paused M&A for 6–8 months** [V].
9. **MMTC's consolidated vs standalone gap is NOT the Singapore subsidiary.**
   - MMTC Transnational Pte Ltd (Singapore) has been **under court-ordered liquidation since 27-Oct-2023**, with Deloitte as liquidators. It is not consolidated, and a CBI fraud case was registered on 15-Oct-2024 [V AR; V-snip Q1 FY27 notes].
   - The gap is the equity-accounted **MMTC-PAMP** share.
   - The primer says Singapore "explains part of the gap". It does not.
10. **"Solar distribution" at Redington is immaterial.**
    - "Renewable energy" (solar panels and inverters) revenue was **₹103 Cr in FY26 (−33% YoY), 0.09% of revenue**; ₹71 Cr in Q1 FY27 [V decks].
    - Redington reports **100 MW of solar capacity created in India** [V corporate deck].
    - Treat solar as an option, not a segment. The real adjacency is **SSG (software/cloud/security): ₹19,834 Cr, 16.6% of FY26 revenue, +37%** [V].

### 1.2 Value chains (inputs → customer)

1. **Tech distribution (Redington model)**
   - Chain: OEM/vendor (Apple, HP, Dell, Lenovo, Samsung, Microsoft, AWS, Nvidia) → **distributor** (buys, holds stock 25–31 days, finances the channel 59–66 days, collects) → tier-2 distributor/reseller/LFR/e-tailer/SI → end user.
   - Redington runs **168 warehouses, 71 sales offices, ~450 brands, 73,000+ channel partners and ~5,300 staff across 40 markets (33 in-country)** [V corporate deck Q1 FY27].
   - The distributor earns:
     - (a) front margin, the invoice spread;
     - (b) **back-end vendor rebates/incentives** for hitting sell-in targets, price protection and marketing funds [K, industry norm];
     - (c) interest charged to channel partners for delayed payment, which Redington books in other income [V transcript];
     - (d) logistics/services fees.
   - It pays for warehousing, credit loss (AR provision **0.39% of sales in Q1 FY27 vs 0.15%** a year earlier, Middle East caution), inventory write-downs (0.07% of sales), freight and interest [V].
2. **Pharma distribution (Entero model)**
   - Chain: manufacturer → C&F agent (manufacturer's regional depot, paid ~2–3% [K]) → **stockist/distributor** (buys at price-to-stockist, PTS) → retailer (buys at price-to-retailer, PTR) → patient at MRP.
   - Conventional Indian trade margins on branded generics are **~20% retailer margin on PTR and ~10% stockist margin on PTS** [K, AIOCD trade convention; not re-verified].
   - Under DPCO-2013 the ceiling price of scheduled (NLEM) drugs embeds a **16% retailer margin**; wholesalers customarily get ~8% on scheduled drugs [K].
   - Entero's reported **gross margin of 10.3% (FY26) / 11.4% (Q1 FY27)** sits on top of the 8–10% stockist convention. Scale-led purchase discounts, medtech mix and demand-generation fees add the rest [V; E].
3. **Canalised state trading (historical MMTC model)**
   - Chain: foreign supplier → MMTC (opens the LC, takes title, earns a sliver) → Indian buyer (jeweller, fertiliser board, steel mill, utility).
   - Decanalisation over 1991–2016 removed the legal monopoly commodity by commodity. MMTC was directed to stop all business from April 2022 [V AR; primer].

### 1.3 Where value is captured

- **The vendor captures the product margin. The distributor is paid for capital, credit risk and reach.**
  - Redington's FY26 gross profit was **₹6,042 Cr on ₹1,19,347 Cr (5.06%)**. Opex absorbed ~₹3,630 Cr (3.0%), leaving EBITDA of ₹2,414 Cr (2.02%) [V; E opex derived].
  - Entero's gross profit was ~₹679 Cr (10.3%) on ₹6,591 Cr, with EBITDA ₹266 Cr (4.0%) [V].
- **Distributors with ROCE above their cost of capital are the ones that win share without loosening credit.**
  - Redington SISA Q1 FY27: **ROCE 28.3%, ROE 26.6%, WC days 31** [V].
  - Redington ROW including Arena: WC days 32 (was 40). Arena itself is value-destroying at the current scale [V].
- **Software/cloud carries a higher gross-to-capital ratio:** no inventory, short collections. SSG grew to 16.6% of revenue in FY26 (14.5% in FY25) [E from V], but Redington does not disclose SSG margin.
- **In pharma, value sits with brand owners and, increasingly, with whoever controls demand:**
  - Medtech (Entero plays a "demand-generation rather than pure-fulfilment" role, with a higher margin) [V];
  - GLP-1 cold chain;
  - private label [primer].
- **In MMTC's case the residual value is entirely in the balance sheet:** bank deposits ₹1,786 Cr, the PAMP JV and some legacy land/office property. A defined benefit for nobody but the state.

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 Tech distribution

- **GST** at 18% on most IT hardware, phones and software; input credit flows through the distributor [K].
  - **E-invoicing is mandatory above ₹5 Cr turnover** (since Aug-2023), plus e-way bills. This favours organised distributors because grey-channel arbitrage becomes harder [K].
  - The Sep-2025 GST 2.0 rate rationalisation cut TVs/ACs to 18% [V-sib consumer-electronics]. PCs and phones were unchanged at 18% [K].
- **Customs duties** on mobile phones and parts, and the laptop import licensing regime (Aug-2023 notice, then "import management system" authorisations) [K]. These favour local assembly and shift the distributor's sourcing.
- **Gulf:** UAE corporate tax 9% from FY2024; KSA VAT 15% [K]. **Turkey: IAS 29 hyperinflation accounting from Apr-2022**, which Redington applies [V-snip].
- **Credit insurance / factoring:** Redington uses non-recourse factoring (cost fell to ~₹8 Cr in Q1 FY27) [V]. A war-related **withdrawal of insurance policies** forced new cover in the Gulf [V transcript].
- **SEBI "listed entity with no promoter" classification** applies to Redington (2019–22) [V].

### 7.2 Pharma distribution

- **Drugs & Cosmetics Act / CDSCO / state drug controllers.**
  - Wholesale drug licence (Form 20B/21B), registered pharmacist/competent person, storage rules, cold chain [K].
  - GDP (Good Distribution Practices) guidelines [K].
- **DPCO 2013 / NPPA:**
  - Ceiling prices on NLEM drugs (~18% of IPM [K]); annual WPI-linked price revisions.
  - **Trade Margin Rationalisation under para 19:**
    - 2019 pilot: 42 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs capped at 30% trade margin; MRPs of 526 brands cut up to 90%.
    - **17-Oct-2026 gazette: 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, effective 9-Nov-2026, MRP cuts up to 70%** [V newsonair].
    - A Parliamentary Standing Committee (Aug-2026) noted there is **no timeframe for a permanent TMR framework in DPCO** [V-snip PRS].
    - Reports say the trade has agreed to a **30% cap (on PTS, i.e. ~43% mark-up to stockist price) for all non-price-controlled drugs**, and that stockists and retailers may seek compensation from manufacturers [V-snip; FLAG — press report of a proposal].
- **AIOCD (All India Organisation of Chemists and Druggists):** the trade body that historically enforced "no-objection certificates" for new stockists. **CCI penalised AIOCD and state chemist associations for anti-competitive practices** (several orders 2012–2018) [K]. It is the reason India stayed fragmented and the reason roll-ups were possible after the CCI orders [E].
- **E-pharmacy:** draft e-pharmacy rules (2018) remain un-notified. Litigation (Delhi HC) continues; AIOCD opposes e-pharmacy and quick-commerce medicine delivery [K, FLAG].
- **GST** on medicines: 12% for most, 5% for some; the Sep-2025 GST 2.0 cut most medicines to 5% [K, FLAG — verify for the writer].

### 7.3 State trading (MMTC)

- Canalisation lists under the Foreign Trade Policy (DGFT) were progressively removed. MMTC was **denotified as a canalising agency** (around 2018–19) [V-snip ibtimes].
- **MoCI directed MMTC to stop all business from April 2022.** **DIPAM granted an exemption** (dividend/capital restructuring guidelines), and DPE waived the MoU [V AR].
- CPSE closure follows the DPE "Guidelines for time-bound closure of sick/loss-making CPSEs" (2018) [K]. **A Cabinet decision is required; none is reported** [V AR "awaited"].

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

- **1963–1991: the canalisation era.** MMTC was formed in 1963 and became India's largest canalising agency (ores, metals, fertilisers, bullion) [primer].
- **1991–2013: decanalisation commodity by commodity.** MMTC stayed big through bullion (turnover ~₹66,000 Cr at peak, early 2010s [primer, approx.]).
- **1993: Redington's Indian business starts** with an HP contract [V corporate deck]. Synnex takes 36% (2003–06). Chrys Capital 11%. Expansion to Dubai, KSA and Africa. IPO Feb-2007 [V].
- **2012:** Redington buys **49% of Arena (Turkey)** [V]. MMTC's sub-1% free float briefly makes it one of India's "largest" companies by market cap [primer].
- **2013–14:** gold 80:20 rule and duty hikes break MMTC's bullion engine [primer].

### 9.2 Why a 2% margin earns 20%: the DuPont of distribution [E]

- **Redington FY26:**
  - Revenue ₹1,19,162 Cr ÷ average equity ~₹9,450 Cr = **12.6× equity turnover**.
  - × PAT margin 1.08% → **ROE ~13.6% reported**. Attributable ex-impairment: 1,565/9,450 = **16.6%**. The company says 16.8% [V].
  - **Capital employed** (equity 10,160 + borrowings 2,842 = ~₹13,000 Cr) **turns ~9.2× a year**. EBIT ~₹2,175 Cr (OP 2,348 − dep 206 + OI 33) → ROCE ~17% on year-end capital (screener 18.4%; company 19.7% ex-impairment).
  - Lower WC days → higher turns → higher ROCE at the same margin.
  - **Sensitivity:** each 1 day of WC at FY26 revenue = **~₹327 Cr** of capital. At a ~9% funding cost that is ~₹29 Cr of pre-tax profit, ~1.2% of EBITDA.
- **Entero FY26:**
  - Revenue ₹6,591 Cr ÷ capital employed ~₹2,370 Cr (equity 1,689 + debt 677) = **2.8× turns**. × EBIT margin ~3.5% = **ROCE ~10%**.
  - The capital base includes ~₹749 Cr of goodwill and leftover IPO cash. **Ex-goodwill ROCE ≈ 14–15%**, matching the primer's 14.6% [E].
  - Q1 FY27 annualised EBIT ~₹350 Cr on ~₹2,400–2,500 Cr capital ≈ 14–15% reported. The company's 21.1% likely excludes goodwill/cash [E; FLAG basis].
- **The general rule:** ROCE ≈ EBIT margin × 365 ÷ (net WC days + fixed-asset days).
  - Redington: 1.85% × 365/(30+~10) ≈ 17%.
  - Entero: 3.4% × 365/(60+~55 incl. goodwill) ≈ 11% [E].

### 9.3 The memory-price illusion (Redington, Q1 FY27) [E]

- **ESG revenue +35% with PC unit growth in "single digits"** implies ~25–30% ASP inflation [V transcript; E].
- Group gross margin fell 23 bp (5.13% → 4.90%), but **gross profit rose 28%** and EBITDA +67% because opex grew far slower.
- **Pre-buying risk:** IDC says the channel built inventory "aggressively". If prices stabilise or fall in H2 (CEO: "prices will stabilize at these prices"), the distributor faces (a) unit declines, (b) possible price-protection claims and (c) slower collections.
  - **The FY23 analogue:** after the FY22 PC boom, Redington's CFO went to **−₹3,234 Cr** and ROCE fell from 28% to 19% over two years [V screener].

### 9.4 Entero's roll-up arithmetic [E]

- Buy ₹100 Cr of revenue at ~0.3–0.35× sales (₹30–35 Cr) [primer].
  - Example: Ace Cardiopathy, 60% for ₹59–77 Cr on FY25 revenue of ₹154.8 Cr, implies **~0.64–0.83× sales for 100%** [V filing; E]. Medtech multiples are higher than pharma.
- The target earns ~3–5% EBITDA (₹3–5 Cr). After integration at 5–6% EBITDA on growing revenue, payback is ~5–7 years pre-synergy.
- Minority partners keep 20–49% until the call options are exercised (2–5 years at original multiples). **Attributable profit therefore lags total profit** (27% minority interest in Q1 FY27).

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert leads with)

1. **Thin-margin, high-velocity economics.**
   - A distributor keeps ₹2 of EBITDA per ₹100 (Redington) or ₹4–5 (Entero) and earns 18–22% ROCE only by turning capital 9–12× a year.
   - The scoreboard is **rupees of gross profit, opex/gross profit and WC days**, not margin percentage. Redington's Q1 FY27 shows it: gross margin −23 bp, yet EBITDA +67% [V].
2. **Working capital is the whole game, and its funding cost just turned.**
   - Each WC day at Redington's scale is ~₹330 Cr of capital.
   - Redington pays ~₹89 Cr a quarter in finance cost after the repo moved to 5.50% (7-Oct-2026) [V].
   - The FY23 precedent (CFO −₹3,234 Cr after the FY22 boom) is the downside template [V].
3. **Vendor concentration and vendor pricing set the cycle.**
   - Apple is 31% of Redington. The top-10 brands are 75% [V].
   - In 2026, memory inflation pushed PC prices up 25–50% and phone ASPs +14%. The trackers expect units to fall in H2 and the channel to correct (IDC: PC sell-in "dwindling from Q4 2026") [V].
4. **Geography and currency.**
   - Redington's ROW is 40% of revenue. The West Asia war (Feb–Apr 2026, Hormuz) and Turkey (CPI ~30%, Ind AS 29, Arena losses of ₹64 Cr/qtr) are the swing factors in reported PAT [V].
5. **Pharma distribution: consolidation versus regulation.**
   - Entero's runway is fragmentation (~65k stockists, <10% organised [K/primer]). It wins at ~1.3–1.5× IPM growth organically, plus acquisitions.
   - The opposing force is **NPPA trade-margin rationalisation** (137 anti-cancer drugs from 9-Nov-2026; a possible 30% cap on all non-scheduled drugs) and vertical integration (Apollo/Keimed) [V/V-snip].
6. **The state trading house is a closure option, not a business.**
   - MMTC has done no business since Apr-2022, and the government says there is "no requirement" for it [V AR]. Value = cash + PAMP stake − Anglo Coal and contingent liabilities.
   - The market price (3.6× book) embeds a corporate-action premium with no timetable.

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Distributor ROCE = (Gross margin % − Opex % ) × (1 − tax) ÷ (Net working-capital days/365 + fixed-capital days/365)**, or equivalently:
- **EBITDA = Revenue × GM% − Opex**, where Revenue = Σ (units × ASP) by category, and GM% = front margin + vendor rebates + channel interest − price-protection gaps.
- **PAT = EBITDA − D&A − (Net WC × funding rate) − tax − minority interest**.
- **Capital = Revenue × (Inventory days + Debtor days − Creditor days)/365 + fixed assets (+ goodwill for roll-ups)**.

### C.1 Worked example 1 — Redington, ₹100 Cr of incremental India PC sales (Q1 FY27-type) [E composite]

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue | ₹100 Cr (e.g. ~17,000 laptops at ~₹58,000 ASP; ASP up ~30% YoY) |
| Gross margin @ ~4.3% (SISA Q1) | ₹4.3 Cr |
| Incremental opex @ ~45% of GP (operating leverage) | ₹1.9 Cr |
| EBITDA | ₹2.4 Cr (2.4%) |
| Net WC @ 31 days | ₹8.5 Cr tied up |
| Funding cost @ ~8.5% (post-hike CP/bank blend) | ₹0.72 Cr |
| Tax @ 25% | ₹0.42 Cr |
| PAT | ~₹1.26 Cr |
| ROCE (EBIT ÷ capital) | 2.4 / (8.5 + ~0.5 fixed) ≈ **27%** (vs SISA reported 28.3%) |

- **Sensitivities:**
  - +10 WC days → capital ₹11.2 Cr → ROCE ~21%.
  - GM −50 bp → EBITDA ₹1.9 Cr → ROCE ~21%.
  - A 1% bad-debt write-off (₹1 Cr) wipes out ~80% of the deal's PAT.
