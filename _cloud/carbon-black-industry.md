## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **Rain reports on a calendar year; PCBL and Himadri on April–March.**
   - "FY26" means different twelve months: PCBL/Himadri FY26 = Apr-25→Mar-26; Rain's latest full year is **CY25 = Jan-25→Dec-25** (screener column "Dec 2025"). Rain's "Q2 2026" = Apr–Jun 2026 — the same months as PCBL/Himadri's **Q1 FY27**. Rain's "Q3 2026" (due 6-Nov) = Jul–Sep 2026 = Himadri's **Q2 FY27**.
   - **Aggregates in Part 3 use two stated bases:** (a) **latest reported fiscal year** (FY26 for PCBL/HSCL, CY25 for Rain — a three-month offset, flagged every time); (b) **aligned trailing-12-months to Jun-2026** (Jul-25→Jun-26 for all three, built from screener quarters; Himadri's newer Q2 FY27 is shown separately). Basis (b) is the cleaner one for any "sector" number; prefer it.
   - Many web pages label Rain's Oct–Dec 2025 quarter "Q2 FY26" (an Indian-FY reflex) — **never mix those with CY quarter labels** [V-snip: search results showed both].
2. **Rain makes no furnace carbon black. Why is it in this bucket?**
   - Rain's segments: **Carbon** (~75% of revenue: calcined petroleum coke [CPC] for aluminium anodes; coal-tar distillation → coal tar pitch [CTP] for anodes/graphite electrodes, plus "other carbon products" including **carbon black oil [CBO]** sold to carbon-black makers); **Advanced Materials** (~20%: Rütgers' resins, chemical intermediates, engineered products incl. battery-anode coatings; mainly Germany/Belgium/Canada); **Cement** (~6%: Rain Cements, Andhra Pradesh/Telangana) [V screener profile "Carbon (75%) … used to manufacture aluminium, graphite electrodes, carbon black and titanium dioxide"; primer].
   - The link to carbon black is **feedstock**: Rain's distillation sells CBO into the carbon-black value chain. In Q2 CY26 "lower calcination volumes were partly offset by higher carbon black oil sales in Carbon distillation" [V Rain Q2 CY26 Q&A]. Arihant: "Distillation volumes rose on stronger carbon black oil demand" [V-broker].
   - The real economic overlap with the other two is **coal-tar chemistry** (Himadri is India's #1 coal-tar distiller/pitch maker; Rain is the world's #1 pitch maker) and **"industrial carbon" exposed to the same crude/coal-tar/freight shocks**. The NSE-750 classification is "Chemicals || Carbon Black"; the writer should say plainly that Rain is a **carbon-materials (CPC/CTP) company** classified here, and that only a sliver of its revenue (CBO) is literally carbon-black-linked. **Do not compute "carbon black market share" including Rain.**
   - Consequence: the "sector" in this report is really three businesses — **furnace carbon black (PCBL ~80% of revenue; Himadri ~40–50% [E])**, **coal-tar distillation/pitch (Himadri, Rain)** and **calcined coke (Rain)** — plus diversifications (Aquapharm phosphonates; battery materials; cement; tyres).
3. **Himadri is not a carbon-black pure-play either.** Its eight businesses: coal tar pitch, carbon black (incl. specialty), naphthalene/SNF/PCE, specialty oils, advanced carbon, power, plus battery materials (anode, LFP cathode, CNT) and now tyres [V presentation]. **Himadri does not disclose revenue by product.** Any "carbon black share of Himadri revenue" is an estimate (we use ~40–50% [E] — 250 ktpa CB capacity × ~85% utilisation × ~₹1.0–1.1 lakh/t ≈ ₹2,100–2,400 Cr of FY26's ₹4,661 Cr).
4. **Himadri's "sales volume" is ALL products, standalone.** 564,206 t in FY26 and 146,806 t in Q2 FY27 is pitch + carbon black + oils + naphthalene + SNF etc. [V presentation footnote "Sales volume figures are reported on a standalone basis"]. EBITDA per tonne computed from it (~₹17,800/t FY26; ~₹21,000/t Q2 FY27 standalone) is a **blended Himadri number, not comparable** with PCBL's carbon-black EBITDA/t.
5. **PCBL's "carbon black EBITDA per tonne" includes power.** Management's figure (₹14,939/t FY26 per primer; ~₹14,900 per Q4 call; **₹22,900/t Q1 FY27** [V call]) ≈ (consolidated EBITDA − Aquapharm EBITDA) ÷ carbon black tonnes: FY26 (1,081 − 162) ÷ 618,956 t = **₹14,850/t** [E check]; Q1 FY27 (400 − 47) ÷ 153,513 t = **₹23,000/t** [E check]. ## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Carbon black is a spread business tied to tyres, priced off crude, and set at the margin by Chinese and Russian supply.**
  - ~65% of Indian carbon black goes into tyres (CRISIL: "nearly 65%" / "60–65%") [V CRISIL]; globally ~70% tyres + ~20% other rubber goods + ~10% specialty [K, industry convention].
  - Feedstock (CBFS) is ~65–75% of the selling price; tyre-major contracts use **formula pricing with a one-quarter lag**; ~1/3 of PCBL's volume is spot [V PCBL calls]. The profit is a **₹/tonne conversion spread** (PCBL FY26 ~₹14,900/t; Birla Carbon India FY25 ₹13,273/t) plus tail-gas power [V].
- **FY26 was the spread trough; the West Asia oil shock of 2026 produced a windfall quarter, not a new normal.**
  - PCBL FY26: record volume **618,956 t (+3.8%)** but EBITDA **₹1,081 Cr (−22%)**, PAT **₹198 Cr (−54%)**; CB EBITDA/t fell from ~₹19,350 (H1 FY25) to ~₹15,500 (H1 FY26) on "subdued spot prices… softening crude, dumping by Russia and… US tariff related uncertainties" [V CRISIL; V screener]. CRISIL moved PCBL's AA outlook to **Negative (9-Jan-2026)** [V].
  - Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026): Brent averaged **$97/bbl vs $78** in Q4 FY26; PCBL's EBITDA/t jumped to **₹22,900** on spot pricing and ~₹70 Cr of low-cost-inventory gains (₹40–50 Cr to reverse) — revenue ₹2,474 Cr (+17%), EBITDA ₹400 Cr (+23%), PAT ₹155 Cr (+65%) [V call]. Guidance for FY27: **₹16,500–17,000/t** (+14–15% on FY26) [V].
  - **Himadri** kept compounding through both: FY26 PAT ₹755 Cr (+36%); **H1 FY27 revenue ₹3,123 Cr (+43%), EBITDA ₹616 Cr (+30%), PAT ₹460 Cr (+29%)** [V press release 17-Oct-26] — but margin per rupee fell (Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin 17.9% vs 22.2%) as pass-through inflated revenue, and H1 operating cash flow turned **negative (−₹154 Cr)** on a ₹686 Cr working-capital build [V].
  - **Rain** had its best quarter in four years: Q2 CY26 adjusted EBITDA **₹9,935 Mn (19.2%)**; Carbon EBITDA/t **₹13,653** vs ₹7,756 a year earlier; net debt/EBITDA **2.77×** [V-broker].
- **The group (three members) on an aligned TTM-to-Jun-2026 basis:** revenue **₹31,989 Cr**, operating profit **₹4,915 Cr (15.4%)**, PAT **₹1,730 Cr**; m-cap **₹44,804 Cr** (22-Oct-26) [E: sums of screener]. Himadri is **16% of revenue but 59% of m-cap**; Rain is **58% of revenue but 15% of m-cap** — the market pays for battery optionality and balance-sheet quality, not tonnes.
- **India is now a net exporter of carbon black (since FY2022) and its capacity has run ahead of demand.**
  - Capacity ~2.0–2.3 mtpa (CRISIL) vs domestic consumption ~1.3–1.5 mt [E]; imports only ~8–10 kt/month (~100–120 kt/yr), China ~1,500 t/month, Russia ~1,500–2,000 t/month [V PCBL Q4 call]; exports ~350–400 kt/yr [E: PCBL ~40% of ~619 kt + Birla 10–15% + others].
  - PCBL called out **"surplus domestic capacity"** in FY26 [V Q4 call]. Birla Carbon adds 120 ktpa at Naidupeta (AP) in Q1 FY27 and PCBL plans an AP greenfield [V CRISIL].
  - **The EU ban on Russian carbon black (from 1-Jul-2024)** opened ~500 kt/yr of Western European imports (~80% formerly Russian); India now ships ~100 kt/yr there, China slightly more [V PCBL Q4 call; V-snip ERJ]. Russian tonnes were partly diverted to India (ICRA: "large influx of Russian carbon black in India… hurt domestic realisations") [V-snip].
- **No anti-dumping duty protects Indian carbon black today.** The 2015 ADD on rubber-grade carbon black from China and Russia lapsed on **31-Dec-2020** after the Finance Ministry declined DGTR's sunset-review recommendation (China $494/t; Russia $36.17/t) [V-snip ERJ/antidumping.vn]. **No 2025–26 DGTR carbon-black case was found** [search, §H]. The industry's protection is now cost/freight and trade agreements, not duties.
- **The diversification bets define valuations:** PCBL — Aquapharm phosphonates (~18% of revenue; acquired Jan-2024; goodwill ~₹3,340 Cr) and Nanovace nano-silicon anodes (₹1,000 Cr EBITDA aspiration) [V]; Himadri — LFP cathode (2,000 MTPA demo Q3 FY27; 40,000 MTPA phase I ₹1,125 Cr), anode (200 MTPA live), CNT, SSCB, stakes in Sicona/IBC/Invati, and now tyres [V]; Rain — battery-anode coatings (PETRORES), mesophase micro-beads, India CTP plant (early 2028) [V].
- **Valuations (screener 22-Oct-26):** PCBL **₹11,495 Cr, 43.4× TTM P/E, 2.9× book, ~14× EV/EBITDA**; Himadri **₹26,407 Cr, 30.9× P/E, 5.1× book, ~25× EV/EBITDA**; Rain **₹6,902 Cr, 12.9× P/E, 0.86× book, ~5.5× EV/EBITDA** [V; E for EV].

### 1.2 Value chains (inputs → customer)

1. **Furnace carbon black (PCBL, Birla, Himadri model).**
   - Feedstock oil (CBFS from refinery FCC slurry / cracker tar; or coal-tar CBO) is atomised into a refractory-lined reactor burning natural gas/air at ~1,300–1,800 °C; incomplete combustion yields carbon aggregates; quench → bag filters → pelletise → dry → bulk/bag dispatch [K].
   - **Yield ~50–65% (≈1.6–1.9 t oil per tonne CB)** depending on grade and feedstock aromaticity (BMCI) [K; E range]. Tail gas (~CO + H₂, low calorific) fires boilers/turbines: roughly **1.3–1.5 MWh of power per tonne CB** at PCBL (FY26 839 MU ÷ 619 kt) [E from primer + V volume].
   - PCBL sources **~75% of raw material from the US Gulf Coast** [V Q4 call]; CRISIL: imports ~60% of RM [V]. Freight and the rupee therefore sit inside the feedstock bill.
   - Customers: MRF, Apollo, JK Tyre, CEAT, Bridgestone India (Birla's top-5 = 63% of sales) [V CRISIL Birla]; plus global tyre majors on annual contracts; specialty customers via distributors.
2. **Coal-tar distillation (Himadri, Rain).**
   - Coal tar (~3–4% of coal charged to coke ovens) → continuous distillation → pitch (~50–55% of tar) + oils (anthracene, wash, naphthalene, creosote) [K].
   - Himadri: 600,000 MTPA distillation at Mahistikry (WB) and other sites, 292 heated tankers, port terminals at Haldia and (New) Mangalore for liquid-pitch exports to Gulf smelters [V presentation]. **Integration edge:** heavy oils from distillation feed its own CB reactors, i.e. Himadri makes much of its own CB feedstock.
   - Rain: distillation in Germany (Castrop-Rauxel), Belgium, Canada (Hamilton), USA, Russia (exited? — not verified), India (new unit planned) [K; verify]; CTP sold to smelters globally; CBO to carbon-black makers.
3. **Calcination (Rain).** Anode-grade green petcoke (low-sulphur, low-metal) from refineries (US Gulf, India, Middle East) → rotary kilns/shaft calciners → CPC; waste heat → power [primer; K]. India restricted petcoke imports on air-quality grounds; Rain's two Indian calciners (Vizag area) run at ~90%+ only after the restrictions were relaxed [V Rain Q4 CY25 Q&A].
4. **Phosphonates (Aquapharm).** Phosphorus trichloride/phosphorous acid + amines/formaldehyde → ATMP/HEDP/PBTC etc.; water-treatment, detergent and oilfield customers (P&G, Henkel, Reckitt qualification for green chelates) [V PCBL Q1 FY27 call].

### 1.3 Where value is captured
- **Feedstock access and yield** decide cost: CBFS vs CBO choice is worth ~US$150/t of oil [V PCBL]; ICRA's 2023 view was that Indian producers' CBFS route is cheaper than China's CBO (coal-tar) route [V ICRA PDF].
- **Tail-gas power** adds ~₹1,500–2,500 per tonne of CB in a normal year at PCBL [E: FY26 power external sales ~511 MU × ~₹3.5–4/kWh ≈ ₹180–200 Cr ÷ 619 kt; plus captive savings]. In Q1 FY27 power realisation spiked to ₹5.39/kWh [V].
- **Specialty mix**: specialty EBITDA/t is a multiple of rubber black (industry convention 2–4× [K/E]); Orion's specialty segment earned US$26.6 m on 52.7 kt in Q4 2025 (~$505/t) vs rubber US$28.7 m on 166.7 kt (~$172/t) — **~3× per tonne** [E from V-snip Orion].
- **Location/logistics:** port-adjacent plants (Mundra, Kochi, Chennai) minimise freight on imported oil and exports [V CRISIL].
- **Customer stickiness:** tyre-grade approvals take 6–18 months; once approved, annual volume contracts [K].

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 Trade remedies — carbon black
- **2012:** safeguard duty on carbon black from China recommended/imposed [V-snip antidumping.vn].
- **Nov-2015:** definitive ADD on "carbon black used in rubber applications" from **China PR and Russia**, five years [V-snip].
- **May-2020:** sunset review initiated on the Carbon Black Manufacturers' Association's application (PCBL, Himadri et al.) [V-snip].
- **Dec-2020:** DGTR recommended continuation — **US$494/t (China), US$36.17/t (Russia)**; **Finance Ministry declined; duty expired 31-Dec-2020** [V-snip ERJ "India decides against extending carbon black duties"]. Scope was rubber grades only (not inks/paints).
- **2021–Oct 2026:** **no ADD in force; no new carbon-black investigation found** [search; FLAG — check the DGTR initiation list and CBIC customs (ADD) notifications for 2025–26 before printing "no case"]. Himadri's SNF has had ADD protection since 2018 [primer].
- **Interpretation:** with India a net exporter and imports ~7–8% of demand, a fresh ADD case is less likely than in 2015–20, but Russian diversion in 2024–25 is the kind of "price undercutting" that could revive one [E].

### 7.2 Trade agreements and tariffs
- **India–EU FTA** concluded Jan-2026, ratification pending: EU has **no import duty on carbon black** (no direct gain) but would scrap the **4.5% duty on Indian tyres** [V PCBL Q4 call]. India–UK CETA, India–EFTA TEPA in force; Oman and New Zealand agreements [V PCBL Q1 call].
- **US tariffs:** Indian CB attracts "a materially lower tariff than competing Asia and Middle East origins"; China faces "significantly higher effective duties" [V PCBL Q1]. The US-sales share is small: ~5% of PCBL revenue; CRISIL estimated a 0.5–1.0 pp margin impact at the 50% peak, mitigated because the tariff was computed net of US-origin raw material [V CRISIL]. PCBL expects a **₹40–45 Cr IEEPA tariff refund** (PCBL + Aquapharm) after the SCOTUS ruling [V Q1 call]. **Conflict:** autocarpro says US tariffs on Indian tyres were "reduced to 18% in Feb-2026" — inconsistent with the verified macro chronology (IEEPA struck down 20-Feb-2026; Sec 122 10% to 24-Jul; 10% Sec 301 since); likely refers to the Feb-2026 India–US interim deal rate before the SCOTUS ruling. Writer should use the macro chronology [§H].
- **Section 232 (50% on aluminium derivatives):** Rain says CPC, CTP and its key raw materials are on the **exempt list**; "no direct or material impact"; indirectly positive via US smelter restarts [V Rain Q4 CY25 Q&A].

### 7.3 Environment
- CB plants are Red-category industries under CPCB; ZLD at PCBL and Himadri plants [V CRISIL; V Himadri]. Petcoke import curbs (Supreme Court/EPCA era, 2018 onward: imports allowed only for cement, calciners within quotas) constrained Rain's Indian calciners; relaxation restored ~90% utilisation [V Rain; K for the 2018 order].
- EU CBAM does not (yet) cover carbon black or tyres [K]. Himadri reports TCFD/IFRS S2-aligned climate disclosure; EcoVadis Platinum [V].

### 7.4 Battery-materials policy
- PLI for ACC (advanced chemistry cells, 50 GWh) creates domestic demand for anode/cathode materials; customs duty structure and the "non-China supply chain" pull from US IRA/FEOC rules are the demand drivers Himadri cites [K; V Himadri deck "ex-China"].

### 7.5 GST
- GST 2.0 (22-Sep-2025) cut GST on tyres from 28% to 18% [K, as part of the GST 2.0 rate rationalisation; FLAG verify] — supportive of replacement demand in H2 FY26.

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)
- **1960–2000:** PCBL (Phillips Carbon Black, Durgapur) is the national champion; Birla's Hi-Tech Carbon starts at Renukoot in 1988 [V CRISIL]; Himadri incorporated 1987, distillation from ~1990 [primer]; Rain began as a cement/calcining company in Andhra Pradesh [primer].
- **2007–2013: Rain's leveraged globalisation** — CII Carbon (US) 2007; Rütgers (Germany/Belgium) 2013 [primer; K]. This created Rain's dollar/euro debt structure.
- **2015–2020: protection era** — ADD on China/Russia rubber black (2015–2020) [V-snip].
- **2020–2022: Covid trough then boom** — PCBL's revenue jumped from ₹2,660 Cr (FY21) to ₹5,774 Cr (FY23); Rain CY22 record PAT ₹1,577 Cr; Himadri FY22 trough (PAT ₹39 Cr) [V screener]. Europe's gas crisis (2022) hit Rain's Rütgers.
- **2022–2024: Russia sanctions** — EU 10th package (Feb-2023) quota then ban from Jul-2024; Indian exports to Europe ramp; India becomes a net exporter from FY2022 [V ICRA].
- **2023–2024: diversification wave** — PCBL buys Aquapharm (completed Jan-2024; borrowings jump to ₹4,983 Cr) [V CRISIL; V screener]; Himadri buys 12.79% of Sicona (AU$10.32 m, 2023), co-acquires Birla Tyres (Oct-2023, ₹347 Cr), announces LFP plant [primer; V-snip]; Rain's CY23 loss (−₹938 Cr attributable) on impairments/energy [V screener EPS].
- **FY25–FY26: spread compression** — Russian and Chinese oversupply, crude softness, US tariff turmoil; PCBL PAT halves; Birla's EBITDA/t falls to ₹13,273 [V].
- **2026: West Asia war** — crude/freight/LPG shock from 28-Feb; formula lag and spot pricing deliver windfall Q1 FY27/Q2 CY26 margins; working capital balloons (Himadri H1 CFO negative; Rain safety stocks; PCBL "significant pressure on working capital") [V].

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert analyst leads with)

1. **The per-tonne spread, not the price, is the business — and the spread is set at the margin by Chinese and Russian surplus.**
   - PCBL's FY26 proved it: record tonnes (+3.8%), EBITDA −22%, PAT −54% [V]. Birla Carbon's EBITDA/t fell from ₹16,817 (FY24) to ₹13,273 (FY25) on "cheaper imports" [V CRISIL]. When Russia lost Europe (Jul-2024) and China cut export prices (~−21% YoY by Nov-2025), the overflow landed on India's spot market [V; V-snip].
   - Implication for the writer: every company section should open with tonnes × ₹/t and a bridge, not with revenue.
2. **Feedstock pass-through with a lag turns crude shocks into windfalls (and crude collapses into squeezes) — and into working-capital swings.**
   - Tyre-major contracts re-price quarterly; ~1/3 of PCBL volume is spot [V]. The 2026 West Asia shock (Brent ~$60 → ~$100–120; Q1 FY27 avg $97) delivered PCBL a ₹22,900/t quarter including ~₹70 Cr inventory gains [V], Rain a ₹13,653/t Carbon quarter "aided by… favourable inventory pricing" [V-broker], and Himadri record absolute EBITDA — but **cash went the other way**: Himadri H1 FY27 CFO −₹154 Cr; Rain NWC +₹1,373 Cr in CY25 and further safety stocks in Q2 CY26 [V].
3. **Tyres are the demand engine, and India's tyre-export boom is the volume story.**
   - ~65% of Indian CB goes into tyres; 60–70% of tyre demand is replacement; tyre exports ₹27,312 Cr (+9%) in FY26 and +16% in Q1 FY27; EU FTA would remove the 4.5% tyre duty [V]. Orion's complaint about "historically high" low-tier tyre imports into the Americas is the mirror image [V-snip].
4. **Capacity has run ahead of demand in India; exports are the release valve.**
   - India ~2.0–2.3 mtpa capacity vs ~1.3–1.5 mt consumption; PCBL 900 ktpa, Birla +120 ktpa (FY27) [V; E]. Without protection (ADD lapsed 2020) Indian producers must win abroad: Europe (~500 kt import gap), US (tariff advantage vs China/Russia) [V].
5. **Diversification (specialty, battery, chemicals) is what the market prices — and is still small in the P&L.**
   - Specialty ~11–13% of PCBL volume; Aquapharm ~18% of PCBL revenue but loss-making at EBIT for two quarters in H2 FY26 [V]; Himadri's battery and CNT lines are 200–2,000 MTPA scale against 564,206 t of total volume [V]. Yet Himadri trades at ~25× EV/EBITDA and PCBL at ~14× vs global CB peers at ~6–7× [E].

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Furnace carbon black:**
Revenue = Σ grades [tonnes sold × (feedstock cost pass-through per t + conversion spread per t)] + power sales (MU × ₹/kWh)
EBITDA = tonnes × EBITDA/t (all-in, incl. power) = tonnes × [realisation − (oil t/t × oil price + freight) − gas − conversion cost] + power margin.
PAT = EBITDA − depreciation − interest − tax (− acquisition amortisation for PCBL).
