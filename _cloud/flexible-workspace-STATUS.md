Indian flexible workspace: <em>long leases, short licences</em>

## Forces
1. Flex's share of office leasing: 5% in 2017, 21% in 2025, 27% in Q2 CY26 and 24% in Q3 CY26 (CBRE; C&W puts Q3 at 16%). GCCs took ~42% of 9M CY26 absorption.
2. "Three EBITDAs": Ind AS 116 versus rent-paid EBITDA, with a different definition at each company.
3. Long leases against short licences: ₹11,239 Cr of lease liabilities against net cash of ₹106 Cr excluding leases; lock-in cover 2.9× (WeWork) and ~2.1× (Smartworks); WeWork breakeven occupancy 56.6%.
4. Three operating models: premium straight lease, enterprise managed campuses, and managed aggregation plus design-and-build.
5. Ownership and governance (Embassy pledge and brand OMA, the Keppel-linked holder, sponsor exits at Awfis) and valuation.
