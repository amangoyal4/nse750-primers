## READ THIS FIRST: twelve definitional traps

1. **"Adjusted EBITDA" excludes ESOP cost (and other income) at PB Fintech and Pine Labs; Paytm's headline "EBITDA" now includes ESOP.**
   - PB Fintech FY26: **adjusted EBITDA ₹725 Cr** [primer] vs screener operating profit **₹508 Cr** [V] — the ~₹217 Cr gap is mostly ESOP [E].
   - Pine Labs FY26: **adjusted EBITDA ₹559 Cr** (excludes ESOP, other income, impairment, FX, exceptional) vs screener OP **₹358 Cr** [V datapack; V screener]. Statutory EBITDA (PBT + finance + D&A) = 138 + 84 + 270 = ₹492 Cr; the adjustments net to ~₹200 Cr, of which ESOP is the bulk [E]. Q1 FY27 ESOP **₹26 Cr** (Q1 FY26 **₹66 Cr**) [V datapack].
   - Paytm FY26 EBITDA **₹502 Cr** (screener OP ₹501 Cr) is **after** ESOP of **₹174 Cr** [primer; V screener]; "EBITDA before ESOP" ≈ ₹676 Cr [E]. FY25 included a **one-time non-cash ₹492 Cr ESOP charge** in Q4 FY25 when the founder forfeited 2.1 crore options [V-snip].
   - Zaggle "adjusted EBITDA" ₹191.6 Cr vs screener OP ₹185 Cr — small gap [primer; V].
   - **Rule:** in peer tables use screener OP ("reported EBITDA excl. other income") for everyone; show company-adjusted EBITDA in a separate column.
2. **GMV ≠ GTV ≠ TPV ≠ premium — each company's volume metric is different and not additive.**
   - **Paytm merchant GMV** (₹6.5 lakh Cr FY26; ₹7.1 lakh Cr Q1 FY27 quarter) = value of payments made to merchants on Paytm's rails (all instruments, incl. UPI) [V IR]. **Consumer UPI GTV** (₹5.9 lakh Cr Q1) is separate and overlaps.
   - **Pine Labs Platform GTV** (₹17.15 lakh Cr FY26) = DITP GTV (₹16.51 lakh Cr) + IAP GTV (₹0.64 lakh Cr); "Flow, Affordability & Transaction Processing GTV" (₹2.75 lakh Cr) is a **subset** of DITP — never add it [V datapack].
   - **AvenuesAI TPV** (₹5.04 lakh Cr FY26 [primer]; ₹1.48 lakh Cr Q1 FY27 [V-snip]) includes gateway, BillAvenue and international; the company's own **"payments net take rate" (5.3 bps Q1)** uses payments-only net revenue.
   - **PB Fintech "total insurance premium"** (₹29,934 Cr FY26; ₹8,372 Cr Q1 FY27) includes new + renewal premium, online + PB Partners (offline POSP) [primer; V inc42]. "Core online premium" (₹20,390 Cr FY26) is the subset.
   - **Zaggle** discloses no GMV; it gives cards issued (>5 Cr cumulative), users (~4 mn active) and customers (>4,000) [V transcript].
3. **Gross vs net revenue (AvenuesAI and Zaggle).**
   - AvenuesAI FY26 **gross** revenue ₹8,116 Cr (+103%) [V screener] is mostly pass-through (interchange/bank fees, and since FY26 large low-margin volume deals). Q1 FY27 gross ₹2,680 Cr (+109%) but **net revenue ₹147.5 Cr (−3%)** and payments take rate **5.3 bps (−49%)** [V-snip investywise]. **OPM 3.7–4.3% is on gross; the company's "EBITDA margin 68%" is on net revenue.** Never compare AvenuesAI's revenue with Pine Labs' or Paytm's (which are largely net).
   - **Zaggle books both Propel (rewards) and Program Fee gross.** Propel = full voucher/points value on redemption (consolidated margin ~7.1%, standalone ~5% in Q1 FY27); Program Fee = interchange-linked fee, **but 66.3% of it is paid back as cashback/incentives** to corporates (Q1 FY27; 65.7% Q1 FY26; ~69% Q4 FY26) [V transcript]. The primer's statement that the program fee is "booked net" is wrong (Primer issues).
4. **Paytm's "contribution profit" is a company measure:** revenue minus direct expenses (payment-processing charges, promotional cashback, other direct costs) — **₹1,350 Cr in Q1 FY27, 55% margin** [V IR]. **Pine Labs' "contribution margin"** = revenue − transaction-related costs − stock-in-trade/inventory change: **72.3% Q1 FY27** [V datapack]. Not comparable: Pine Labs' device sales and IAP distribution costs sit in different lines.
5. **Paytm's FY26 "clean operating profit" is a treasury profit.** FY26 PBT **₹582 Cr** vs other income **₹668 Cr** [V screener] — strip out other income (largely interest on ~₹13,300 Cr of cash/investments) and PBT is **−₹86 Cr** [E]. Q1 FY27: PBT ₹247 Cr vs OI ₹182 Cr → core PBT ₹65 Cr, **the first quarter with positive PBT ex other income** [E]. Pine Labs FY26: OI ₹133 Cr vs PBT ₹137 Cr — **97% of PBT** [E]. PB Fintech: OI ₹372 Cr = 53% of PBT ₹707 Cr [E]. All five together: other income = **67% of PBT** [E, Part 3].
6. **Paytm one-offs make FY25 and FY26 PAT non-comparable.**
   - FY25 PAT −₹663 Cr includes the **₹1,345 Cr exceptional gain** on selling ticketing (Insider/Paytm Movies) to Zomato (Q2 FY25 PAT ₹930 Cr) and the **₹492 Cr one-time ESOP charge** (Q4 FY25 PAT −₹545 Cr) [V screener quarters; V-snip].
   - FY26 PAT ₹552 Cr includes **Q2 FY26 PAT of only ₹21 Cr**, after a ~₹190 Cr provision on loans/advances to associate **First Games Technology** (real-money gaming, hit by the Aug-2025 online-gaming ban) [K; consistent with Q2 other income falling to ₹32 Cr from ~₹200 Cr run-rate in screener]. Pre-exceptional FY26 PAT ≈ ₹740 Cr [E].
   - Paytm also monetised **PayPay (Japan)** stock-acquisition rights to SoftBank (~₹2,364 Cr, 2025) [K] — check whether any gain sits in FY26 other income.
7. **PB Fintech: "Core" vs "New Initiatives", consolidated vs segment.** Core = Policybazaar online insurance + Paisabazaar online credit; New Initiatives = PB Partners (POSP), PB UAE, PB for Business, PB Healthcare-related, etc. FY26 core online premium ₹20,390 Cr at ~21% adjusted-EBITDA margin; New Initiatives margin −9% → −4% [primer]. Q1 FY27 "core adjusted EBITDA ₹222 Cr" (angelone) vs consolidated adjusted EBITDA ₹186 Cr (inc42) — the ₹36 Cr difference is New-Initiatives loss [E reconciliation].
8. **PB Fintech "renewal revenue" has two definitions.** Q1 FY27: "core renewal and trail revenue ₹1,003 Cr (+38%)" vs "core insurance renewal revenue, quarterly annualised ₹999 Cr (+48%)" [V inc42]. The primer's "renewal ARR ~₹1,126 Cr (+63%)" is a third (exit-rate) number. Quote the definition each time.
9. **Take rate = revenue ÷ volume, but which revenue?**
   - Pine Labs FY26: ₹2,711 Cr ÷ ₹17,15,000 Cr = **15.8 bps** (0.158%) [E from V].
   - Paytm FY26: payment-services revenue ₹4,646 Cr ÷ GMV ₹6.5 lakh Cr = **~71 bps gross**, but **net payment margin is ">4 bps"** after processing costs [primer; V IR]. Device subscriptions sit inside payment revenue.
   - AvenuesAI: gross revenue/TPV = 161 bps (meaningless); PAT/TPV = 5.9 bps (₹295 Cr/₹5.04 lakh Cr) [E]; company payments NTR 5.3 bps (Q1 FY27).
   - PB Fintech: insurance revenue ÷ premium ≈ 19–20% (insurance broking ~92% of revenue) [E]; total revenue ÷ premium = 22.7% overstates it because Paisabazaar/other revenue is in the numerator.
10. **Treasury/cash: company figures vs screener.** Paytm "cash balance" ₹13,315 Cr (Mar-26) / ₹13,529 Cr (Jun-26) [primer; V IR] is far above screener "investments" ₹4,501 Cr — the rest sits in "other assets" (fixed deposits, bank balances, and possibly merchant-settlement float; FLAG). Pine Labs company net cash ₹2,449 Cr (Mar-26) / ₹2,123 Cr (Jun-26) [primer; V datapack] vs screener investments of only ₹28 Cr. **Use company cash figures in prose; screener-basis net cash is useless for these companies.** Pine Labs' ₹6,952 Cr "other liabilities" include merchant/prepaid float payables (and its "days payable" of 382 is float, not supplier credit).
11. **Consolidated vs standalone and minorities.** AvenuesAI FY26 PAT: primer **~₹332 Cr** vs screener consolidated **₹295 Cr** [V] — likely a different line (PAT before minority / share of associates, or "total comprehensive income") (FLAG). Zaggle Q1 FY27 "standalone" revenue ~₹390 Cr (+18%) vs consolidated ₹423 Cr (+27.5%) — acquisitions (GreenEdge, Zagg.Money/Rio.Money, TaxSpanner, Effiasoft) supply the difference [V transcript]. Paytm's consolidated includes PPSL (PA), Paytm Money (broking), Paytm Insurance Broking, Paytm Services; **Paytm Payments Bank (49% associate) is not consolidated** [K].
12. **UPI "zero MDR" is changing, but has not changed yet.** NPCI's 15-Sep-2026 circular introduced **0.4% MDR on P2M UPI above ₹2,000** from 15-Oct-2026; on **13-Oct-2026 it was deferred to "immediately post Diwali on a date to be notified separately"** [V ThePrint/PTI]. As of 24-Oct-2026 **no MDR is being charged** and no new date exists. Any FY27 revenue estimate that includes UPI MDR is an assumption.

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **Five members, four different businesses, one common question: can an Indian fintech earn money on rails the state made free?**
  - **Merchant payments + distribution (Paytm):** FY26 revenue ₹8,437 Cr, OP ₹501 Cr (5.9%), PAT ₹552 Cr [V screener].
  - **Merchant acquiring + issuing infrastructure (Pine Labs):** ₹2,711 Cr, OP ₹358 Cr (13.2%), PAT ₹113 Cr [V].
  - **Online payment gateway, gross-revenue model (AvenuesAI):** ₹8,116 Cr gross, OP ₹351 Cr (4.3%), PAT ₹295 Cr [V].
  - **Insurance + credit marketplace (PB Fintech):** ₹6,794 Cr, OP ₹508 Cr (7.5%), PAT ₹670 Cr [V].
  - **Corporate spend management / prepaid programmes (Zaggle):** ₹1,908 Cr, OP ₹185 Cr (9.7%), PAT ₹139 Cr [V].
  - **All five: FY26 revenue ₹27,966 Cr (+43.8% YoY), OP ₹1,903 Cr (6.8%), PAT ₹1,769 Cr (vs −₹132 Cr in FY25); m-cap ₹1,74,669 Cr on 23-Oct-2026 = ~99× FY26 PAT, ~88× TTM** [E: sums of screener 24-Oct-26]. Strip AvenuesAI's gross-up and the four others grew **28.4%**.
  - **FY26 is the first year in which all five reported a positive annual PAT.** Paytm and Pine Labs did it for the first time ever; PB Fintech for the third year [V screener].
- **Two months, two regulatory shocks, opposite directions.**
  - **23-Sep-2026: IRDAI's consultation paper "Recalibrating Economics of Insurance Distribution"** proposes reinstating product-wise commission caps — new individual health **15% (IDE) / 20% (agent)**, health renewals **5% / 10%**, term multi-year year-1 **25% / 30%** — and cutting expenses-of-management ceilings (life 15% → 12.5% of GDPI; general 25% → 20%) [V inc42]. **PB Fintech fell 35.98% to ₹1,210 that day** [V inc42]; it is at **₹1,015 now (52-week low ₹945; high ₹1,964)** [V screener].   - **FY26: 241.6 bn transactions, ₹314.2 lakh Cr** (RBI PSI) [V]; Sep-2026: **24.07 bn, ₹29.37 lakh Cr** (+23% / +18% YoY), **802 mn/day** [V startuptalky citing NPCI].

### 1.2 Value chain of one ₹1,000 card swipe vs one ₹1,000 UPI scan [E/K]

- **Credit card swipe at a large merchant, MDR ~1.8–2.0%:** ₹18–20 MDR. Interchange to the issuing bank ~₹15–17 (network-set; rewards and EMI subsidies are funded from it); network fee ~₹1; acquirer/aggregator keeps **~₹1.5–3** (15–30 bps) out of which it pays device, connectivity and sales costs [K: card economics; ranges].
- **Debit card:** MDR capped since 2018 at **≤0.40% (₹200 cap) for small merchants (turnover ≤₹20 lakh) and ≤0.90% (₹1,000 cap) for others** [K: RBI Dec-2017 circular]; **RuPay debit and BHIM-UPI carry zero MDR since 1-Jan-2020** (Finance Act 2019: s.269SU Income-tax Act + s.10A PSS Act) [K].
- **UPI P2M today:** ₹0 MDR. The acquiring PA/app earns nothing on the transaction; costs (switching, PSP bank fees ~₹0.25–0.5/txn, servers) are paid out of device rentals, government incentives and cross-sell [K/E].
- **UPI P2M under the deferred NPCI framework (₹1,000 is below the ₹2,000 floor):** still ₹0. On a ₹10,000 P2M payment: 0.4% = ₹40, split ₹16 remitter bank / ₹12 payment gateway (acquirer-side PA) / ₹8 UPI app / ₹4 app's sponsor bank [E from V split]. **For a PA like CCAvenue or Pine Labs, 30% of 0.4% = 12 bps on eligible value; for a TPAP like Paytm or PhonePe, 20% = 8 bps + 4 bps to its sponsor bank; Paytm (also a PA via PPSL and a merchant acquirer) can capture more legs on its own merchants** [E].
- **PPI-on-UPI interchange:** since Apr-2023 NPCI levies up to **1.1% interchange** on merchant UPI payments >₹2,000 funded from PPIs (wallets) [K] — small but relevant to Paytm/MobiKwik wallets.

### 1.3 Where value is captured (FY26) [E]
- **Insurance distribution captures most per rupee of volume:** PB Fintech kept ~₹19–20 of every ₹100 of premium as revenue and ~₹2.2 as PAT [E: ₹670 Cr / ₹29,934 Cr].
- **Lending distribution:** Paytm's financial-services revenue (₹2,594 Cr FY26) on an estimated ₹60–70k Cr of loans distributed implies ~3.5–4.5% of disbursal value [E; disbursal value not disclosed this session — FLAG].
- **Payments capture slivers:** Pine Labs 15.8 bps of GTV as revenue and 0.7 bps as PAT; AvenuesAI ~5–6 bps PAT/TPV; Paytm net payment margin >4 bps of GMV [E/V].
- **Gift cards/prepaid** sit between: Pine Labs IAP revenue ₹874 Cr on IAP GTV ₹64,000 Cr = **1.37%** of value [E from V datapack]; IAP processing ~100% contribution margin, distribution much lower [V datapack].

### 7.3 The Sep-2026 UPI MDR framework (deferred) [V ThePrint/PTI; V sahi; V-snip angelone/moneylife]
- **Circular date:** 15-Sep-2026 (NPCI). **Intended start:** 15-Oct-2026. **Deferred:** 13-Oct-2026 by NPCI's UPI & Services Steering Committee to "immediately post Diwali on a date to be notified separately".
- **Terms:** 0.4% on P2M > ₹2,000; ₹300 cap (≥ ₹75,000); flat ₹5 per txn > ₹2,000 for railways, telecom, fuel, insurance (and agri inputs per one source); capital markets (MF, broking) 0.02% capped ₹300; P2P and P2M ≤ ₹2,000 free; small merchants (P2PM ≤ ₹1 lakh/month) exempt; **merchants may not surcharge customers**.
- **Split:** 40% customer's bank (remitter), 30% payment gateway, 20% UPI app, 10% app's sponsor bank.
- **Politics:** ~20 trade leaders met the Finance Minister on 30-Sep-2026; opposition called it a "tax"; CAIT welcomed the deferral as "timely and pragmatic" [V ThePrint]. Fintech stocks rebounded from intraday lows on the deferral [V-snip].
- **Who gains most (if implemented):** remitter banks (40%, no cost); PAs on large online merchants (CCAvenue, Razorpay, PayU, Pine Labs' Plural) — 30%; TPAPs (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm) — 20% + sponsor-bank 10%. Paytm, as both app and PA/acquirer, could stack legs on its own merchants. ## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert analyst leads with)

1. **The state made the rail free; profit lives in what you sell beside it.** UPI is ~86% of India's retail digital transactions by volume and carries zero MDR since 2020 [V RBI PSI; K]. Every member's economics therefore depend on a non-UPI layer — device rentals (Paytm, Pine Labs), EMI/affordability (Pine Labs), gift cards/prepaid (Pine Labs IAP, Zaggle), loan distribution (Paytm, Paisabazaar), insurance commission (PB). The **deferred NPCI MDR (0.4% > ₹2,000)** is the first crack in zero-MDR in six years; whether it is implemented post-Diwali is the single largest swing factor for Paytm, AvenuesAI and Pine Labs' online business.
2. **The regulator sets the price of the product.** RBI caps DLG at 5% and dictates LSP conduct; RBI's PA directions set capital and escrow rules; NPCI sets MDR and app-share caps; IRDAI is now proposing to cap distributor commissions (health new 15%, renewals 5% for IDEs). In fintech, "pricing power" is mostly regulatory forbearance. Sep-2026 showed how fast it moves: PB Fintech lost 36% in a day [V inc42].
3. **Profitability arrived via cost cuts, mix and treasury income — not via take-rate.** All five were PAT-positive in FY26, but **other income was 67% of the group's PBT** (Paytm 115%, Pine Labs 97%, PB 53%) [E from V screener]. ESOP costs (₹174 Cr Paytm; ~₹200 Cr Pine Labs [E]; ~₹217 Cr PB [E]) are excluded from the "adjusted" metrics companies headline. The quality of earnings is improving (Paytm Q1 FY27 core PBT turned positive [E]) but not yet "clean".
4. **Credit distribution is the profit engine, and it is pro-cyclical.** Paytm's financial-services revenue (+45% in Q1 FY27) and Paisabazaar (+33% disbursals) ride on lenders' appetite for unsecured small-ticket loans — the segment RBI tightened in Nov-2023 and which DLG rules constrain. The repo hike to 5.50% (Oct-2026) and any rise in delinquencies would hit this engine first.
5. **Ownership churn and foreign exits set the share-price supply.** Promoter-less registers (PB, Paytm, Pine Labs) mean founders/PE/strategics sell into every rally: Ant fully out of Paytm (Aug-2025), Berkshire out (2023), SoftBank/Tencent out or nearly out of PB, Pine Labs' PE syndicate entering lock-in expiry. PB's FIIs fell from 37% to 27% in one quarter [V screener].

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Payments platform (Paytm / Pine Labs):**
Revenue = (Device base × monthly subscription × 12) + (GMV × non-UPI share × net MDR take) + (Affordability/EMI GTV × fee) + (Loans distributed × distribution take) + other.
Profit = Revenue × contribution margin − indirect costs (people incl. ESOP, sales, tech, marketing) − D&A + other income (treasury) − tax.

**Worked example — Paytm Q1 FY27 annualised [E from V IR + V screener]:**
- Device subscriptions: 1.57 Cr × ~₹110/month [assumption] × 12 ≈ **₹2,070 Cr**.
- Net payment revenue ₹601 Cr × 4 = **₹2,404 Cr** (net of processing costs; overlaps devices — so do not add both to revenue; this is the *net* payments line).
- Financial services: ₹814 Cr × 4 = **₹3,256 Cr**.
- Total revenue run-rate ₹2,448 Cr × 4 = **₹9,792 Cr**; contribution 55% = **₹5,400 Cr**; indirect costs ≈ ₹4,590 Cr → **EBITDA ~₹810 Cr** (Q1 × 4) → D&A ~₹525 Cr, interest ~₹28 Cr → **operating PBT ~₹260 Cr** + other income ~₹730 Cr → PBT ~₹990 Cr → PAT ~₹880 Cr at ~11% tax → **~118× run-rate P/E** at ₹1,03,771 Cr m-cap.
- **UPI MDR sensitivity [E]:** if Paytm captured ₹500–800 Cr/yr of the app + gateway legs on eligible P2M value (an assumption), PAT run-rate would rise ~55–90% — that is the bull case the market priced between July and October.

**Insurance marketplace (PB Fintech):**
Revenue = New premium × first-year take + Renewal premium × renewal take + rewards/other operating revenue from insurers + credit revenue (disbursals × sourcing fee + cards × per-card fee).
Profit = Revenue − (advertising + people incl. ESOP + POSP payouts + tech) + treasury income − tax.

**Worked example — PB Fintech FY26 core insurance, illustrative [E]:**
- Total premium ₹29,934 Cr; assume new premium ~₹18,000 Cr and renewal ~₹12,000 Cr [E split; not disclosed this session].
- Insurance revenue ≈ ₹6,250 Cr (92% of ₹6,794 Cr) → blended ~20.9% of premium.

**Spend management (Zaggle):**
Revenue = Program fee (card spend × interchange share, booked gross) + Propel (voucher/points value redeemed, gross) + SaaS (users × ₹/user/month).
Gross profit = Program fee × (1 − cashback ratio ~66%) + Propel × ~5–7% + SaaS × ~90%.
- Q1 FY27: 160 × 0.337 + 251 × 0.071 + 12.5 × 0.9 ≈ **₹54 + ₹18 + ₹11 = ₹83 Cr** gross profit → OP ₹31 Cr [E from V].

**Gateway (AvenuesAI):**
Profit = TPV × net take rate (5.3 bps payments, Q1 FY27) × (1 − opex share of net revenue ~32%) + other income − D&A − tax.

## 4.5 New insights and new primer issues

### 4.5.1 Insights the first pass missed (expert-level; for the report's argument)

1. **The state is playing both hands on UPI economics — and the law has already moved.**
   - In Aug-2026 Parliament amended **s.10A of the PSS Act**, swapping the hard-coded zero-charge guarantee for a **government notification power**. NPCI's circular No. 237 followed on 15-Sep.
   - At the same time the Budget still funds **₹2,000 Cr** of UPI/RuPay incentive for FY27, after spending **₹2,196 Cr** in FY26 against a ₹437 Cr budget line.
   - So the Diwali deferral is a timing decision on a power that now exists — not a reversal. The "incentive collapse" story in Part 1 is wrong.
   - For modelling: an incentive-funded floor (~₹2,000 Cr/yr across banks/PSPs) plus an MDR option on high-value P2M. Not either/or.
2. **For PB Fintech the IRDAI paper is a general-insurance (health) problem, not a life problem, and it is an FY28 problem, not FY27.**
   - PB's own NPV read: life roughly unchanged; **GI revenue to one-third–40% of today**.
   - The regulator's evidence (broker retail-health commission ~10% → ~30%, FY23–25) shows exactly which pool it is targeting. PB's renewal annuity is mostly health (IDE renewal cap 5%), so the "compounding renewal book" thesis is the most exposed part of the story.
   - Mitigants are real but partial:
     - Volume elasticity of ~1 (PB's estimate: 15–20% back).
     - A contact centre costing ~20% of revenue, after hiring ~6,000 people in H1.
     - The +10%/+20% under-served uplift, which favours PB Partners (78% of its GWP is Tier-2/3).
     - Service revenue via separate group companies — but the paper counts "all remuneration, direct or indirect" as commission, so routing insurer payments through affiliates will be scrutinised (cost audits).
3. **PB's credit engine was smaller and riskier than headlines implied.**
   - FY26 disbursals of ₹30,740 Cr were inflated by PB Connect (New Initiatives, B2B2C). That book has now collapsed: **Q1 FY27 total ₹4,366 Cr, −38% YoY**, while core Paisabazaar grew 33% from a much smaller base and is only at operating break-even.
   - Commission expense (₹1,934 Cr, +50%) is the POSP/Connect payout line — the same channel the IRDAI paper "challenges".
4. **Paytm's profit is still mostly treasury, and management expects it to stay that way through FY27.**
   - Other income is guided "broadly steady" (₹182 Cr in Q1 = 74% of PBT).
   - The cash pile is reported conservatively — net of ₹5,119 Cr escrow and client money. The **growth of escrow/nodal balances (₹2,832 Cr → ₹5,119 Cr in a year)** is itself evidence of rising merchant float after the PA restructuring.
   - The legal tail is smaller than headlines:
     - ED/FEMA: most of the ₹611 Cr is resolved (~₹72 Cr residual [E]).
     - First Games: the ₹704 Cr GST (+ equal penalty) sits in a liquidating JV with nil carrying value. But Paytm *raised* its stake to 82.6% by converting the loan in Apr-2026; whether it would support FGTPL is a governance question for the writer to pose, not answer.
5. **AvenuesAI is telling investors that volume will not become earnings in FY27.**
   - Payments net take rate fell from 11.7 bps (FY25) to 7 bps (FY26) to 5.3 bps (Q1 FY27). Net revenue was flat-to-down while gross revenue doubled.
   - The company guided **FY27 EPS of ₹8.75–9.50 against an FY26 actual of ₹9.42**, explicitly reinvesting accruals in AI.
   - Its headline "adjusted PAT ₹332 Cr" excludes fair-value movements.
   - Its contingent liabilities include an **upstream ₹400 Cr corporate guarantee** from a step-down subsidiary for the parent's bank facility.
   - The 0.85× P/B is the market's answer to all of this.
6. **Pine Labs' reported-profit swing is mostly ESOP roll-off, and its "GTV slowdown" is a definitional artefact.**
   - Quarterly ESOP fell from ₹66 Cr to ₹26 Cr (Q1 FY26 → Q1 FY27) — larger than the whole Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹20 Cr.
   - The +4% GTV reflects one bill-pay client in-sourcing per-transaction-priced volume; India DITP grew 20–25%.
   - Watch: CM dilution from distribution-led international entry and merchant-bought terminals, and a **₹214 Cr GST contingent** (gift-card input credit) under a Karnataka HC stay.
   - The "Nov-2026 lock-in cliff" in Part 1 does not exist; the six-month unlock passed in May-2026.
7. **Zaggle has chosen cash over growth, and it has unused money to do it.**
   - Management is deliberately slowing program fees (~+10%), trimming cashback, shedding cash-hungry Propel clients and capitalising less. Reported margins will look worse before OCF looks better.
   - **₹383 Cr of unused QIP cash ≈ 19% of its ₹2,022 Cr market cap**, against negative OCF and a 67% cashback ratio. The bull and bear cases both hinge on whether that cash funds acquisitions (DICE-style) or covers working capital.
8. **Domestic institutions are the marginal buyer of every member's regulatory sell-off.**
   - PB: ICICI Pru MF crossed 5% after the IRDAI shock.
   - DIIs rose at PB, Paytm and Pine Labs in Jun→Sep-26 while FIIs sold PB and Paytm.
   - The MDR circular itself moved Paytm only −4.3% then +3.5%: the market is pricing Paytm on lending and operating leverage, not on UPI MDR.
