# Pharmacy Retail — primer issues

## From Pharmacy Retail research (2026-11-14, pharmacy-retail-industry.md §3.4)

- **medplus-health.html — the central "convergence" thesis misreads the company's own disclosure (§03, §10, §11 bull case, bottom line):** the primer says the blended ~5.3% margin "should climb toward the mature one [~11.9%]" and that the gap "is the drag from young, sub-scale stores." The ~11.9% is a **store-level** EBITDA before warehouse, logistics, HO and marketing costs (~5–6% of revenue). The company's own bridge shows 12+-month stores earn **6.7% fully allocated (Q4 FY26: ₹113.3 Cr)** and only **4.3% in Q1 FY27**; young stores (−₹3.6 Cr) and pre-operative costs (−₹7.6 Cr) are a small drag (~₹30–45 Cr a year, ~0.5–0.7% of revenue). Rewrite the "one number that settles the thesis" box: the realistic ceiling at today's cost structure is ~6–7%, and the levers are PL mix, discounting and wage/overhead leverage, not store ageing. Also soften "Slow the store openings and the blended margin would rise almost immediately" (it would rise ~50–70 bps, not ~600).
- **medplus-health.html — private label described as "climbing" (§04, whatsnew, bull case):** PL share has fallen since Q4 FY25: **23.3% (Q4 FY25) → 21.9% (Q4 FY26) → 20.0% (Q1 FY27) → 21.4% (Q2 FY27, re-based)**; PL pharma 13.6% → 10.7%. The CEO admitted staff "may have gone a little overboard" pushing PL and annoyed customers; target is +0.25–0.5 pp per quarter. Q2 FY27 gross margin was **25.1% (−110 bps YoY)**. The "~13% around IPO" point is approximate and unsourced. PL SKUs now **1,750+** (not 1,550+). Also, the primer says PL "ran ~22% of revenue by late FY26" — correct for Q3 FY26 only; Q4 FY26 was 21.9%.
- **medplus-health.html — valuation stale (cover, §10, bull/bear):** "~₹9,600 Cr market cap" and "~39× P/E" → **₹7,440 Cr, 35.2× TTM P/E, P/B 3.6×** (13-Nov-2026, ₹619; 52-week ₹566–1,022; −21% over 1 year).
