## Conventions used throughout

- **Tags:** **[V]** = verified this session from a fetched page (screener.in via fetch script, company filing/presentation PDF, broker PDF or news page actually fetched); **[K]** = known from memory or from a search-result snippet only, with source named (treat as unverified); **[E]** = estimate or composite calculated here from [V] inputs.
- **Screener basis:** all "Snapshot" P&L/BS/ratio figures come from `https://www.screener.in/company/<NSE>/consolidated/` fetched 13-Oct-2026 [V]. Screener "Sales" = revenue from operations; "Operating profit" (OP) = sales − expenses, **excluding other income**. For developers this differs a lot from company-reported EBITDA, which usually *includes* other income, interest income on deposits and (for Godrej) JV/partner income. Both are shown where available.
- **Screener PAT** = consolidated net profit including minority interest and share of JV/associate profit. For Phoenix, Prestige and Godrej the profit attributable to owners is materially lower; this is flagged per company.
- **Net debt:** screener "Borrowings − Investments" ignores cash and bank deposits (which sit inside "Other assets") and treats strategic investments (e.g. DLF's DCCDL stake) as liquid. The **company-reported net debt / net cash is the more credible number and is used as the primary figure**; the screener-derived number is shown only for transparency.
- **EV/EBITDA** = (m-cap 13-Oct-2026 + company net debt, or − net cash) ÷ EBITDA. The basis (screener TTM OP or company EBITDA) is stated each time. Minority interests are not added, so the figure is a simple, uncorrected one.
- **Pre-sales vs revenue:** Ind AS 115 recognises developer revenue on handover (completed contract), so revenue lags bookings by 3–5 years. **Pre-sales (bookings), collections, net debt and launch pipeline are the real-time KPIs.** P/E is a weak tool for this sector.
- **Listing status:** all seven companies remain listed on NSE and BSE as of 13-Oct-2026 (live screener pages carry Jun-2026 or Sep-2026 quarter shareholding). **Anant Raj has a board-approved demerger** of its data-centre/cloud arm into Ashok Cloud Pvt Ltd (1:1 entitlement, separate listing intended). This is flagged in its section. No mergers or delistings are pending for the other six.
- **Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026):** as of 13-Oct-2026 no member in this batch had published a full Q2 FY27 operational update that could be found and fetched. Searches turned up only smaller peers (Keystone, Max Estates, Raymond Realty) reporting so far. Where a Q2 data point exists (DLF The Aureva sell-out; Oberoi 360 North bookings; PL Research Q2 previews), it is given and tagged.

- **DCCDL (equity-accounted, 66.67%):** ~44.6 msf offices (Cyber City, Cyber Park, Downtown Gurugram/Chennai) and ~4.9 msf retail (Mall of India, Promenade, Emporio). DCCDL revenue mix in Q1 FY27: office rent ₹1,189 Cr, retail rent ₹255 Cr, services and other ₹457 Cr [V].
- **Concentration:** the bulk of FY26 bookings came from Gurugram luxury (Privana, Dahlias). Dahlias alone is ~₹18,500 Cr cumulative [K, primer sources]. Geographic concentration in NCR is very high.

### Capex / expansion & guidance
- FY27 bookings guidance ₹20,000 Cr. Management "will not chase higher pre-sales for their own sake" and prioritises margins and cash flow [V multibagg FY26].
- Rental expansion pipeline ~27 msf. Three retail launches in FY27 (Midtown Plaza Delhi, Summit Plaza Gurugram, Promenade Goa). Atrium Place (JV with Hines) is fully leased [V].
- DCCDL FY26 capex ₹2,467 Cr [V]. Data centres: DLF will build the shells but will not operate the technology [V].
- Management calls FY28 a P&L inflection as completed-contract revenue from Arbour onwards starts to flow [V].

### Story bullets
1. **Bookings hiatus, not a demand collapse.** Q1 FY27 bookings were ₹657 Cr (−94%) because approvals were delayed. The Aureva (₹1,985 Cr sold out in October) and the next DLF City launch are needed to reach the ₹20,000 Cr guide, so H2 FY27 is heavily back-ended.
2. **Fortress DevCo balance sheet:** net cash ₹15,200 Cr in Jun-26 (~9% of m-cap), although 74% of it is RERA-escrowed. Surplus-cash potential is ~₹43,650 Cr from sold and launched inventory.
3. **The annuity compounds.** DCCDL PAT grew 38% in FY26 and 21% in Q1 FY27. FY27 exit rentals are guided at ₹7.3–7.5k Cr, with a ~₹10k Cr medium-term ambition. DCCDL leverage is 0.18–0.19× GAV.
4. **Derating and ownership rotation:** FIIs cut from ~16.7% to 11.4% in two years while DIIs doubled. The P/E is still ~38× and the stock is ~15% below its 52-week high of ₹786. Goa and other legal/PIL risks are an emerging watch item.
