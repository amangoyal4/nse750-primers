## The forces
1. Value-add share (bought-in materials per ₹100, from ~25 at Azad to ~78 at Syrma) decides margins, working capital and pass-through risk.
2. Policy-made demand overshoots. Ethanol capacity is ~1,970 cr litres against ~1,100 cr needed, and Praj's ROCE fell from 31% to 6%. ECMS and semiconductors carry the same risk.
3. Global energy and aerospace demand is limited by supply (gas turbines, aero engines, grid), and it is pulling Indian precision and T&D suppliers.
4. Indian private capex is broadening, not booming: RBI seasonally adjusted capacity utilisation has been ~75% for five quarters.
5. Valuations are far ahead of cash conversion: ~73× TTM P/E, and the fastest growers have negative CFO.
