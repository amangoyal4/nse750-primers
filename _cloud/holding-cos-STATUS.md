## Forces
1. The holding-company discount is structural and widening (CFHL ~60% to SOTP / ~57% to the CIFC stake alone, from ~53–54% in June; REL ~37% base, range 5–50%).
2. Look-through vs reported earnings (CFHL ₹5,485 Cr PAT incl. NCI vs ₹2,441 Cr attributable; REL ₹86 Cr attributable vs ~₹357 Cr look-through because Care is consolidated on IGAAP 1/n).
3. Regulators hold the unlock keys (RBI CIC rules incl. 60% dividend cap and two-layer cap; RBI's Aug-2026 refusal of REL's demerger; IRDAI's 30-Jul-2026 holdco-into-insurer amalgamation rule).
4. The jewels' cycles are out of phase (CIFC strong; Chola MS CoR 115–120%; Care +42% growth on 1.58× solvency).
5. Capital flows down the chain, not up (CIFC QIP board 28-Oct; Care rights/sub-debt; ₹881 Cr of REL warrants under water; ₹24 Cr dividends paid in total).
