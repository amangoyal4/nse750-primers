## READ THIS FIRST: ten definitional traps

1. **"India's gaming market" is three different numbers depending on whether real-money gaming (RMG) is in.**
   - **Including RMG (pre-ban):** WinZO–IEIC put 2024 at **₹31,938 Cr (~US$3.7–3.8bn), of which RMG 85.7% = ₹27,438 Cr** [V Outlook Business 22-Dec-2025 citing the Mar-2025 report]. Lumikai put **FY24 at US$3.8bn (FY23 US$3.1bn)** [V-snip Outlook Business]. FICCI–EY put **online gaming 2024 at ₹23,200 Cr (₹232 bn), 77% transaction-based (RMG)** — PIB repeats this figure in its 30-Apr-2026 backgrounder [V PIB PDF].
   - **Excluding RMG (post-ban reality):** Lumikai's CY25 report (Mar-2026): **India "video games" market US$1.5bn, +17% YoY**; the RMG category it removed was **US$2.3bn** [V Outlook Respawn 23-Mar-2026]. Naavik/Sensor Tower: non-RMG **mobile** IAP + ads ≈ **US$1.1bn in 2025 (US$0.8bn IAP + US$0.3bn ads)** [V-snip]. FICCI–EY 2025: **"esports and casual gaming" ₹5,300 Cr in 2024 → ₹6,300 Cr 2025 → ₹8,300 Cr 2026 (projection)** [V-snip].
   - **Rule for the writer:** never compare a pre-ban "US$3.8bn" with a post-ban "US$1.5bn" as if the market shrank 60% — the former contains ~US$2.3bn of RMG platform revenue (GGR net of bonuses), which the law abolished. The surviving market grew ~17% in CY25.
2. **RMG revenue has three layers: face value (deposits/stakes) ≫ GGR (rake) > net revenue.**
   - A fantasy contest with ₹100 of entry fees typically returns ₹85–90 as prizes; the platform's **GGR (rake / platform fee) is ₹10–15** [K industry norm; E]. Dream Sports FY25: **GGR ₹10,284 Cr but net operating revenue ₹6,759 Cr** after promotional credits/discounts [V Entrackr 09-Feb-2026, RoC].
   - GST history: 18% on GGR until 30-Sep-2023; **28% on the full face value of deposits from 1-Oct-2023** — on a ₹100 deposit that is ~₹21.9 of tax (28/128) versus ~₹2.7 under the old regime on a ₹15 rake, i.e. **~8× the tax** [E arithmetic]. The Supreme Court in May-2026 upheld the levy on face value and its retrospective reach (see §7).
3. **Nazara's Q2 FY27 onward will not be comparable with anything before it.** From Q2 FY27 Nazara consolidates Bluetile/BestPlay (Q1 FY27 standalone: revenue ₹518 Cr, EBITDA ₹55 Cr) on top of its own Q1 FY27 revenue ₹429 Cr and EBITDA ₹46 Cr — the company's own "illustrative" aggregation is **₹947 Cr revenue and ₹101 Cr EBITDA per quarter** [V Q1 FY27 deck, labelled "not a forecast or pro forma"]. Revenue will roughly double; margin will be ~10–11% because Bluetile spends **85% of revenue on UA** [V].
4. **NODWIN (esports) left Nazara's consolidated numbers on 13-Aug-2025.** FY26 reported revenue ₹1,829 Cr includes ~4.5 months of NODWIN; Q3 FY26 onward has none. Company says **Q4 FY26 revenue −24% reported but +8% ex-NODWIN; Q1 FY27 −14% reported but ~+9% ex-NODWIN** [V]. Use "comparable" growth in prose and say so. NODWIN's own FY26 revenue was **₹658 Cr (FY25 ₹524 Cr), EBITDA ₹21 Cr (FY25 −₹14 Cr)** [V FY26 deck] — none of which is now in Nazara's revenue; Nazara books ~47% of NODWIN's **net** profit/loss as "share of associates".
5. **Screener's "operating profit" for Nazara is NOT the company's EBITDA.**
   - Screener quarterly OP: Q1 FY26 ₹23 Cr, Q2 FY26 **−₹164 Cr**, Q3 ₹68 Cr, Q4 ₹44 Cr, Q1 FY27 **−₹38 Cr**; FY26 **−₹28 Cr** [V screener].

## 0. Top-line framing (what an expert leads with)

- **One law split the industry in two.** The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (passed Lok Sabha 20-Aug-2025, Rajya Sabha 21-Aug-2025, assent 22-Aug-2025; Act and Rules in force **1-May-2026**) bans every "online money game" — skill, chance or mixed — plus its advertising and payment facilitation, while promoting e-sports and "online social games" [V PIB backgrounders 21-Aug-2025 and 30-Apr-2026].
  - **What was abolished:** a ~₹27,000 Cr (2024) RMG revenue pool — 85.7% of industry revenue by WinZO–IEIC's count, 77% by FICCI–EY's [V]. Lumikai sizes the removed category at **US$2.3bn** [V].
  - **The government's case:** "according to an estimate, **45 crore people** are negatively affected by online money games and faced a loss of **more than ₹20,000 crores**" (MeitY minister Ashwini Vaishnaw, Rajya Sabha) [V PIB]. **1,524** betting/gambling websites and apps were blocked between 2022 and June 2025 [V PIB].
  - **The survivors:** casual, mid-core, PC/console, kids, esports, gaming ad-tech — a **US$1.5bn market growing 17%** in CY25 (Lumikai) [V], dominated by global franchises (**10 of India's top-10 grossing games are global IPs**) [V].
- **The RMG unicorns went from India's most profitable consumer internet companies to near-zero revenue in one week of August 2025.**
  - Dream Sports FY24 PAT **₹1,295 Cr** → FY25 loss **₹479 Cr** (exceptionals) on ₹6,759 Cr revenue; CEO Harsh Jain: **"95% of revenues and 100% of profits"** gone after the ban [V Entrackr; V-snip interview]. Gameskraft FY25 revenue ₹3,896 Cr, PAT ₹706 Cr (EBITDA margin 24.9%) — its last full year [V Entrackr].
  - Industry estimates of **>2 lakh direct and indirect jobs** affected; layoffs at Gameskraft, Head Digital Works (A23), PokerBaazi, Games24x7, MPL, Zupee, Probo; Hike shut entirely (Sep-2025); Flutter shut Junglee (expected ~US$200m revenue, ~US$50m adj. EBITDA in 2025) [V Outlook Business 22-Dec-2025].
  - Then the tax bill arrived: in **May-2026 the Supreme Court upheld 28% GST on the full face value of stakes, retrospectively** (bench J.B. Pardiwala, R. Mahadevan) [V Inc42 27-May-2026]. Demands outstanding: **₹1.12 lakh Cr across 71 notices (FY23–FY24) per FinMin to Parliament (Dec-2023)** [V-snip]; Vision IAS cites **~₹91,685 Cr online gaming + ₹16,820 Cr casinos**; Dream Sports alone faces **>₹28,000 Cr**, Gameskraft **₹21,000 Cr** [V-snip]. The constitutional challenge to PROGA itself is pending before a three-judge bench (CJI Surya Kant) [V Outlook Respawn 19-Aug-2026].
- **Nazara is the only listed pure-play and it was on the right side of the ban — except for one ₹1,000 Cr bet.**
  - Its ~₹832 Cr [K] 2024 stake in Moonshine (PokerBaazi) was written down by **₹914.7 Cr in Q2 FY26** and to zero in Q1 FY27 [V]. That is roughly **one-quarter of Nazara's Mar-2025 net worth (₹2,863 Cr)** [E].
  - The rest of the portfolio (Fusebox's Love Island game, Kiddopia, Animal Jam, Curve's Human: Fall Flat, Datawrkz ad-tech, Sportskeeda, Smaaash/Funky Monkeys, NODWIN as associate) is non-RMG and earns **~75–80% of revenue overseas** [E: Fusebox, Kiddopia, Animal Jam, Curve, Space & Time, Sportskeeda/PFN are US/UK-facing; see §9] — so the **weak rupee is a reported-earnings tailwind**.
- **Nazara is being re-made into a global casual-games operator run by a foreign founder-CEO.** The US$303m all-cash buy of Bluetile/BestPlay (CY25 EBITDA ₹254 Cr ≈ Nazara FY26 EBITDA ₹255 Cr), Raymond Stauffer as CEO, the founder stepping back to non-executive, a new CFO and a ₹731 Cr preferential issue to the sellers (Aug–Oct 2026) [V]. The market approves: stock **₹402, near its 52-week high (₹420), +49% in a year**; m-cap **₹16,425 Cr** [V screener].
- **Valuation is rich on every trailing measure, reasonable only on the pro-forma.** M-cap/FY26 company EBITDA **~64×**; P/E n.m. on TTM loss (~200× FY26 reported, ~100× "core" PAT) [E]. Pro-forma with Bluetile (FY26 EBITDA ₹255 Cr + CY25 ₹254 Cr = ₹509 Cr; EV adjusted for the US$303m cash outlay and the ₹731 Cr raise) → **EV/EBITDA ~35×, EV/sales ~5.5×** [E] vs global listed game companies at **4–10× EBITDA** (Krafton 4.0×, Embracer 4.9×, Playtika 4.5×, NetEase 8.0×, Nexon 10.0×, Tencent 10.0×) and outliers Take-Two 52× / Roblox (loss-making, 6.6× sales) [V SA 23-Oct-26].

### 1.2 Value chain (mobile free-to-play, the dominant format)

1. **IP / concept** → licensed TV format (Love Island from ITV), owned IP (Animal Jam), evergreen genre (solitaire, mahjong, yatzy).
2. **Development** → engine (Unity, Unreal), art, level design; AI now writes code and content (Bluetile: **90–95% of data architecture and 80% of back-end code AI-developed; 5 games shipped in 6 months**; Nazara's Pixel Blast built in **2 months vs a typical 12–18**) [V Bluetile deck; FY26 deck].
3. **Distribution** → Apple App Store, Google Play (platform fee **15–30%** of IAP/subscriptions [K]); Steam/console stores (~30% [K]); web shops and alternative billing growing after regulatory pressure (CCI fined Google ₹1,337 Cr + ₹936 Cr in Oct-2022 [K]).
4. **User acquisition** → paid installs on Meta, Google, TikTok, ad networks; Bluetile spends **85% of revenue on UA (Q1 FY27; 78% a year earlier)**; Fusebox ~44% (₹36 Cr on ₹82 Cr) [V Q1 FY27 deck].
5. **Monetisation** → IAP (currency, passes, bundles), subscriptions, ads (rewarded eCPM in India **US$2.43** vs blended **US$1.1**) [V Lumikai].
6. **Live-ops** → events, content drops, AI-personalised offers; retention benchmarks in India **D1 ~30%, D7 sub-10%** [V Lumikai founder quote].
7. **Audience → media/esports** → streams, tournaments, sponsorship (NODWIN, Sportskeeda).

### 1.3 Where value is captured
- **Platforms (Apple/Google) and IP owners** take the safest cut: 15–30% of every IAP rupee with no content risk [K].
- **UA networks (Meta, Google, AppLovin, Unity)** take the largest single slice in casual (Bluetile 85% of revenue) — the casual-games business is in effect an arbitrage on cost-per-install vs LTV [V].
- **Hit IP owners** earn 25–40% EBITDA margins (Curve 39%, Animal Jam 25%, NetEase/Tencent games 35–38%) [V]. Middling studios earn 10–20%; ad-tech resellers 5–6% (Datawrkz ₹26 Cr EBITDA on ₹454 Cr, 5.7%) [V].
- **Pre-ban, RMG platforms captured the most value in India** (Gameskraft 24.9% EBITDA margin; Dream11 FY24 PAT ₹1,295 Cr) because they owned the wallet, not the game — the rake on peer-to-peer stakes needed no IP, minimal content and no app-store fee (RMG apps were mostly sideloaded APKs or allowed only in limited Play Store pilots) [V; K].

## 7. Regulation and policy

### 7.1 Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (PROGA) — the defining event
- **Timeline [V PIB; V Outlook Respawn; V SAM; V galalaw]:**
  - 19-Aug-2025 Cabinet approval [V-snip Wikipedia — tagged unreliable].
  - **20-Aug-2025** introduced and passed in Lok Sabha by voice vote without debate (opposition protesting Bihar voter-roll revision) [V-snip Tribune/Deccan Chronicle].
  - **21-Aug-2025** passed by Rajya Sabha [V PIB].
  - **22-Aug-2025** Presidential assent (Act No. 32 of 2025) [V Outlook Respawn].
  - Operators shut paid play within days (Dream11, MPL, Zupee, Games24x7, PokerBaazi etc.) — **ahead of legal commencement** [V Outlook Business].
  - **1/2-Oct-2025** draft Rules published (proposing registration for all games) — date given as October 2025 by galalaw; "12-Oct-2025" by Wikipedia [V galalaw; V-snip Wikipedia]. Use "October 2025".
  - **8-Sep-2025** (approx.) Supreme Court transferred High Court challenges (Karnataka, Delhi, MP) to itself [V-snip; date per Wikipedia, year missing — flag].
  - **22-Apr-2026** Rules notified (Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026); gazette notifications bring **the Act and the Rules into force on 1-May-2026**, constitute the **Online Gaming Authority of India (OGAI)** and empower investigating officers [V SAM; V PIB 30-Apr-2026 backgrounder].
  - Between passage and commencement, enforcement ran through banks cutting payment rails, ED searches and IT Act blocking [V galalaw].
- **Core provisions [V PIB]:**
  - Complete ban on online money games — **chance, skill or both**; ban on advertising/promotion; banks and payment systems barred from processing related transactions; blocking under IT Act 2000; applies to offshore operators serving India.
  - **Penalties:** offering/facilitating — up to **3 years and/or ₹1 Cr**; financial transactions — similar; advertising — up to **2 years and/or ₹50 lakh**; repeat — **3–5 years and ₹1–2 Cr**; key offences cognisable and non-bailable. Independent/non-executive directors protected if due diligence shown.
  - **E-sports** recognised as a legitimate sport; Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports to set guidelines, academies, incentive schemes. An online money game **cannot** be registered as an e-sport under the **National Sports Governance Act, 2025**.
  - **Online social/educational games** may be recognised and registered; dedicated platforms and awareness campaigns.
- **Rules 2026 / OGAI [V PIB 30-Apr-2026]:**
  - OGAI = attached office of MeitY, digital-first, chaired by **Additional Secretary MeitY**, with Joint Secretary representation from **Home, Finance, I&B, Youth Affairs & Sports, Law & Justice**.
  - **Determination** (money game vs social game vs e-sport) on suo motu basis, application or Central notification; objective tests: payment of stakes, expectation of monetary winnings, revenue model, monetisation of in-game assets outside the game; within **90 days**; determination is **game- and provider-specific** [V].
  - **Registration** only where notified; **mandatory for all e-sports**; certificate valid up to **10 years**; criteria include risk, scale, financial transactions and **country of origin** [V].
  - User safety (age-gating, time limits, parental controls), two-tier grievance (OGAI within 30 days; second appeal to Secretary MeitY), penalties proportionate, proceedings within 90 days; penalties to the Consolidated Fund [V].
  - **Grey zone:** no deemed-clearance for social games awaiting determination; advertisers/agencies face uncertainty; rewarded/"cash-out" mechanics may be tested by the "monetisation of in-game assets outside the game" criterion [V galalaw; E]. **This matters for BestPlay** — a rewards app where "the user has an option to withdraw the rewards" [V Bluetile deck] — although BestPlay's users are predominantly overseas [E; flag for writer].
- **Litigation status (as of 19-Aug-2026, latest found):** constitutional challenge (legislative competence — betting/gambling is a State List subject, Entry 34; Article 19(1)(g)) before a **three-judge bench: CJI Surya Kant, Justices Joymala Bagchi and V. Mohana**; parties directed to complete pleadings; Solicitor General Tushar Mehta for the Union; a CASC PIL lists ~2,000 betting apps [V Outlook Respawn]. Earlier: Nov-2025 notice to Centre; Dec-2025 CJI indicated a larger bench [V-snip agbrief/storyboard18]. **No verdict found as of 23-Oct-2026** [V-snip search].

### 7.2 GST on online gaming — 18% on GGR → 28% on face value → Supreme Court (May-2026)
- **Pre-Oct-2023:** most skill-gaming platforms paid 18% on GGR/platform fee [K].
- **11-Jul-2023 (50th GST Council)** decision; **2-Aug-2023** clarification "on full face value"; law amended; **28% on deposits from 1-Oct-2023**, with review after six months [K].
- **Collections:** first six months of the new regime **₹6,909 Cr vs ₹1,349 Cr in the prior six months (+412%)**; casinos +30% to ₹214 Cr (FM after the 54th Council, Sep-2024) [V-snip Tribune/Deccan Herald]. FY25 was projected at up to ₹14,000 Cr (Revenue Secretary, Feb-2024) [V-snip]. **Annualised ~₹13,800 Cr — about half of the ₹27,438 Cr RMG revenue pool** [E] — this tax take is what the ban gave up.
- **Retrospective notices:** 71 notices worth **>₹1.12 lakh Cr for FY23–FY24** (FinMin to Parliament, Dec-2023) [V-snip]; Gameskraft **₹21,000 Cr** SCN (quashed by Karnataka HC, 2023) [V-snip].
- **Supreme Court (reported 27-May-2026; Justices J.B. Pardiwala and R. Mahadevan):** 28% valid; online gaming "involving staking upon uncertain outcomes" is **"betting and gambling for the purpose of GST"**; platforms are suppliers of actionable claims, not intermediaries; levy on **full face value**; 2023 amendments **clarificatory → retrospective demands survive**; Arts 366(12)/(12A) not breached [V Inc42; V-snip Quint/Vision IAS]. Reported totals: **~₹91,685 Cr online gaming + ₹16,820 Cr casinos** (Vision IAS) vs ">₹2.5 lakh Cr" (Quint) — **unreconciled; use the official ₹1.12 lakh Cr notice figure and say "over ₹1 lakh crore"** [E judgement].
- **Implications:** Delta Corp's screener TTM PAT swung to **−₹289 Cr** (FY26 PAT ₹85 Cr) [V screener] — consistent with GST provisioning after the judgment [E, cause not verified]. Nazara says its exposure to Moonshine's tax liabilities "is limited to the extent of our investments… written off completely" [V 04-Aug-2026 call].

## 8. History and cycles (narrative; full timeline in §G)

- **1999–2012: telco VAS era.** Nazara (founded 1999 by Vikash Mittersain) sold Java/feature-phone games via operator portals; revenue share with telcos 30–50% [K].
- **2016–2019: Jio data shock.** Free data (Sep-2016) created hundreds of millions of mobile gamers; PUBG Mobile (2018) and Ludo King (2016; lockdown 2020 surge) defined the era; Dream11 became India's first gaming unicorn (Apr-2019) [K].
- **2017–2021: Nazara builds the "friends of Nazara" portfolio** — NODWIN (2017–18), Kiddopia/Paper Boat Apps (2019), Sportskeeda/Absolute Sports (2019–20), Halaplay/Qunami (fantasy/quiz, small) [K]; IPO **30-Mar-2021** (OFS only, ~₹583 Cr; listed at ~₹1,550 vs ₹1,101 issue) [K].
- **2020–2022: China app ban and the RMG boom.** PUBG Mobile banned (Sep-2020); Free Fire banned (Feb-2022); BGMI launched (Jul-2021), banned (Jul-2022), returned (May-2023) [K]. RMG raised ~₹25,900 Cr FDI [V]; Dream11 FY24 PAT ₹1,295 Cr [V]; MPL, Games24x7, Zupee, WinZO, Gameskraft became unicorns or near-unicorns [K].
- **2022: Nazara buys WildWorks (Animal Jam, US; ~US$10m) and Datawrkz (ad-tech)** [K]; stock peaks then de-rates through 2022–23 [K].
- **2023: the 28% GST shock.** Council decision Jul-2023; effective 1-Oct-2023; ₹1.12 lakh Cr of retrospective notices [V-snip]. RMG firms cut marketing and staff; Gameskraft FY25 PAT −25% "due to full-year GST effect" [V].
- **2024: Nazara's biggest deal year.** Fusebox Games (UK, Aug-2024; ~₹235 Cr [primer]); **Moonshine/PokerBaazi associate stake (~₹832 Cr [K], Jun–Aug 2024)**; Space & Time (UK ad-tech, consolidated Oct-2024); Curve Games (UK, signed 2024, consolidated 1-Apr-2025; ~₹247 Cr [primer]); preferential raises (equity issued ₹843 Cr in FY25 cash flow) [V FY26 deck].
- **Aug-2025: PROGA.** Ban passed 20–22 Aug; Nazara stock fell on the day [primer; K]; Nazara scrapped its follow-on PokerBaazi stake purchase [V-snip]. **13-Aug-2025 NODWIN deconsolidated** (₹1,098.5 Cr fair-value gain) [V]. Smaaash acquired via IBC resolution (6-Jun-2025) [V].
- **Q2 FY26 (Nov-2025 results):** ₹914.7 Cr Moonshine impairment; consolidated loss ₹33.9 Cr; standalone loss ₹966.95 Cr [V deck; V-snip The Week]. ED raids on WinZO/Gameskraft/Pocket52 (18–22 Nov-2025) [V]. Dream Sports restructures into 8 units (Dec-2025) [V-snip].
- **Sep-2025:** split (₹4 → ₹2) + 1:1 bonus [V-snip]; promoter group reclassified to include Plutus/Khandelwal and Axana/Sacheti (promoter 8.3% → 35.45%) [V screener].
- **Mar-2026:** Bluetile/BestPlay 50% deal (US$100.3m) [V]; Lumikai CY25 report — ex-RMG market US$1.5bn (+17%) [V].
- **Apr–May 2026:** PROGA Rules notified (22-Apr) and in force with the Act (1-May) [V]; **SC upholds retrospective 28% GST (May-2026)** [V].
- **Aug–Oct 2026:** Bluetile amended to 100% for US$303m; Stauffer CEO (1-Sep); Moonshine fully written off (Q1 FY27 loss ₹82 Cr); ₹731 Cr preferential to sellers; founder to non-exec; new CFO (12-Oct) [V]. PROGA challenge pending before CJI's bench [V].

**Cycle lesson [E]:** Indian gaming has had three "regime breaks" in six years — China app bans (2020–22), GST on face value (2023), and prohibition (2025). Each wiped out the then-dominant revenue pool. Durable value has accrued only to (a) global IP owners selling into India and (b) Indian companies earning abroad.

### 9.2 Illustrative unit economics — Kiddopia (iOS) [E arithmetic on V inputs; label ILLUSTRATIVE]
- ARPU US$7.45/month; monthly churn 6% → expected 24-month revenue per subscriber = 7.45 × (1 − 0.94²⁴) ÷ 0.06 = 7.45 × 12.89 ≈ **US$96** [E].
- CAC per paying subscriber = cost per trial US$35.8 ÷ activation (trial→paid) 52% ≈ **US$68.8** [E].
- Naive LTV/CAC ≈ **1.40×** on gross revenue — below the company's reported **1.77–2.08×**, so the company's LTV must include annual-plan prepayments/renewals or a different activation base [E; flag]. After a 15–30% store fee, payback is ~12–16 months [E].
- Why it matters: subscription apps are worth acquiring customers only above ~1.5× LTV/CAC; the FY26 rise from 1.67 to 1.89 (and 2.08 in Q1 FY27) is what justified scaling UA (Q1 FY27 UA ₹28 Cr vs ₹22 Cr; revenue +19%; EBITDA ₹7 Cr vs ₹8 Cr) [V].

### 9.3 Illustrative unit economics — Bluetile-type casual publisher [V inputs; E]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹518 Cr; UA ₹438 Cr (85%); revenue − UA ₹80 Cr; EBITDA ₹55 Cr (10.7%) [V].

- The lever: dial UA down and margin rises immediately (management: "we can definitely play with the EBITDA in the future to increase the margins") but growth slows [V call]. Valuation therefore hinges on cohort ROAS, not reported margin.

### 9.4 Illustrative RMG unit economics (pre-ban) [E; Dream11-type; label ILLUSTRATIVE]
- ₹100 of contest entry → ₹85 prizes + ₹15 platform fee (GGR). Promotions/bonus cash ~₹5 → net revenue ~₹10 [E calibrated to Dream Sports GGR ₹10,284 Cr vs net ₹6,759 Cr = 66%].

## 11. Technology and disruption risks

1. **Generative AI in development and live-ops** — collapses production cost and time (Bluetile: 80% of back-end code AI-written; Pixel Blast in 2 months; AI playable ads at "near-zero marginal cost at 4–5× the volume") [V decks]. Two-edged: lowers barriers for competitors; floods stores; favours companies with UA scale and data.
2. **UA platform dependence** — Apple ATT (2021) and Google Privacy Sandbox changed targeting; any CPI inflation hits Bluetile-type models first (85% UA ratio) [K; V].
3. **Search/traffic shocks** — Sportskeeda MAU fell from 115m to ~61m after Google's March core update; FY26 revenue ₹147 Cr (EBITDA ₹18 Cr) [V]. AI search answers (zero-click) are structural risk to content sites [E].
4. **Platform policy** — app-store fees, alternative billing, kids' privacy (COPPA, DPDP), loot-box rules [K].
5. **Hit and licence risk** — Love Island = 27% of Nazara gaming revenue; Human: Fall Flat licence runs to 2030 [V]; TV-format popularity cycles.
6. **Regulatory drift** — OGAI's "monetisation of in-game assets outside the game" test could capture rewarded/cash-out mechanics (BestPlay) or skill tournaments with prizes [V PIB; E].
7. **Offshore betting and illegal apps** — 1 in 3 RMG players may have moved offshore [V indicative]; competes for attention and ad inventory.
8. **Cloud/console shifts** — Switch 2 (2025) opened a new catalogue window for Curve (Wobbly Life >300k units since Switch launch in FY26) [V; K].

## A. The forces that define this sub-sector (what an expert analyst leads with)

1. **Prohibition, not regulation, of the biggest revenue pool.** PROGA removed 75–85% of industry revenue (₹18,000–27,500 Cr in 2024 depending on the source) at a stroke, without grandfathering or a licensing alternative [V]. Every Indian gaming valuation, job and ad budget now sits on the "social game / e-sport" side of a line the OGAI will draw game by game. The constitutional challenge (legislative competence over betting/gambling) is the single largest binary for the sector; Nazara's exposure is now asymmetric (Moonshine already at zero).
2. **Tax as the precursor shock — and a retrospective bill that outlives the business.** 28% GST on face value from Oct-2023 (₹6,909 Cr in six months, +412%) halved RMG economics before the ban; the May-2026 SC ruling made **>₹1 lakh Cr of retrospective demands** enforceable against companies that no longer have revenue [V/V-snip]. This is why Dream Sports reports "a runway of a couple of years" and why Delta Corp's TTM P&L swung to a loss [V-snip; V screener].
3. **The surviving market is small, global-IP-dominated and IAP-led.** US$1.5bn (+17%), 555m gamers, 25% payers, ARPPU US$3 (casual) – US$15 (mid-core); 10/10 top grossers are global franchises [V Lumikai]. Indian companies that matter monetise abroad: India is the world's #1 download market (8.45bn installs) but ~0.8% of global revenue [V-snip; E].
4. **M&A-led platform building and the goodwill/write-down cycle (Nazara's defining force).** Nazara's investing outflows were ₹2,803 Cr over FY22–FY26 (screener cash-flow statement; acquisitions alone ₹1,559 Cr in FY25 and ₹220 Cr in FY26 per the company) and Bluetile adds US$303m (≈₹2,900 Cr) — **~₹5,500–6,000 Cr of capital deployed in five years**, funded mostly by equity (₹843 Cr FY25, ₹494 Cr FY26, ₹731 Cr Oct-2026) [V; E]. The scorecard is mixed: Curve (39% margin) and Fusebox (TV-IP engine) worked; Moonshine (~₹1,011 Cr carrying value → 0) and NODWIN's Freaks 4U (₹384 Cr impairment inside NODWIN) did not [V].
5. **UA arbitrage as the economic core of casual gaming.** With Bluetile, Nazara's group A&P rises to ~55–60% of revenue; profitability becomes a choice (cut UA → margin up, growth down). The KPIs that matter are ROAS/payback and LTV/CAC (Kiddopia 2.08×), not reported EBITDA margin [V; E].
6. **Accounting complexity is a feature of the listed proxy.** Deconsolidation gains (₹1,098.5 Cr), exceptional impairments (₹915.8 Cr), associate losses (₹278 Cr FY26; ₹62 Cr Q1 FY27), company EBITDA vs screener OP (₹255 Cr vs −₹28 Cr in FY26) — readers must be taught to read four PATs [V].
7. **Currency and geography.** ~75–80% of Nazara's revenue is earned in US$/£/€ [E]; the rupee at ₹94.9–96.5/$ (vs ~₹83–84 a year earlier [K]) inflates reported revenue and EBITDA (~13–15% translation tailwind YoY on foreign revenue [E]) but also the rupee cost of the US$214m Bluetile balance due by Apr-2027 [V; E].
8. **Esports as the officially favoured category — but a low-margin one.** PROGA "recognises" e-sports and MYAS will promote it; but NODWIN, the category leader, made a 3% EBITDA margin on ₹658 Cr in FY26 and needed a ₹384 Cr write-off [V]. The Olympic Esports Games are paused [V-snip]. Policy tailwind ≠ profit pool.
9. **Governance transition.** Founder to non-exec; foreign seller-CEO with 4.67%; promoter group reclassified to include financial investors (Plutus/Khandelwal, Axana/Sacheti) with 87.7% of promoter shares shown as pledged; CFO change mid-deal [V]. A new independent Investment Committee is the governance answer [V].

## C. The REVENUE / PROFIT EQUATION

**Free-to-play mobile (dominant post-ban):**
Revenue = Installs × Retention-to-active (DAU/MAU) × [Payer conversion × ARPPU + Ad impressions per DAU × eCPM ÷ 1,000] ; Contribution = Revenue × (1 − store fee %) − UA spend ; EBITDA = Contribution − dev/live-ops staff − servers − overhead.

**Subscription (Kiddopia):** Revenue = Subscribers × ARPU × months ; Subscribers(t+1) = Subscribers(t) × (1 − churn) + Trials × Activation ; UA = Trials × Cost-per-trial.

**Premium (Curve):** Revenue = Units × Net price (after platform 30% and discounts) + DLC ; Gross profit = Revenue − developer royalty − dev amortisation.

**RMG (abolished):** Net revenue = Deposits × Rake % − Bonus/promotions ; GST (pre-Oct-23) = 18% × GGR; (post) = 28% × Deposits.

**Holdco (Nazara):** Reported PAT = Σ subsidiary EBITDA − corporate costs − D&A (incl. acquired-intangible amortisation) − interest − impairments + other income (incl. fair-value gains) − tax + share of associates' profit/loss (NODWIN, Moonshine) ± exceptionals.

### C.1 Worked example 1 — Bluetile/BestPlay, Q1 FY27 [V inputs; E derivations; ILLUSTRATIVE]
- Revenue ₹518 Cr − UA ₹438 Cr = ₹80 Cr contribution after UA → − other costs ~₹25 Cr → **EBITDA ₹55 Cr (10.7%)** [V for 518/438/80/55].
- Counterfactual: hold UA at Q1 FY26's 78% ratio → UA ₹404 Cr, contribution ₹114 Cr, EBITDA ~₹89 Cr (17%) on the same revenue [E; ignores the lost growth that the extra UA bought].

Under the old structure, 50% of Bluetile's cash would have leaked to the founders, and a CEO with earn-out targets tied to one subsidiary would have been conflicted.

## 4.5 New insights the first pass missed, and new primer issues

### 4.5.1 Insights an expert would add
1. **Nazara is ~87% an export business, not "75–80%".** The FY26 AR puts India at **12.7%** of revenue (₹232.7 Cr) and the US at **41.9%**. With Bluetile (US 50%+), group revenue from India falls to **~6–8%** in FY27 [E]. Nazara's earnings are a US/UK consumer-spending and USD/GBP story with an Indian listing. The Indian gaming market's 17% growth is almost irrelevant to its P&L; the PROGA ban mattered only through Moonshine and NODWIN's sponsors.
2. **The PokerBaazi bet cost ₹1,058.5 Cr, not ~₹830 Cr, and the investee carries a ₹10,369 Cr GST notice.** The write-off equals **~30% of Nazara's Mar-26 equity (₹3,473 Cr)**. Moonshine's own FY26 revenue (₹312.8 Cr) was barely 3% of its tax notice. The AR still says management anticipates "no present or future liability", although the Supreme Court restored such notices on 27-May-2026. That is moot for Nazara (carrying value nil; liability limited to equity), but it is a vivid case study for the RMG section.
3. **Nazara's "promoter" is now a financial consortium with pledged shares, and capital is being raised continuously.** Sequence: Axana ₹495 Cr preferential at ₹990 (₹247.5 adjusted, Jun-2025) → SAST open offer with only 1,889 shares tendered → promoter reclassification → warrants at ₹260 to Riambel/S Gupta/Plutus/Founders Collective (Jun-2026) → ₹306 preferential to the Bluetile sellers (Oct-2026). **~₹2,540 Cr of equity in ~2.5 years at rising prices (₹247.5 → ₹260 → ₹306 → market ₹402)** [E]. 87.7% of promoter shares are encumbered, almost all on the financial-investor side [V NSE; V SAST]. Governance is a theme in its own right, not a footnote.
4. **The FY26 "best year ever" was manufactured below EBITDA.** Owners' PAT ₹95.9 Cr came from a ₹1,098.5 Cr non-cash NODWIN revaluation, a ₹15.1 Cr Openplay sale gain and a ₹31 Cr Rusk revaluation, against a ₹914.7 Cr Moonshine impairment and ₹278.5 Cr of associate losses. Retained NODWIN was valued at ₹1,445.9 Cr (≈ ₹3,034 Cr for 100%) on 13-Aug-2025, against NODWIN FY26 EBITDA of ₹21 Cr — about **145× EBITDA** [E]. The ₹1,226.9 Cr carrying value now depends on NODWIN's IPO pricing.
5. **Bluetile is a cash-call business before it is a cash cow.** About US$75m falls due within ~90 days of 04-Aug-2026 (≈ early Nov-2026), ~US$35m in Dec-2026 and the remainder by 1-Apr-2027. Nazara is already pooling subsidiary cash (Kiddopia, Sportskeeda lending to Nazara UK) [V NSE]. Expect debt on the Mar-27 balance sheet for the first time at scale [E].

Churn rose to 6.4–6.6%, above the 6.0% assumed in the company's LTV, so the headline LTV/CAC of 2.08× is flattered [V; E].
