4. Kurlon integration, deleveraging and Sheela's 11–12% → 15% margin guidance; Furlenco stake.
5. Wakefit's profit quality vs its IPO valuation (pre-lease EBITDA 7.5% vs reported 12.2%; ₹98 Cr DTA in FY26 PAT; ₹958.6 Cr cash; −31% vs IPO).
