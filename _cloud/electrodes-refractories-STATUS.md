## h1
Electrodes & refractories: <em>a spread, a toll and a cash pile</em>

## Forces
1. The electrode spread (realisation − needle coke), set by Chinese supply and Western discipline (2026 turn: GrafTech Monterrey closure, +$600–1,200/t Mar-26, ≥30% Sep-26; ICRA's $1,600/t floor).
2. EAF growth outside China vs falling kg/t — India's own growth is BF- and induction-led (Apr–Dec 2025: BOF 42.5%, IF 37.7%, EAF 19.7%).
3. Trade policy cuts both ways: US AD/CVD now names India (combined prelim deposits GI 13.56%, HEG 8.14%; China ~199%); finals ~12-Feb-2027.
4. Treasury books → anode capex: other income = 70% of the electrode makers' FY26 OP; TACC (₹2,200 Cr, 20 kt) and SGAM (₹4,330 Cr, 30 kt).
5. Refractories as a toll on Indian steel tonnes: RHIM price-led Q1 FY27 (shipments −5%), Chinese magnesia, ~₹1,500 Cr of write-downs FY23–FY26.
