## Forces

2. The roll-up: group builds/transfers, AVTL buys (Mangalore ₹968 Cr from Sea Lord Containers); IPO; related-party transfer prices; MPS sell-down to 75% by Jun-2028.
3. Liquids earn, gas waits: segment pre-tax ROCE ~11.6% vs ~6.5% (indicative); LPG turns ~22× vs design.
4. The landlord's reset: port leases — all Pipavav sub-leases expire 29-Sep-2028; Kandla leases held over; market-value rent resets.
5. Leverage and the valuation gap: finance cost ₹19 Cr → ₹40 Cr/qtr; EV/EBITDA ~47.5× TTM (lease-incl.) vs Vopak ~9.5× (2026E).
